### The calendar, and how to check it

**July's Thursdays are the sixth, the thirteenth, the twentieth and the twenty-seventh**, derived in
Volume 5 from the twenty-ninth of June. From those: **July 1 is a Saturday, July 6 a Thursday, July 10 a
Monday, July 12 a Wednesday, and August 1 a Tuesday.** Volume 5 ran one chapter a day and no chapter
named any weekday except Thursday. **The monthly crew repaints the side board on the first of each month
and the Thursday pass is a different crew in a different shade** — the first of August repaints, and so do
the Thursdays on the thirteenth and the twentieth, in two shades, without touching each other.

**Counts, derived from the anchors and not read off the page.** From the twenty-third of June: Wren's
finding is **eighteen days** old on the eleventh, nineteen on the twelfth, twenty-seven on the twentieth,
thirty-nine on the first of August. From the sixth of July: the true report is **five days** in intake on
the eleventh, fourteen on the twentieth. From the fourth of June: Petra is on no roll **thirty-seven days**
on the eleventh, forty-six on the twentieth. **Print the arithmetic in the row for any figure you use.**
