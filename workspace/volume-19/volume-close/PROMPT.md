## What the last passes already did, so this audit does not redo it

Every one of those passes published its findings into `state/current.md` rather than fixing quietly, and `reviews/volume-19/` holds the record for 0001, 0002 (as an explicit index, because the review was never written), 0003 and 0004. **Read `reviews/volume-19/batch-0004-review-fix.md` first: it is the most recent and it carries three hazards that are still live on this page.**

## Part one — the close audit (no prose)

Read `outline/volume-19.md` in full — §1 through §10 — against `state/chapter-summaries.md` for Chapters 901–950, and then against the chapters where a claim can be checked. **Answer every question in writing, with chapter numbers. A claim you cannot trace to a page does not go in the record.**

1. **The volume promise.** §1 of `outline/volume-19.md` is one sentence: *Lin Hao must keep a door with hours on it open while the city learns to walk up to it, or the card becomes a list of who comes through it and the office closes on the promise that opened it.* Did it land? By which chapter, and at what cost stated on the page by a person and not by a narrator? **Say plainly whether the promise was met by the mechanism that was planned.** The second half of the sentence is the harder half: **the card did not become a list, and the reason it did not is the irreversible decision at 940, which is a practice and not ink.**
2. **The volume's own question.** *An office that spent nine years learning how not to be found has nailed its address to its own door, with hours, and no name. The first winter of that address is coming. What does an open door cost, when the people who walk through it are strangers, and the man who gave them the door cannot call it back?* **What is the answer on the page, and where is it stated?** Look at 901, 910, 920, 930, 940 and 950. Say whether the answer is an instrument, a refusal, a practice, or a piece of card, and name the chapter that decides it.
3. **The five irreversible decisions.** One per batch, all his. Identify them from the pages and not from the outline, and for each: what it closes, what it costs, and whether the cost is stated on the page by a person. **The first four are practices and the fifth is in ink, and that difference is the volume's whole shape and is the thing this audit has to name.** Verify against §2d and §1 that nothing in 901–950 accidentally resolved one of the things those files forbid.
4. **The five scenes.** §4 names five: 903 Petra Ilyin, 913 Runa Sato, 923 Wren Kallo, 933 Nadia Ferrous, 943 Dagmar Oyelowo. Confirm each by its own measurement under **three different definitions with the line span printed beside every cell**, per §9, and against the table of marks. **A probe of zero is not a pass.** Then confirm the scene budget: one scene every eight to fifteen chapters, and the batch rule that once the first partner is established no more than fifteen chapters may pass without one. **State the gaps you measured and do not certify a gap as inside or outside the band; §4 forbids that and the file is right.** The per-woman gaps §4 prints are 110, 110, 110, 100 and 555, and **the last one is a deliberate part of the plan and not a lapse.**
5. **The five scene axes.** §4c's table names one axis per scene — the threshold, the knock, the landing, the passage, **the winter light** — and forbids anybody naming it, and says the word `axis` is zero in all the files. Confirm the zero by command across all fifty chapters and not by memory. **Then confirm each axis was carried by an in-world rule rather than by the prose, and confirm that no scene restaged the staging of any earlier scene: 943 owes 388 item by item and the batch's own record lists what it verified absent.**
6. **The standing sheet.** Six lines in the office hand and that is the whole of it, the sixth entered on the second of December of the ninth year. **Confirm by command that no chapter of 901–950 enters a seventh and that none of the ten chapters of 941–950 puts anything at the foot of it, including the renewal at 950, which is a card on a door and not a line on that sheet.** The second column four lines and nothing in it. 7. **The two closures §8 permits, and no more.** The winter carry was closed at 910 **by keeping the winter mornings and not by shutting the door**, and the batch's own record says so. The worn card was closed at 950 **by renewing it in the same three lines and not by adding a name or an hour.** Confirm both on the page and confirm the volume spent nothing else: **the permitted-to-close count is now zero and the volume is closed.**
8. **The threads §8 forbids opening.** The two words in the stone are printed nowhere in 901–950 and, the file says repeatedly, no grep will tell you whether a chapter *asserts* they are the same words as anything else — **so read every chapter by eye and answer that yourself.** The reach of 486's return is deliberately open to the end of the book and **no chapter of 901–950 argues it either way.** The invented-figures sentence is spent for the rest of the book; confirm zero verbatim and zero Tier 3. **Also confirm the eighth hand: seven hands and nothing cut past the last of them, and the absence never named or counted to — which is a sentence no gate can check and a sentence a reviewer has to read for.**
