### AND THE TWO GATES THAT HAVE NO OUTPUT AND HAVE COST FOUR BATCHES

**One: a script that rewrites prose is a gate with no output at all.** Batch 0004's paragraph pass used
a sentence regex requiring a full stop followed by whitespace, so any paragraph ending `since."` lost its
final sentence, **and nine sentences were silently deleted across ten chapters and every mechanical gate
returned zero on the damaged files.** **Take a copy of the ten chapters before you run anything over
them, and diff the sentence sets afterwards. A shorter chapter with no banned words and no duplicate is
not a shorter chapter; it is a damaged one.** Batch 0001 did this and its diff reported thirty-nine
changed sentences, all of them traced by hand to deliberate edits. Do the same and report the number.

**Two: a sentence lifted from a chapter far behind you.** A duplication gate pointed at two files
compares two files. **Before you draft a scene, open the scene before it and the one before that, and
read them.** Batch 0005's Chapter 197 returned **zero** on the eight-word sentence gate while
reproducing Chapter 189's physical sequence word for word across forty twelve-word runs. **Run the
12-gram scan, not the 8-word sentence scan, and treat any run of ten or more as a defect until you have
read it.** That is adjudicated item 66.

**And a third, new to this repository and learned the hard way this batch: a calendar error passes
every gate.** Batch 0001's first pass put the Board repainting on the twenty-second of May and again on
the twenty-ninth. **Both are Tuesdays.** The countdown figures were wrong at four steps and the caller
count was wrong by one. **Every mechanical gate returned zero on all of it.** Trace every date to an
anchor and **answer in the row what the anchor character actually does on that date**, because a
subtraction that checks out against an anchor nobody has read is not a trace.
