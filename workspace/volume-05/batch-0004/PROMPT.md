### AND THE THREE GATES THAT HAVE NO OUTPUT AND HAVE COST THIRTEEN BATCHES

**One: a script that rewrites prose is a gate with no output at all.** Take a copy of the ten chapters
**before** you run anything over them, then diff the SENTENCE SETS and, independently, an eight-word

the shingle check backwards and reported 4,599. Batch 0003 took the snapshot first and reports **zero

**Two: a sentence lifted from a chapter far behind you.** A duplication gate pointed at two files compares
two files. **Before you draft a scene, open the scene before it and the one before that, and read them.**

you have read it.** Batch 0002's Chapter 213 transposed ten words of Chapter 210's staged physical
