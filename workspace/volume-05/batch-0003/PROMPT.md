### AND THE THREE GATES THAT HAVE NO OUTPUT AND HAVE COST SIX BATCHES

**One: a script that rewrites prose is a gate with no output at all.** Batch 0004's paragraph pass used
a sentence regex requiring a full stop followed by whitespace, so any paragraph ending `since."` lost its
final sentence, **and nine sentences were silently deleted across ten chapters and every mechanical gate
returned zero on the damaged files.** **Take a copy of the ten chapters before you run anything over
them, then diff the SENTENCE SETS and, independently, an eight-word SHINGLE set. A shingle set will
report thousands of false positives for shingles that span a paragraph break, which is expected and is
not a defect; what it catches is a sentence that has actually gone. Batch 0002 ran both and reported
zero sentences lost and seventeen sentences that gained a leading quotation mark.** Do the same and
report the number.

**Two: a sentence lifted from a chapter far behind you.** A duplication gate pointed at two files
compares two files. **Before you draft a scene, open the scene before it and the one before that, and
read them.** **The 12-gram scan, not the 8-word sentence scan, and treat any run of ten or more as a
defect until you have read it.** Batch 0002's Chapter 213 transposed ten words of Chapter 210's staged
physical sequence and lifted eleven words of a character's own line, **and the eight-word sentence gate
returned zero on both.** That is adjudicated item 66.

**Three: a calendar error passes every gate.** Batch 0001 found eight and Batch 0002 found twelve, and
every mechanical gate returned zero on all of it. **Trace every date to an anchor and answer, in the row,
what the anchor character actually does on that date, because a subtraction that checks out against an
anchor nobody has read is not a trace.** **Two of Batch 0002's twelve were structural and not numeric: a
public board painted on the first of the month carrying a date three weeks in the future, and a standing
lodged on the seventh described as reaching a public filing nine days later when that filing had already
gone in on the twenty-fourth.** **Ask what each document was physically doing on the day it is dated.**
