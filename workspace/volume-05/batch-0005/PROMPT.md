## CH 241: AGNETE LINDQVIST'S SCENE — DECIDED, AND WHY

**Ch 241 is AGNETE LINDQVIST'S SCENE. Batch 0003 proposed it, Batch 0004 took it, and this prompt
fixes it: the woman's name is on this batch.** The argument, inherited whole: her gift is that a room

at that address. **So 241 is the first time in eleven years somebody asks her a question she cannot
decline. The certificate is in the next house and does not come in. It is the first scene in the book
about a woman choosing to be visible — the volume's sentence arrived at from the other end.**
