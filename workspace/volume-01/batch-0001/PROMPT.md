Write Chapters 1 to 10 of *Ranked by Desire* — Volume One, Batch 0001.

## The job

Ten finished chapters, in ascending order, into `chapters/volume-01/chapter-0001.md` through
`chapter-0010.md`. Chapter files, not summaries, not cards, not outlines. Start with Chapter 1 in your
very first action.

This batch does one thing: Lin Hao's merit reading comes back unsettled, he learns what spending it costs
by hurting somebody, and he is read by the only person in the city who can read records. By Chapter 10 he
knows what he is carrying, who is counting, and that being classified is a corridor with no window in it.

It does **not** change his rank, does **not** give him a Channel, a quiet account, or any Volume 2+
vocabulary, and does **not** resolve anything about his brother.

## Hard gates

**Chapter 1, inside roughly the first eight hundred words, all three of:**
1. A concrete objective with a deadline — submit the Thursday plate, keep the posting.
2. An anomaly he can see and cannot explain — a figure with no interval on it.
3. A consequence of what he does about it — he takes it back to the counter and lands on a review list.

**Chapter 3, by its end, by event and not by explanation:** the reservoir moves merit out of a stranger and
the stranger feels a cold minute. The reader sees the cost before anyone names it.

**Chapter 8:** the explicit scene, written as the scene. See below.

## The scene

One scene in this batch, Chapter 8, Ines Duarte, Lantern Row. Write the actual scene — explicit, physical,
unhurried once it begins, arousing, and generous. Not implication. Not a fade. It is the product the
reader bought.

Both of them are adults, awake, unmedicated, unrestrained, and neither is impaired in any way. The
reservoir is in play as **relief** — what she is owed after four months of carrying her sister's reading —
and never as compulsion. He gives it back. She decides, quickly and out loud. Nobody is talked out of
anything. Afterwards she can feel the ledger through the contact and says a name from it aloud.

After the scene, the register changes completely. The next chapter is cold, procedural and frightened. Keep
the two apart.

## Craft for this batch

- Paragraphs of two to four sentences. Break anything over roughly ninety words. Never over 120.
- Sixty to seventy per cent plainly readable prose, twenty to thirty documentary, and never three
  documentary paragraphs in a row without somebody standing in the room.
- Long accumulating sentences are the voice. Break them at every revelation. Short plain sentences when
  something is understood.
- One System message per chapter at most, in square brackets on its own line, plain language, in the
  ledger notation. Chapters 3, 4 and 6 have one. The rest do not need one.
- No bold, no italics, no section headings unless a chapter genuinely has two sections.
- Chapter titles come from the title bank in `outline/volume-01.md`. Use them exactly.
- Every feeder who feels a cold minute gets a name, a room, a job, and one line of dialogue in the chapter
  they are spent on. Never a device. This is the moral spine.
- Ration: "nobody asked", "nobody thanked him", "there is no form for this", "there is no column for it",
  "the record remained". Each at most once in the whole book; the notes say where the rest are spent.
- After any institutional passage, one plain sentence about what it means for one named person. The stock
  clerk's sister on the fourth floor is available for this.

## Before Chapter 7 ends

Lin Hao has one felt emotion and one concrete want on the page. He wants rank six. He wants the branch's
ninety-one people not to be sent to a room with no window. He wants to stop hurting people by accident.
Pick the one that is true for the chapter and let it drive what he does.

## If you run short

Stop at a chapter boundary. Keep the chapters you finished, update the state files to match what is
actually on disk, note in `state/open-threads.md` which chapters of this batch are still unwritten, and
exit. Never restart a chapter that already exists. Never leave a chapter half-written. Never pad a scene
to fill a word count.

## Do not

- Do not write prose for any chapter outside 1–10.
- Do not open a new plot thread that belongs to a later volume, and do not name a faction or office that
  `bible/terminology.md` does not already define.
- Do not have anybody coerced, sedated, restrained, or psychically touched as a way of producing a scene.
- Do not let Lin Hao become strong. He is a man with a supply and a bad night's sleep.
- Do not declare the series finished or write a completion marker. This is one batch of a five-hundred
  chapter book and the plan in `outline/series.md` reaches chapter 500.
