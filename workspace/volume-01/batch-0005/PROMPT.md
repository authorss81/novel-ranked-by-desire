## Craft

- **Length: land every chapter between 1,800 and 2,700 words.** Batch 0004 ran 1,922–3,349, mean 2,383. Four
  chapters went over the ceiling and were cut by **removing sentences, not scenes**. Do not pad to reach
  the band and do not split a finished scene to stay inside it. **One exception is recorded and is not
  precedent: Ch 36 is 3,349 because its scene was found summarised and was written out, and
  `AGENTS.md` calls length a guide. Do not use it to run long on a chapter that does not need the room.**
- **Paragraphs two to four sentences, under 120 words.** Batch 0004 ran a maximum of 119.
- **No horizontal rules.** Nineteen were removed from Batch 0004 and **Chapters 1–40 carry none**. A
  markdown sweep over the whole book is a hard gate, not a formality: a rule that has held for forty chapters
  is not one the forty-first chapter may quietly break.
- **Bold is not used anywhere in this book.** Document text prints plain. **Italic is scarce** — Batch 0004
  used **eight** across ten chapters, three of them inside the Chapter 36 scene, and zero in four of them.
  Spread it; one scene must not carry the batch.
- **At most one ledger message per chapter and never the same panel twice.** Batch 0004 used Ch 37 and Ch
  40. Expect this batch's at **Ch 48**, where the ledger gains a line that has never been there.
- **Never speak reservoir, quiet account, the Channel, the Ninth Seal or the Concil in the prose.** The cards
  and `state/current.md` use those words to describe what is withheld. Chapters 1–40 contain none and must
  still contain none. **Never write *there is no form for this*, never write *there is no column for it*, and
  do not paraphrase either one.** Sweep for the construction, not the wording — a paraphrase survived a
  word-list into Batch 0004's Chapter 32 and had to be cut.
- **No age is stated in narration or in dialogue**, for named characters or walk-ons. **Match bare
  number-ages** — *was about sixty*, *in his forties*, *twenty-three years of my life* — not only *years
  old*. **Five residual instances exist and are registered with line numbers in `state/open-threads.md`:**
  Ch 3, Ch 8, Ch 16, Ch 18, **and Ch 29 — which is the first deliberate one, written into a chapter that was
  certified clean.** Do not touch them. Do not add a sixth.
- **No weekday name except Thursday and Monday the 19th**, where load-bearing, otherwise dates and intervals.
  Fifty-eight illegal names survive behind Chapter 20 as an open repair. Do not copy them, do not fix them.
- **`nobody` / `no one`: thirty or fewer across these ten chapters, none over five.** Batch 0004 ran
  thirteen, four in Ch 39. *The person nobody looked at* is **retired**. *Nobody has asked me to do this* was
  spent once, in Ola's notebook in Ch 38.
- **No feeder thanks him**, and *nobody thanked him* is Chapter 50's and unavailable before it.
- **One felt emotion and one concrete want per chapter.** Institutional passages land on one named person.
- **Scene Ch 46 only** — Marguerite Vale, the best rooms in Ostara, and neither of them pretending about
