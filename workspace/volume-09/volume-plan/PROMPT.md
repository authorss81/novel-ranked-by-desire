**THE DAY-COUNTING CONVENTION IS FIXED AND IT IS NOT NEGOTIABLE.** A month is thirty days and has no thirty-first.
A count of days remaining in a quarter is **thirty minus the date of the month, and it is exclusive.** A count
of elapsed days may be written either way **and the date must be subtracted, never calculated against a real
calendar.** A figure may not be derived from `bible/power-system.md`'s band table. **`PC-28` is the standing
example of what happens when a chapter gets one of these wrong: a section headed *The Nine Days* for a
