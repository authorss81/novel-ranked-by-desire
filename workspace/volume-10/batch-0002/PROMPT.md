## 6. THE TEN CARDS

### 461 — **The Hearing He Cannot Refuse.** 19 July. Two days after the chest.
Adeyemi forces a public hearing, and it is his instrument and not a favour. **The only defence against a requisition is a hearing anybody may attend.** Nobody can be compelled to testify in it. **What changes:** there is now a room in this city in which the reservoir is going to be discussed, and he did not ask for it, and he cannot stop it. *May not:* start the four days; call the reservoir by any of its names in front of a stranger.

### 462 — **Eleven Thousand In A Vestry.** 21 July.
**The Church of the Quiet Number sends eleven hundred of its eleven thousand.** Agnete Lindqvist's board of nine does not choose her and she has never once in twenty years asked it anything, and she has put her own name on a book and not the household's. **What changes:** the Church is in the room with a position and it is not a hostile one, and nobody in this book has yet argued against people he respects.

### 463 — **Four Days Set By A Clerk.** 23 July.
**Four days, the twenty-sixth to the twenty-ninth, inclusive four and elapsed three. Set by a clerk, because a clerk may only enter and cannot be asked to hurry.** *May not:* print an interval against a real calendar. Print the operands.

### 464 — **A Room That Cannot Be Compelled.** 24 July.
The room itself, and the rule that makes it the only place in this book where the reservoir is discussed in public. **Agnete's gift makes a room where nobody can be compelled to testify, and its cost is that she has not been outside one in eleven years.** *May not:* make it a scene.

### 465 — **THE SCENE. BEATRIX HALM. 25 July. TITLED *The Hour Before On A Roof*.** §5 above.

### 466 — **The Man Who Filed It Instead.** 26 July. **THE MOVEMENT'S TURN.**
`outline/ending.md`:91–95. **Perrin Sowe testifies and he does not defend Lin Wei.** He says, on the record, in front of nine seals, that Lin Wei was right, that he knew, that he filed instead of giving, and that the file he filed is the reason Lin Wei was removed. **The book does not punish him. It puts it in the record.** And Lin Hao has to sit there while the most honourable man he knows destroys his own standing to say a true sentence. *May not:* forgive him; ask him about the second folio; let anyone in the room call it revenge.

### 467 — **Three Hours And No Use To Anyone.** 27 July.
Agnete stands up in a room where nobody can be compelled and spends three hours giving testimony nobody can use against anybody. **This is the most Agnete thing in the book and it must not be a speech.** *May not:* let her be thanked for it.

### 468 — **Put In The Record And Kept.** 28 July.
The reservoir discussed in public, the Church's eleven hundred in the room, and **nine thousand people at the Reading who were not in it.** *May not:* explain the mechanism again to a reader who has had it since Volume 2.

### 469 — **The Claim The Church Makes.** 29 July. **THE MOVEMENT'S SECOND TURN.**
`outline/ending.md`:97–100. **The account belongs to the unrecorded, not to Lin Hao, and the correct disposition is distribution rather than return.** A genuine, well-argued, morally serious opposition from people he respects, and **it nearly wins.** *May not:* have him say the claim has been settled or has not; may not have him say the account is his.

### 470 — **The Argument Against Good People.** 30 July.
**He has to argue against the Church in public. That is the volume's real fight.** **What he cannot say, and the reason it is hard, is that the Church's claim is what his brother would have said.** *May not:* win by authority, by rank, or by a document.
