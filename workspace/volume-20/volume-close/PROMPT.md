## Why this phase exists

The pass that wrote 991–1000 closed the volume in prose: 1000 is the quarter-end, the fifty-second sheet goes up on the same two nails carrying no new line, the head is printed for the fourth and last time in the volume **with both of its figures in one breath**, and **he says the three lines out loud one last time at the bottom of his own stair in the dark, says the cost of it out loud, and says he is not going to say them again.** `AGENTS.md` and `PHASE_SYSTEM.md` are unambiguous: when a batch completes a volume, the writer creates exactly one volume-close prompt. 951–1000 complete it.

**Chapter 1001 must not be drafted by this phase, and this phase does not decide whether a Volume 21 is wanted.** `state/complete.md` is not this phase's to create — it belongs to whoever is told to, and `scripts/completion_guard.py` reads the planned final chapter out of `outline/series.md`, `outline/ending.md` and `NOVEL_SPEC.md`, all of which still say five hundred, while the manuscript runs to one thousand and the overrun flag at the head of `state/open-threads.md` is still open. Do not touch those three files and do not reconcile the manuscript's length against them here; **that is a series decision and this volume close is not where it gets made. Writing fifty chapters of the last planned volume did not decide it and may not be read as deciding it.**
