## 1. WHAT THIS BATCH IS

**Ten chapters, the first to the twenty-sixth of January. Card 358 is the scene, it is Sun Meiling's, and it
is her second scene in the book and the first since Chapter 63.**

**The batch's job, in one line: to take a man who cannot be requisitioned and make him the holder of the

**AND THE OTHER THING IT IS DOING, WHICH IS NOT DECORATION:** a woman who decided on the night of the
thirtieth of December to speak has chosen and has not said whom, and he is **barred from asking and does not
ask**, and the shape she handed over is already on its way to a building off a third court where the man on
the third floor has never come down the corridor in three years.

standing answer exists and it works: *you may not put one question to me about that address and I will say I

table, or lets the eight women help him with it, has found a worse page and must go back.** Nobody helps him.
Nobody helped him on the twenty-sixth of November either, for the same reason: *helping him would have been the
same as writing it for him.*
