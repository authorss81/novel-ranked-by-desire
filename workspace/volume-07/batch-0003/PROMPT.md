**2. THE STYLE THAT CAME OUT OF THAT REPAIR, AND THE FAILURE MODE OF IT.** A dialogue paragraph opens with a
quotation mark and closes with one, and no speech runs across a paragraph break. **When you split one, close the
speech in both halves. Batch 0002 left eleven speeches open and found every one by re-running the whole-file
quotation count after each round.**

record is a claim. Re-run it from outside the pass and let the re-run outrank the record.**

**4. SEARCH YOUR OWN CHAPTERS FOR `it went on a long time` AND `afterwards`, AND TREAT EVERY HIT AS AN
