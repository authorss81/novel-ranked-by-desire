### The title bank is fifty **names**, not fifty descriptions

**This is a correction carried forward from the Batch 0005 review.** All ten of Volume 3's batch titles are
scene summaries and `chapter-0144.md` *Nine Minutes Before* is a clock restatement, which `AGENTS.md`
bans outright. They were mandated verbatim from `outline/volume-03.md` §11, which is where the failure
lives, so **the fix is made here and not by retitling Volume 3.**

A title names the chapter the way a chapter title reads in a table of contents: **three to twelve words,
never much over ninety characters, unique book-wide.** It does **not** restate the scene, the weather, the
hour, the date, the ages of the people present, or the dialogue. **Twelve Volume 3 titles are the worked
example of what is being rejected:** *What She Will Not Read · What He Tells Them First · The Hall Fills ·
Nine Minutes Before · He Gives It Away In Public · The Ninth Is Himself · What Goes In The Sixth Space ·
A Description In His Own Hand · The Woman Who Is Not On The List · The Board Repaints*. Nine of those ten
describe what happens; one of them is a clock. **Write the bank, then read every entry and ask whether a
reader who has not read the chapter would learn anything about the chapter.** If they would, replace it.

**Register it in `state/open-threads.md` before drafting prose**, as a proposed-canon block at the head,

## 1. The sentence Volume 4 opens on, which Volume 3 has already earned

> **A household that does not stop him is not a shield, it is an audience.**

It is in **Sun Meiling's mouth**, on a landing above a chop-house in Ferrier Street, on the thirtieth of
the third month. She had been right since the first of the month and says she has been saying it in the
wrong words to people who could not act on it. **She closes her own refusal at the same moment and

## 4. HARD LOCKS. These bind the batch and they are not negotiable inside a chapter.

1. **The account moves only on contact with the person it came from.** A hand on a stranger moves
   nothing and costs grey at one temple, a grey night, a night of sleep like being woken on the hour,
   proportional, never improving. **He cannot relieve a room.** The only legal movements are the people
   who fed him and himself. **He has promised, on the record, never to spend on himself again.** Any
   chapter that has him touch a stranger and have anything happen is mechanically false.
2. **The word *Channel* and routing are Volume 5.** Do not name it. Do not imply it.
3. **He cannot raise his rank from the account.** Not one grain. If a chapter contains a figure that
   would allow a conversion, it is a defect, not a scene.
4. **No folio opened. No classification entered. No rank lost.** Rank six may be held, seated, suspended,
   restored onto paper, or attached to an instrument; it may not be earned.
5. **No age stated for anybody. No channel word, no routing, nothing named or implied.**
6. **No system panel except where a count actually turns.** A panel that does not report a turn is a lie
   the System does not tell. The ledger stands at 115 lines and nine turned; every rise is work with the
   cause named on the page where it happens, and the volume's total should sit where
   `outline/volume-04.md` says it sits.
7. **`nobody <verb>`: at most one per batch, in a character's own mouth, once, with work to do.** Volume
   3's batches had to be repaired twice for this. Start at zero.

- **Do not reopen a Volume 3 case.** Anything in 151–160 that could be lifted out and dropped into a

  chapter must change the situation.
- **Depth beats length.** 1,800–2,700 words a chapter; 2,600–3,900 for a scene, with a named reason for
  anything over 2,700. **Volume 3's Batch 0005 ran three chapters over the ordinary band — 3,822,
  3,655 and 4,099 — and all three were defensible, because each was a labelled scene or the volume's
  climax and none of them could be cut without splitting a finished scene.** Do not use that as a
  precedent. Cut instead.
- **Scene budget:** one explicit scene every 8 to 15 chapters. 157 is Volume 4's first and 169 its

  anybody, violence and sex in separate registers.** An impaired state is never the mechanism.
