## Five hard constraints on this volume

**1. Sun Meiling (T10) is planted in Volume 2 and not started.** She has waited three years for the right
question. **Chapter 55 is her first scene and is immovable. Chapter 63 is immovable and is the sentence
that unlocks Volume 8** — the one that reconciles everything. Volume 2's five scenes are **55, 63, 74,
86, 97**, and 63 is fixed as well as 55. **She appears before 55 by name and not on the page**, and the
outline should say how she is planted without being started. She is core, age 33, Lin Wei's former
research partner, and her gift is that she sees the same shape in different data.

**2. Alma Quayle's office notices the thirty-first entry's finding number.** The Classification Wing
register has carried his entry — the thirty-first, dated the 3rd — since Chapter 30, and at the close of
Volume 1 the finding number is written beside it in red ink. **An office that has no procedure for a
rank-six petitioner with no merit behind the band is the natural instrument of this volume's pressure, and
the finding number is how they find him.**

**3. Volume 2 must not classify him in Batch 0001.** Not in Batch 0001 and not, if the volume's own logic
can be honoured, inside Volume 2 at all without the volume earning it. Classification is the terminal
failure of the early game and it empties a man out; **it is a threat the volume holds open, not a state the
volume enters.**

**4. No new final enemy.** Anouk Ferreira is the chair of the Concil of the Nine and **is not yet on the
page** — Volumes 7, 9 and 10. Alma Quayle is the head of classification and is the natural Volume 2
authority; she is **Volume 2 and Volume 7**, not a final opponent. Perrin Sowe is Lin Wei's colleague, the
man who noticed what Lin Wei noticed and **filed it instead of donating** — **Volumes 5 and 8, and the last
volume, and not this one.** Do not put any of the three on the page as a new antagonist.

**5. Decide where the held-account pressure comes from, and make it different from Volume 1's.** Volume 1's
pressure was an audit and a Review — a public machine measuring him. **Volume 2's must be an Office with no
procedure for what he is**, which is a different kind of pressure: not *are you good enough* but *what are
you, and who has the standing to say*. Work out what that Office actually does when a man presents a rank
the plate cannot carry, and let the answer be uncomfortable.
