## Craft — the gates this book has actually failed before

- **No horizontal rules. Zero bold. Zero italic.** Volume 1 carried 101 italics and **none from Chapter
  40 on**; Chapters 51–60 carry none and the tail is the register. **Twenty-six horizontal rules were
  written into Batch 0001's first pass as scene separators and all twenty-six had to be removed.** Use
  a blank line.
- **No weekday name except Thursday**, and Monday the nineteenth only where the world turns on it. Use
  dates and intervals. Batch 0001 had to rewrite thirty-five.
- **Never speak the planning register** — *reservoir*, *quiet account*, *Concil*, *Ninth Seal*,
  *Channel*. Verified zero across Chapters 1–60.
- **Never write *there is no form for this* or *there is no column for it*, and do not paraphrase either
  one.** All three escalations of the first were spent in Volume 1 and none remain before Volume 7.
  **The Office's version of that fact is now on the page in Drant's mouth** — a form describes a thing
  that has already been described — and that is the shape to reach for instead.
- **`nobody thanked &lt;anyone&gt;` is retired outright.** The sentence *Nobody has thanked him* belongs
  to `chapter-0050.md` alone. **The `nobody asked` note in Ola's hand is spent; the motif is available**
  and the book uses it on purpose.
- **`He wanted, concretely` is not available anywhere.** Volume 1 closed with eight consecutive chapters
  ending on it and it is item 35 in `state/current.md` and an unpaid repair. **It must not appear in
  Chapters 61–70 and it must not appear in your final paragraphs.** Test with a *contains* check and the
  string **with no trailing comma** — a version with the comma matches seven and silently drops Ch 42.
- **At most one system message per chapter, only where the count turns, and never the same panel
  twice.** Batch 0001 used two: `Ninety-seven lines. Eight turned.` and `One hundred and three lines.
  Eight turned.` **Those two are spent. Do not reuse either wording.**
- **No age is stated for anybody, named or walk-on.** Match bare number-ages in dialogue as well as in
  narration — *about sixty*, *in his forties*, *twenty-three years of my life* — and not only *years
  old*. Nine chapters of Volume 1 carry ten registered instances and a reviewer who finds mistakes will
  not look for decisions; **a deliberate decision registers in `state/open-threads.md` at the moment you
  make it.**
- **`nobody` / `no one`: thirty or fewer across the ten, and none over five in a chapter.**
- **Paragraphs two to four sentences. Break anything over ninety words. Nothing over 120.** Batch 0001's
  first pass ran to 177 and needed a repair; Volume 1's own tail maxes at 117.
- **Chapter length follows the arc.** Batch 0001 ran 2,129–5,065. The guide is 1,800–2,700 and it is not
  a quota. **Never pad, never split a finished scene, and never run long because a passage is fun.**
- **One felt emotion and one concrete want per chapter.** **After any passage of institutional
  machinery, one plain sentence about what it means for one named person.**
- **The batch needs a changed situation, not a larger one.** An option closed, a cost incurred, a
  relationship moved, a threat answered or raised, a rule understood well enough to exploit, a reversal.
  **Every case in this batch must change something outside itself.** If a chapter could be lifted out
  and dropped into another without changing anything, it belongs to the volume outline and not the
  chapter sequence.
- **At least one irreversible decision, something he cannot undo, that costs him something real and
  closes off an option.** Batch 0001's was Ch 54.
- **One scene, Ch 63, and it changes something.** Adults, awake, unrestrained, unmedicated; consent in
  plain statements before anything starts; violence and sex in separate registers; **no draw is spent in
  it**, because the account scoring or moving is a separate thing from this. **It must not repeat a
  Volume 1 shape or the Volume 2 Ch 55 shape.** Ch 55 was *wanted for what he carries rather than audited
  for it*; Ch 63 must be a different direction entirely, and `outline/volume-02.md` says what it is for.
