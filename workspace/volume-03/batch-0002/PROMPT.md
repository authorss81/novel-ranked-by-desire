## What this batch must land

1. **The record gets built in the open.** The roll exists; now the Office assembles what each item means

   lines a day with a refused signature. A lift log with forty-one names. Every document belongs to the
   household and the household is not on the roll.
2. **One buff square gets named at 113, by somebody else, on a visitors' book page.** The unnamed caller
   of the 3rd is this thread's other end. The other two squares stay unnamed and unentered.
3. **Sun Meiling's refusal advances at 113 and is not closed.** She states it in her own words, on her own
   ground, and the household's protection logic takes the hit without breaking.
4. **The instrument enters as the Office's case (115–117).** The Certification desk is asked to certify
   that the counter-forensics tool can do what the Wing says it can do, and the officer outside the
   standing roll who built it is asked to confirm she built it. **Wren Kallo is named for the first time at
   116, in the certification request, and is on the page from 118.** No card before 116 names her.
5. **Scene at 118, Wren Kallo, immovable.** Core and unsworn: sworn to a rank-six man she would be a woman
   nobody in her division speaks to. Her cost is fixed and is the whole of her — **she cannot keep a
   secret.** Her direction, never used before: **she asks to be made a party to the thing she is losing
   her post over**, and he decides in the room, out loud, and she repeats it back because she cannot help
   herself. Adults, awake, unrestrained, unmedicated, no impaired state, **no draw spent**, violence and
   sex in separate registers. No age stated.
6. **The Office's sentence goes into the sixth space at 120** (per `outline/volume-03.md` §13): Sarn's
   paragraph — *a settled mass in the possession of a member, accumulating by the member's own condition,
   no source identified, no measure obtainable* — entered under Anneke Roos's hand as received, not as
   agreed. **It is not true**, and no chapter in this batch says it is; it is what four offices believe.
   Sarn may be named as the hand three floors up; he is not a villain, not a final enemy, and does not
   survive as an opponent past Volume 4.
7. **Irreversible decision for this batch (116–118, landed by 118).** He makes Wren a party to the thing
   she is losing her post over, out loud, in the room — deciding that a woman who cannot keep a secret
   will answer for it as a party rather than discover it as a stranger. He cannot un-tell what she will
   repeat, and by the volume's own arithmetic the secret is already on its way to being four people deep.
8. **Option closed: he is not associated with these people for free.** Every person named on the roll is
   now a person the city can ask about him, and the roll is public, and it cannot be unrolled.
9. **Hinge into Batch 0003.** The record complete, the instrument certified on paper, the hall sixty-odd
   days out, and the next question already visible: whether the instrument can actually produce what its
   proponent says it produces — which is Batch 0003's proof, not this batch's.
