## What Volume 2 handed you

**The position.** He holds band six on an instrument and not on his plate. **Plate 8,138, floor 8,141,
tolerance three, 11,862 under his own band, and nothing the account did was ever converted into a grain.**
The ledger stands at **112 lines, eight turned, one of them reading himself.** File 4,112, **one folio
open and no second.** The branch standing is discharged and the fourth floor with it. **The dates are now
somebody else's.** The band is **suspended pending description**, which is a worse shape and not a loss.

**What he learned, and it is a correction rather than a discovery.** A ledger entry is a pair — a person
relieved and a person it came from. His line has one name on it. It reads *to*, not *from*, from a side
of the entry with no name on it. **He is not a tank, he is a door.** Ines Duarte can write that in a
fortnight and **has refused to**, on their schedule rather than forever. **The line is unfiled and legible
to two auditors. He has never seen it.**

**The threat.** Not classification — the classification paper is unsigned and has gone back down three
floors twice. **The threat is that the Office can only hold him in the open by standing him still, and
standing him still is costing other people.** He gave up every calendar he had to stay undescribed. Three
women have paid for that decision in their own handwriting.

**The question Volume 3 exists to answer.** *If the inquiry becomes a Reading, the map becomes a list, and
a list becomes a summons for every feeder.* Ch 100's last line is the volume's whole engine: **the
measurement will not be a description of him. It will be a description of everyone who ever fed him.**

The outline must carry, at minimum:

   as** — Volume 2 learned that by restating nothing. Read `reviews/volume-02/volume-close.md` §4.

   an option. The resolution is a consequence, not a neutral action. **The account does not score a grain

   *learning what it is to refusing to be described by anybody*. **Volume 3 must move the flaw again.**

9. **Planned power and relationship progression.** No rank earned, no rank lost, no grain from the
   account. Ledger rises with stated cause; turned count rises and never falls. At most one system
   message per chapter, only where the count turns, never repeated.
