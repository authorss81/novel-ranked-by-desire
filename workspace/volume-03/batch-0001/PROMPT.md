## The file that is NOT to be trusted over the prose

The prose is the canon. If a page and a state file disagree, the page is right until somebody has read both
and said otherwise. **A state file asserting a figure is not evidence that the figure is right** — the two
most consequential defects in this repository's history were a month length and a weekday that state files
asserted and a reviewer then "corrected" two correct chapters to match its own inference. **An inference is
not an anchor.** Derive every date from something printed on a page.

## What this batch must land

   the question is *may a member's account remain undescribed*. It is a fair question and the answer is
   already legally no. Volume 2 was an office asking *what are you* behind a door; **Volume 3 asks the same
   question in a hall with the doors open, and that is the whole of the change.**
2. **Yusuf Adeyemi enters at 103 and is not an enemy.** He is honest about his profession, willing to be

3. **Mid-batch turn, 105: his name is not on the Board.** The strip stopped when the standing was
   discharged. Four months of being publicly mismeasured end, and what replaces it is not relief. **A man who
   has spent two volumes refusing to be measured in public discovers in one square that being unmeasured
   publicly means nobody has to care about him.**
4. **Scene at 106, Marguerite Vale.** She asks to be made dangerous on his behalf — the first time anybody
   in this book has asked to be a weapon. Adults, awake, unrestrained, unmedicated, no impaired state,
   **no draw spent** (a spend would turn a line and no line turns in this batch), and violence and sex in
   separate registers. No age stated. Her terms stay down, unsigned and out of force.
5. **The exhibit roll at 108 is made of documents, not people**, and every document belongs to somebody in
   his household. The Office does not ask the household. It asks about the household.
6. **Irreversible decision, 109.** He signs the acknowledgement of the roll with a written objection
   attached, and the objection excludes **the household notebook** — six women's record, which is not his and
   which he has never been allowed to sign. **Excluding it names it in a document that goes to a registrar,
   which puts six women's record into a Bureau file by being excluded from one.** He cannot withdraw it and
   the option of standing outside his own file closes.
7. **Resolution at 110 is a consequence, not a neutral action.** The objection is granted in a single line
   by a clerk who was not told why, and the answering line **describes the notebook without naming it** — six

8. **The hinge into Batch 0002.** The roll closes, the hearing is confirmed for the day already printed on

## Discipline, binding

- **No rank earned. No rank lost. No grain scored from the account. Plate 8,138 in every chapter that
  prints a figure.** No chapter in this volume contains a number that would let a grain convert.
- **No draw is spent anywhere in this batch.** Ten chapters, no spend, no cold minute, **the turned count
  stands at eight from 101 to 110 and does not move.** He has not spent since the Review.
- **HARD LOCK ON WHAT A SPEND CAN EVER BE — carry this for the whole volume.** `bible/power-system.md`
  rule three: the account moves **only on contact with the person it came from**, and the failure clause is
  explicit — a hand on a stranger moves nothing and costs him grey at one temple, a grey night, and a night
  of sleep like being woken on the hour, proportional to what he was trying to move, and it never improves.
  **He cannot relieve a room and no chapter in this book may have him touch a stranger and have anything
  happen.** The only legal movements are the hundred and twelve people who fed him and himself. This matters
  to this batch because Batch 0002 carries Wren Kallo and the reversal, and the reversal only works if the
  only other movement available in the Cobb Street hall is him.
- **The ledger rises only with stated cause** — work: washrooms, a corridor, a queue, a doorway. About 112 to
  about 114. **Name the cause on the page where the rise happens or do not print the rise.**
- **Expect zero system panels.** No count turns in this batch, so there is nothing for one to report. A
  system message is square brackets on its own line, plain language, never bold, never italic, never a
  heading, never batched with anything, at most one per chapter.
- **No age is stated in narration or in dialogue for anybody, named or walk-on.** Ivo Sarn is forty-six and
  that number lives in `state/open-threads.md`, not on a page. Three false certifications about ages have
  happened in this repository.
- **Rationed constructions.** `He wanted, concretely` — spent. `For <person>, his <verb> meant` — Ch 41, 44
  and 48 only. `nobody thanked <object>` — **retired outright, never with any object.** `nobody asked` — the
  *note* is spent, the *motif* is available. `there is no form for this` — **Chapters 101–110 may not use
  it at all**, because this volume's engine is a form that *does* exist and requires a reason; use the
  specific form, not the general observation. `there is no column for it` — spent at Ch 1 and again at Ch
  52, **do not use it before Volume 7**. `the record remained` — vary or cut.
- **Chapter endings vary across the ten.** **Check what the page actually ends on** — test whether the final
  paragraph *contains* the construction, not whether it *begins* with it, the way the Volume 2 audit had to
  for Chapters 42–49. No two consecutive chapters may close on the same shape.
- **Emotional temperature varies.** 101 flat and cold, 102 intimate and unhelpful, 103 vertigo, 104 dread
  through other people, 105 the hollow middle, 106 pleasure and warmth, 107 uncanny, 108 the roll,
  109 anger, 110 a worse-than-nothing bargain. **Give Lin Hao one felt emotion and one concrete want in
  every chapter.**
- **Nothing is classified. No folio opens. No draw is spent. The word *Channel* is not spoken and routing is
  not named, learned, used or implied.**
- **Do not open:** the second folio · Ines's second refusal or her pending review · *he is not a tank, he is
  a door* · Ding's chest · the May clipboard · the rota word · September's outcome · Marguerite's terms ·
  Bernt's absence · the Church of the Quiet Number, **which has eleven thousand members and belongs to
  Volume 4** · the word *Channel* · Rhee · Saskia Verhoeven as a character · Alma Quayle · Anouk Ferreira ·
  the Council of Nine by name · anything after Volume 8.
- **Do not close:** Sun Meiling's refusal of the household's protection logic — she is not even in the room
  at 109, her scene is 149, and the refusal advances at 113 and 131 · Runa's disciplinary hearing, which is
