## What the volume's last movement turned out to be about

Not weather. **A door that is being used by everybody for something the office did not put it up for, and a man who finds out that the one person it was built for is the one person it cannot reach.**

- **941** — a person can be produced, and not by this office, and the practice requires the subject to be present and says nothing about the subject having asked.
- **942** — the card's hours run past the end of the light, so he becomes the man at his own door, four times a night, saying his own address in the dark. And the book's back board opens in the frost and he does not send it to a binder.
- **943** — the scene. The last scene in the book. What a door with hours on it is worth to a person with no gift: it is the only address in this city a person can find without producing anything, and she does not want it back.
- **944** — the mechanism, proved in a room: every road that card is on ends at somebody looking for a plate.
- **945** — the memory. The only archive in this city has never been checked and she has been wrong four times in twenty years.
- **946** — the card comes off three nails and goes back on the same three nails with nothing written on it, and he cannot read it in daylight and can read it in the dark.
- **947** — the three lines leave by a mouth, and not one of the rules covers a mouth.
- **948** — the boy, the bare rail, and a man who nearly gummied three lines to a public board and did not.
- **949** — the last asking of the year, and a wrong copy produced by nobody taking a decision.
- **950** — **the card renewed in ink**, the head printed at a counter under a window in front of strangers, the rail broken at ninety, and a promise made at the bottom of his own stair that he cannot enter and is not going to.
