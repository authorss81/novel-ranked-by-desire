**THE IRREVERSIBLE DECISIONS ARE 295, 296 AND 298, AND ONLY 296 IS THE ONE THE VOLUME TURNS ON.**

- **295, and it is his.** He asks a broker to enter the mark of the turn of the quarter against a house she
  sleeps in, and she refuses, and makes him give her a **dated written undertaking that he will not ask her
  again**. **That paper is the first document in this book that is about him and not about his household,
  and it is in four divisions by the end of the week, and it is his own handwriting refusing himself.**
- **296, and it is his.** At ten in the morning on the sixteenth he goes down two flights of a cold stair,
  on the last day a woman is allowed to be the only person in this city who can write a figure about him, and
  **he does not ask her for the sentence under the column. He describes the account** — that it is fed at one
  address, and that the address is his — because she told him in July that she would not price a thing she
  could not describe. **She prices it in ninety seconds and the price is seven**, dated, initialled, not
  signed. **Volume 6's failure condition is fulfilled to the letter and at the lead's own request**, and it
  is unrefusable because a dated figure in a real book is a fact and not an offer.
- **298, and it is his.** **He does not enter a name in the seventh returned line.** The window is twenty-two
  days long, the instrument is in a pencil margin saying that only the head may fill a blank line, and it is
  lawful, and nobody would have asked him why. He lets the book close blank, in front of the woman it is
  about, and says the reason out loud in a kitchen.

**THE SCENE IS 299 AND IT IS BEATRIX HALM'S**, and it is not a fade. See §4.2.

## 2. WHAT THE BATCH ESTABLISHED THAT A LATER BATCH MUST NOT MISREAD

**THE RESERVOIR IS NOW PRICED. That is a change and it is the batch's whole point.**

`bible/power-system.md` says the reservoir is an account with no number: it cannot raise his rank, cannot be
taken by force, cannot be traded, and cannot make a woman want him. **None of those four limits is violated,
because none of them is about being described.** A keeper of real books cannot price a thing she cannot
describe, and what he gave her on the sixteenth was a description: **fed at one address, nowhere else, for
four years, because of what he is and where he stands.** That is describable. **The price she wrote under his
column is seven, and the line reads that an unrecorded account standing against no ledger is carried at the
address of premises 34-6-118 and answerable by the composition of that premises.**

**What it costs, and why it is unrefusable.** It cannot be called back. The only answer it can be given is a
production, and a production is a figure of persons answerable at the premises on the day, and he swore one
at ten to nine on the first of October, in his own hand, in nine seconds, **for the second time in his life
and on the same chained book.** And in about four years the examination passes to whoever is standing in that
room, **and that person will be answerable for seven people they have never met and will not be able to ask
one of them a question.**

**And it does not classify him. That is deliberate and it is the handoff.** The line carries **an address and
a figure and no name anywhere**, and **a classification does not need a man in it. It needs an address, and
a figure, and a reason.** He has put all three on a page and he has not been classified. **He has made
himself describable, which is worse, because describable means somebody else can write the sentence.**

**THE FIGURE OF 8,138 IS ANSWERED, NOT PAID AND NOT ERASED.** An answered figure stays in a column under an
address. Chapter 300 says so in terms and it is the volume's last arithmetic.

## 3. WHAT WAS BROKEN, AND WHAT IT COST

**The tool failure of Batch 0004 did not recur, and that is a decision and not an accident.**

**No paragraph-length splitter was run over this prose at any floor, at any point in the batch.** The only
automated pass was a reporter that prints paragraph lengths and writes nothing. **Twelve paragraphs in the
first draft were over ninety words. All twelve were split by hand at a speech beat, and the whole-file
quotation count was checked after each one.** The rule that came out of Batch 0004 was followed exactly: **a
dialogue paragraph opens with a quotation mark and closes with one, and no speech runs across a paragraph
break.**

## 4. WHAT A REVIEWER SHOULD CHECK FIRST, AND WHY

1. **Read Chapter 296 as a whole and ask whether he could have refused it.** That is the test of an
   irreversible decision and not a moral one. **He could have refused it, she told him so in the room, and he
   could have had a described account that nobody prices, which cannot be called, cannot be answered and
   cannot be requisitioned. He gave that up for a number, because a number he can carry is smaller than a
   number he cannot find.** If that does not read as a man being frightened rather than a man being brave,
   the batch's engine has failed.
2. **Check Chapter 299 for a fade.** Batch 0004's reviewer found 289 was built correctly and still ended in
   one sentence saying it *went on a long time*. **299 was written against that finding.** It is directed by
   her, she consents, she says what she wants and is told to say it, and the beat that changes the
   relationship is the margin correction she will not let him initial. **Search for `afterwards`: it occurs
   twice in the batch and neither is a scene standing in for a sentence.**
3. **Check that Chapter 298's decision is on the page and not in a summary.** He does not enter the seventh
   line. Ines Duarte makes him say her name as the reason, out loud, in a kitchen with seven women in it, and
   she will not let him carry her reason for her.
4. **Re-run the 12-gram scan against all 300 and believe the re-run over this block.** §5 states the
   tokenisation in both variants because Batch 0004's block reported a number without one.
5. **Check the arithmetic out loud against `state/continuity.md` §2.** Three of the ten chapters print a day
   count and **all three were wrong on the first pass** and are right now.
6. **Check that nothing in the batch routes.** The promise of the twenty-seventh of August is tested three
   times — in 291, in 297 and in the shape of Runa Sato's refusal — and **it holds on the honest reason and
   not on the convenient one, and the routing has still not happened.**
7. **Check that the reservoir is priced and that the price is the household, not a figure of merit.**
   `bible/power-system.md` is not contradicted: nothing was spent, nothing was taken, nothing was traded and
   nobody was made to want anybody.
8. **Check that he is not classified and that Volume 7 has its sentence.** He is described. Nobody has opened
   a room on it. **No final enemy was introduced and nothing after Volume 8 was opened.**
