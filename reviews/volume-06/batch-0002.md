## 1. WHAT THE BATCH IS

Ten chapters, one a day, **the twenty-first to the thirtieth of July**. Batch 0001 performed the first half
of the volume's sentence — he called the debts in — and this batch performs the second half: **the account
of what he owes is now in a stranger's book, and the instrument that would end it comes back into the room
without needing the one woman he could have gone to.**

**THE IRREVERSIBLE DECISIONS OF THE BATCH ARE 261, 264, 265 AND 266, and all four are his.**

- **261.** He is told the last room in the city that did not have him in it, and he walks into it and buys a
  seat at the back for twenty pence. **The option that closes is anonymity in the last place he had it.**
  The cost is public and permanent: a verse has named a block of flats and the household is public from
  Chapter 261 for the rest of the volume.
- **264.** He asks Solveig Rask to reconcile the account against its broker's own book. **She tells him the
  price before he says the word** — a dated page in a real book, in four divisions by the end of the week,
  with a broker's name on it — and he says *do it* anyway, once, without qualifying it. **The option that
  closes is his broker's standing in four divisions**, and he spends it to answer a question about who called
  his standing on the twentieth of July.
- **265.** He goes to Cordwain intending to have a two-part carbon priced, which would have converted Runa
  Sato's hearing into a secured hearing, and he pushes it across the counter to her instead. **The option
  that closes is the only thing he had been able to buy anybody with this month.** He agrees to give up the
  hearing's security on the strength of a request made to him in person.
- **266.** He signs a **change of auditor of record** with his own name and the twenty-sixth of July under
  it, which is what puts Sun Meiling's row into ink — **on the first of September, not on the first of
  August.** **The price is Ines Duarte's office**, and he signs it in a kitchen, out loud, with his reason
  stated, and he still has no lawful way to tell her.

**THE BATCH'S ONE SCENE IS CH 261 AND IT IS IMMOVABLE.** Isolde Marchetti, singer at the Bellhouse. It is
Volume 6's first scene and its first explicit scene. **No scene is written anywhere in 262–270.** The budget
is 261, 269, 279, 289, 299 and only 261 has been spent.

4. **Zero system panels, and the zero is structural: the batch contains no `[` character at all.** Nothing is

5. **The Bellhouse house is nine hundred people, not four hundred.** The first draft had four hundred in

6. **`269` carries no explicit scene.** The prompt for this batch is explicit — *there is an explicit scene

## 5. WHAT THE GATES DO NOT TELL YOU

**Nine chapters sit inside the band and one is a scene, and the one that leaves the band is the one the
prompt says is immovable. That is the correct answer and it should be checked rather than assumed.**

**The batch's own failure mode was not a date. It was a rule invented and then restated wrongly.** Three
instruments are introduced in ten chapters — a change of auditor of record, the validity of a hearing paper,
and the acknowledgement day for a change — and each was written once, correctly, and then referenced from a
memory of the first version rather than from the first version. All three are fixed. **A later batch
introducing an instrument should write its rule once, in full, and never refer back to it by paraphrase.**

**The three strongest turns in the batch are the ones where a woman refuses something he could have bought
her** — Runa at the counter, Sun in the kitchen, Marguerite on the twenty-eighth — and in each case the
refusal costs her and it is still hers. That is the escalation the volume is for and it did not need a
system panel to produce.

**One thing the batch could not do and did not pretend to do: the hundred and thirty-fourth mark.** It falls
at ten to nine on the first of August, which is one day outside a batch that runs one day per chapter from
the twenty-first to the thirtieth. **What Batch 0002 delivers instead is everything that decides it** — the
called standing, the pen, the bundled docket, Petra at the foot of the steps, the floor that went up on the
thirtieth, the composition hearing that may adjourn it, and the term he agreed in the dark on the ninth of
July, which is what Ines Duarte's own hand was for. **The mark belongs to Chapter 271.**
