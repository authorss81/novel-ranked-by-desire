## 1 · WHAT THE BATCH OWES ITS WRITER, AND WHAT IT REFUSED

**THE MOVEMENT'S ARGUMENT IN ONE SENTENCE.** The instrument Lin Hao chose instead of a number was a card, and a card is a thing that decays, and the only true copy of it is a man who cannot read the end of his own second line; a kindness welded onto those three lines in January has finished becoming a different door; a statement in three hands is not three witnesses; and he un-said the only irreversible act Volume 20 gave him, in daylight, in the court where he said it.

## 2 · WHAT THE PAGE GAINS, AND IT IS CANON FOR 1011 ONWARD

1. **THE CARD IS GOING, NOT GOING SLOW, AND THE ONLY TRUE COPY IS A MAN WHO CANNOT READ THE END OF HIS OWN SECOND LINE.** Four words gone on the twentieth of January, further than on the third of December, and the card is a year and twenty days old.
2. **THE FOURTH CLAUSE HAS ARRIVED AT THE DOOR.** It was a true thing about the light at ten past four, said at a counter under a window in the last week of January of year thirteen by a man being kind, and by January of year fourteen it is a door at the top of three flights, and nobody invented it.
3. **A STATEMENT IN THREE HANDS IS NOT THREE WITNESSES.** One hand cannot be checked at all; four hands is stronger than two; three is worse than two because two people disagreeing is a thing a person can go and look at. **All three of the hands are his.**
4. **THE RENEWAL IS THE ONLY INSTRUMENT THAT OFFICE OWNS AND IT WAS NOT USED IN THIS BATCH.** A renewal is the same sentence said by the same man in a different month with a pen in his hand, and the month is the whole of it, and a sentence at a top of a door is true of everybody at once, which is why it is better and why it is the wrong instrument for the pavement and the right one for the door. **The card is on three nails at 1010 and the renewal is still at 1045.**
5. **THE BOOK IS LIGHTER AND THE TICK IS GONE, AND THE ONLY PERSON WHO COULD HEAR THAT BOOK IS THE ONE WHO LOST IT.** The back board was re-laid at the end of September and the sound of that book going down four flights at about four seconds a time went with it, and a woman with a bad back on the second landing is the only person in this city who knew, and has told nobody for months.
6. **THE AUTHORITY LEFT THE CARD AND THE ROOM AND THE STAIR.** The first mouth was a counter under a window, a man paid nothing, telling a stranger who never spoke to him again.
7. **FOUR FEET OF PAVEMENT NOW CORRECTS STRANGERS OUT LOUD.** One correction was right and went unrecorded; one wrong variant went uncorrected; nothing comes up four flights, nothing goes to the counter under the window, nothing goes in a book, and that is not because anybody kept it out.
