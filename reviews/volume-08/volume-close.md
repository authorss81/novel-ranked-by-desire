## 1. What the volume promised, and whether it paid

> In this volume, **Lin Hao must find what his brother found and keep the woman who told him**, or **the only
> account he has ever had closes on him and he is a man with a number**.

**The answer in one sentence a stranger could read: he kept the woman who told him, he did not find one word of
what his brother found, and the volume closes with the only account he has ever had still open and a figure
fifteen points under his band painted on a wall in a public square.**

**THE SECOND HALF OF THE SENTENCE IS PAID, IN FULL, AND IT COST FOUR THINGS.** The woman who told him is
**Sun Meiling** (§1a, settled at 372 and not re-opened). He has not asked her the one question he is not
permitted to ask — he is barred from asking **Wren Kallo**, and Wren has never told him whom (§1, 350's closing
beat, standing through 397 and 400). And on the morning of the thirtieth of March:

- **The card comes down at six and prints a floor of 8,146 and, against premises 34-6-118, the figure 8,131**
  (400, read off the wall by Beatrix Halm in the dark before it is four inches off the board). **Fifteen under a
  floor of 8,146. The tolerance is three, so it is out of band by twelve.**
- **The two words went up the wall in paint at about four the same afternoon** (400).
- **Fourteen points down for fourteen points up, ninety days apart, and nobody did anything wrong.** The fourteen
  went up on the thirtieth of December by eight hands on four sheets of plain paper. The fifteen came down
  because a card reads a held figure at the composition of the premises, and the composition has been **seven**
  since nine forty-one on the morning of the thirteenth of March, when Nadia Ferrous gave the address up at a
  counter. `PC-49` and `PC-50` are the written-out cause and both are on the page in a room with a figure on it.
- **And it is not his fault.** He says so in 400 and so does Beatrix Halm: *nobody in this city is going to be
  able to tell me whose fault it is and that is the entire point of it.*

**THE FIRST HALF OF THE SENTENCE IS NOT PAID, AND THE VOLUME KNOWS IT.** Chapter 372 opens Lin Wei's file and
what comes out of it is **a transfer receipt, an index and four lines**. There is no name of his anywhere in it,
**and there was never a place on the form where a name could have gone** — which is `PC-45` written down at 389
as the exact inverse of 372. `outline/volume-08.md` §8 calls this the volume's best turn.

**It is a turn in the reader's favour and not in his, and the audit's judgement is that it still pays.** The
reason is on the page and not in the summary: **his brother found out what a man becomes when he is measured,
and the measurement is taken by somebody else and written on a form that has no box for the subject.** A brother
who reached a file and found his own name absent from it is a man who has learned the thing the whole book has
been circling — that legibility in this city is a thing other people grant you and can withdraw. He did not get
his brother back. He got the shape of the machine that took him. **The volume's want is not satisfied and the
volume's argument is complete**, and a reader is not mis-served by that; a reader is mis-served by a volume that
pretends the want was met, and this one does not pretend. Chapter 400 states the standing position in eight words
in Beatrix Halm's mouth, and there is no scene anywhere in 351–400 in which he is told what his brother found.

### **C5 — THE FIFTEEN-CHAPTER BREACH. THE DECISION IS THE STRUCTURAL FACT, AND THE REASON IS RECORDED.**

`outline/volume-08.md` §4 carries the arithmetic in a fifth column and asks this audit to decide whether the
structural fact or the interval rule stands. **The structural fact stands. Four reasons, and the third is the
one that settles it:**

1. **The breach is forced by canon, not chosen.** `PC-44` says Dagmar Oyelowo has no gift at all and is the only
   partner in the book with none, and that her chapter may not manufacture one. 388 was in the schedule to hold
   the gap at eight. It is written, it is 3,374 words, it is not a sex scene, and it says on the page why
   (*a woman with no gift at all had put three things in front of him and he had given her nothing back*, 388:201).
   **A rule breached because canon forbids the only available repair is not a rule the writer may fix.**
2. **The repair is a structural change, and a close phase may not make one.** Moving 398 three chapters earlier
   would close the arithmetic and was not done, because Wren Kallo's is the volume's last scene and moving it
   trades a structural fact for a metric one. `PHASE_SYSTEM.md`: *a volume audit must not attempt to rewrite the
   entire volume.* `AGENTS.md`: repairs are applied *only where a reader is actively mis-served.* **A reader is
   not mis-served by 398 being where it is.**
3. **The concern the rule exists to protect was answered on the page, and §4 records that it was.** The rule is
   a proxy for *do not leave the reader alone with institutions for fifteen chapters.* **Every one of the ten
   chapters of 391–400 carries a woman in it and what she wants** — Runa Sato and the arithmetic of the quarter ·
   Marguerite Vale and her own name in a fourth line · Mrs Imrie, who will not speak to him at all, and Dagmar
   Oyelowo, who says his name · Wren Kallo, twice, the second time in his own rooms · Beatrix Halm and a wall in
   daylight. **The breach stands as a fact about the volume's calendar and not about its people.**
4. **A reader arriving cold at 398 is not harmed by the gap.** In a serial, chapter boundaries are not load
   bearing. The gap matters to a table, not to a reader.

**VOLUME 9 INHERITS THE RULE AND MAY NOT REPEAT THE BREACH.** If a partner in Volume 9 has no gift, the scene
schedule must be built so the explicit count closes without manufacturing one.

### **C2 — REPAIRED. THE CARD, RESTATED IN 400 IN 391'S WORDS.**

**391:101**, Runa Sato explaining the card to him on the twenty-first of March:

**WHY A READER IS ACTIVELY MIS-SERVED HERE AND NOT AT THE SIX MATCHES BELOW.** 400 is the chapter the volume

**REPAIR APPLIED.** 400 now opens: *The card comes down at six, and it is not put up by the office that keeps
the plate: a different office, a different hand, different paper, and a boy with a paste brush and a list of

## 6. The three claims only this audit can settle

### **C6 — `PC-28`. THE AUDIT'S DECISION IS THAT VOLUME 8 MAY BE CLOSED WITH THE DEFECT IN IT. IT IS RECORDED AND CARRIED FORWARD AND NOT FIXED.**

**What it is, verified on disk and not from the record.** `chapter-0359.md` is dated the nineteenth of January

either figure.** Three numbers in one section are wrong and the section's own head is a fourth. **No chapter in

371–380 or 381–390 carries any figure from 359's section head either. **The defect is sealed: nothing downstream
can propagate it.**

**WHY IT IS CARRIED AND NOT FIXED, IN THE FORM `reviews/volume-07/volume-close.md` USED AT C2 — *RECORDED, NOT
REPAIRED* — and with three reasons.**

1. **It is not a canon error, and every one of the four repairs above was.** A borrowed body is a second person
   carrying the first person's claim, and `AGENTS.md` §4 requires it to come out. **`PC-28` is a miscount inside

2. **The repair is not mechanical and this audit is the wrong authority for it.** Three numbers have to change

   what the volume promised and what it paid, not a re-characterisation of the lead four movements before the
   fall.**
3. **Nothing propagates.** No chapter in 371–400 uses the section's figures. The cost of carrying it is a reader

   character in fifty chapters.**

**CARRIED FORWARD AND NOT FIXED. It is recorded in `state/open-threads.md` in those words, and Volume 9's

### **C7 — `outline/volume-08.md` §5a's UNRESOLVED READING. CHAPTER 400 SETTLES THE INTERVAL AND THE FIGURE AND LEAVES THE NAMING — AND IT MAKES THE UNRESOLVED PART SHARPER.**

**The reading §5a recorded and left standing:** the card of the thirtieth of July, August, September and
December is called a *quarterly card* in Volume 7's own text, while the thirtieth is also *the last working day
of a quarter*, and **both readings stand.**

**What Volume 7's own text actually says, read on disk.** Chapter 328, line 115, **in Lin Hao's mouth**:

> **The quarterly goes up on the last working day of a quarter by a different office in a different hand.**
> 8,142 on the thirtieth of July. 8,143 on the thirtieth of August. 8,144 on the thirtieth of September. 8,145
> on the thirtieth of December, which is the card this run ends on.

**One sentence puts both halves of the tension in the same mouth.** On a calendar where a month is thirty days,
**August and September are consecutive months and cannot both be the last working day of a quarter.** That is
the reading §5a refused to resolve, and it is unresolved in the prose, not only in the outline.

**WHAT 400 SETTLES, AND IT IS THE OPERATIVE HALF.**

- **The card of the thirtieth of March is a printed fact and it prints 8,146.** §5a's 8,146 was an inference from
  four data points and was labelled as one so that nobody would mistake it for a printed figure. **It is now a
  figure printed on a card in a chapter, exactly as §5a required and exactly as Volume 7 did at 328.** The
  inference is promoted to fact and `PC-50` is its entry.
- **The floor rises by exactly one at every card, and the three cards that carry the volume are the thirtieth of
  September (8,144), the thirtieth of December (8,145) and the thirtieth of March (8,146).** Chapters 327:101
  and 328:67 both state in two women's mouths that **the floor stays at 8,144 until six on the thirtieth of
  December** — so no card between the thirtieth of September and the thirtieth of December raised it.
- **Therefore the card IS a quarter-end and the three are ninety days apart.** A card is a quarter-end, it prints
  a floor, and it reads the standing figure of named premises at that premises' composition at the hour it is
  read — which is `PC-49`, written out in a room with a figure on it and the only cause a fall has had in this
  volume.
- **And the card carries a premises-level figure and not only a floor.** That is new: Volume 7's recitation at
  328:115 carried floors only. **It is the operational change that makes the fall possible at all**, and it is
  what makes the thirtieth of March the volume's last day rather than an ordinary one.

**WHAT 400 LEAVES, AND IT IS NAMED HERE SO VOLUME 9 DOES NOT INHERIT IT AS SETTLED.**

- **The thirtieth of July and the thirtieth of August, recited at 328:115 as card dates, cannot both be
  quarter-ends, and on the settled interval one of them is wrong.** 8,142 and 8,143 are printed in Volume 7's
  text and are not reconcilable with a rise of one at each of three consecutive quarter-ends. **THE AUDIT DOES
  NOT REPAIR CHAPTER 328.** It is a different volume, its batch is closed, and the change would be an edit to a

- **Nothing in 351–400 says whether the thirtieth of June, the thirtieth of October or the thirtieth of November

- **THE ANSWER, IN ONE SENTENCE: Chapter 400 settles what a card is, when it comes down, what it prints and
  how often the floor rises, and it settles that the floor rises by one at each of the quarter-ends of September,
  December and March; it does not settle why Volume 7 called a July card and an August card the same thing it
