## 1. THE VOLUME PROMISE

`outline/volume-15.md` §3: **Lin Hao must find out who is using about four hundred pieces of pencil paper
and against whom**, or the receipt he entered on the thirtieth of August goes on being the only answer in
Ostara to a person asking whether what happened to her happened.

**IT LANDED, AT 719, AND IT WAS PAID FOR ON THE PAGE.** Emil Brack, in a lane off Cordwain, with a slate
under his arm: *about four hundred pieces of pencil paper. Every one of them is against one of four men,
and the four men work for one house* — one signs for the works and stands at a yard gate about four days in
a month, one takes a cart and has not given a name in about nine years, one goes round about six sets of
stairs with a rent book, and one sits in an office above a shop off Cordwain and writes every letter the
other three have ever had. Nine pounds on every one of them, the same nine pounds since the ninth month of
last year. `chapter-0719.md`:22–43.

**BY THE MECHANISM THAT WAS PLANNED: YES, AND BY NO OTHER.** The outline's §1 promise was that a claim can
be given a day and that an age is the only figure this city acts on. What actually delivers the answer is
not the column and not the fourth sheet of Perla Quist. It is a man who counts heads at a stall for a
living, is right about every fact, is not thanked, is not diminished, and says the answer **first** and
then says what he wants, which is that it not be printed. The volume's fourth column dates claims; what it
delivers is that somebody had already been counting the same thing for money and nobody had ever asked
him.

**THE COST, STATED ON THE PAGE AND NOT IN A STATE FILE:** at 719 Brack says that if it is printed about
forty people will say it came from Lin Hao, and then about four hundred people will stop writing in the
bottom two inches of their own paper, because a claim everybody knows came from a building is a claim that
waits to be told, and it will wait about four years, and not one of the four hundred has four years of
waiting left in him. `chapter-0719.md`:47–73. That cost is the whole plot of movement five and it is
brought on by 746.

**ONE THING THE RECORD GETS WRONG AND THE PAGE DOES NOT.** `state/current.md`'s batch-0005 block and
`state/open-threads.md`'s both say the volume "is the stage where what he wrote is used to date a person".
The page says something harder. At 719 he learns who the paper is against and is told the answer in the
same breath; at 727 a claim with a day beats a claim without one and the man it is about is not in the
room and does not know there was a day; at 736 a clerk entitled to one question a day spends it on the
rail and Lin Hao answers *no* to his own receipt about himself. **The dating is not the point. The point is
that a man built an office nobody could reach and the office is now reachable, and being reachable is what
it costs.**
