## 4. WHAT THE GATES DO NOT CATCH

- **A count is not a test of whether a scene happened.** Batch 0002 proved it. This batch reports both and the probe is read as words.
- **A gate record is a claim.** Every row in `state/open-threads.md` was produced by command against the files after the last edit, with tokenisation and scope stated. The 12-gram ran first.
- **Documents quoted in one chapter and referred to in another are the sharpest duplicate source.** This batch paraphrases on every referral: standing order/pad, eight lines/fair copy, the list, the priced line, the quarterly floor.
