**THE TWO IRREVERSIBLE DECISIONS ARE BOTH HIS AND NEITHER IS REVERSIBLE.**

**The first is the eleven days.** He knows the band will fall, he knows an account under its floor is callable, and he knows the books that could call it are in hands he did not appoint because nine days earlier he put them there on purpose in front of nine people. He does it anyway, and the stranger lives, and she does not die and will not walk again, and nobody in this book who stays dies.

**The second is 336 and it is worse.** He breaks the standing undertaking of the ninth of July — one hundred and forty-one days old and never once tested — by carrying a leather wallet up two flights of a laundry stair and asking the one woman in this city he swore never to hand a paper to, **in her own rooms**. **The option closed: the term does not exist again.**

## 2. WHAT IT CHANGED

- **The plate has fallen for the first time in this book and it keeps falling.** 8,184 on the fifteenth; 8,139 on the twenty-second; 8,131 on the twenty-ninth. Forty-five points down in the first week he did nothing, eight in the second. **He is eight under the floor and the two words the square has carried since the thirtieth of July are back on the wall.**
- **The account of thirty years is callable at the valuation on the first of December at ten to nine** and there is no production off that door to answer it with. **The instrument that could call it belongs to a woman he unprotected on purpose nine days earlier.**
- **A turned person keeps her count in her own writing on her own hand, and a doctor is the person who would have to name the source.** That is why eleven days of nothing is the only instrument available, and it is why not spending turns nobody while doing nothing else costs somebody everything.
- **Wanda Rej empties the bins on a fourth floor that is on nobody's list and does not die and will not walk again**, and she made him promise that the four marks do not go any further.
- **Ines Duarte read the classification in her own rooms and lost a day she cannot date**, and kept the four sheets in a drawer under her table with a note on top of them that no office in this city knows is there.
- **She read it back to him out loud afterwards, for eleven minutes, and told him nothing is read in that room again — not to her and not by him.** The ninth of July was about what was done to her in a room and never meant she could not read a paper back at a man.
- **She lodged, on the ordinary stream, in her own name, a request to be served with every sheet of file 4,112 and the whole of the check column in advance of the valuation of the first of December.** Not protection. The only door she has.
- **Wren Kallo certified the composition of the address she sleeps in and a false attestation is sitting in her division's file and she has chosen to leave it there**, and the man who would want it gone is forbidden by his own undertaking from asking her anything about that address.
- **The classification is described and not completed. The Concil was not named. Nothing was routed. Lin Wei's file is shut until 372.**

## 3. WHAT THE REPAIR PASS FOUND AND FIXED

2. **THE ELEVEN-DATE COUNT HAD TO BE SAID TWICE BY THE CARD, AND SAYING IT TWICE MADE IT A DUPLICATE.** Ten windows across 331 and 339. Resolved by making it a **framed** return — 339 names what it is coming back to before it comes back, Sun Meiling finishes the count, and 339 counts it **backwards**, which is what a chapter that ends a count should do.
3. **A CONSTRUCTION THIS BATCH BUILT FOUR TIMES WHILE CORRECTING ITS OWN DATE ARITHMETIC.** After the date fix, "of the eleven still to run, the last of them ending at midnight at the end of the twenty-ninth" appeared in 333, 334, 335 and 336 and the 12-gram gate returned **29 windows**. **All four rewritten four different ways.** This is the clearest argument in the batch for running the gate after the prose, not before it.
4. **FIFTY-THREE PARAGRAPHS WERE OVER NINETY WORDS AND EVERY ONE WAS SPLIT BY HAND**, at a speech beat or at a sentence beat inside a narration paragraph, with a line of dialogue opened and closed where a speech was divided. No splitter was run at any floor. **Over-90 0, over-120 0.**
5. **THE HAND SPLITS BROKE FIVE QUOTATION MARKS AND THE WHOLE-FILE QUOTE-BALANCE GATE FOUND ALL FIVE.** Three paragraphs were left with an unclosed speech and two with a doubled closing mark. ## 4. WHAT THE GATES DO NOT SEE

- **A plate falling is not a punishment and the chapter that says so had to be written, not asserted.** 334 exists because the band going down is only a mechanic until a woman who attested to it reads it and says she would do it again and that it got her nothing.
- **A cost paid by refusing to act is invisible on every instrument this project owns.** There is no gate that reads *what he did not do*. The eleven days pass every mechanical check in this file and are the most consequential eleven days in the volume.
- **A framed refrain is allowed to repeat and an unframed one is not.** The eleven dates are said twice because a card requires it; they are framed, and the second telling counts backwards.
- **Two new named characters are on the page and neither has an age:** **Wanda Rej** (bins, eleven years, a heart going since the spring, four pencil marks, a broken leg, a promise) and **Herta Vogel** (laundry, one of the three attestations, would do it again). **Ages are fixed for neither in this file or any other and none may be given on the page.**
- **A LIVING AUTHORISATION IS NOT A DEBT.** Ines Duarte's request of the thirtieth goes on the ordinary stream and is not a favour, is not invoiced, and must not be converted into one.

## 6. LESSON

**The 12-gram gate caught this batch's worst defect and it caught it only because it was run first and run again after every prose edit.** Forty-nine duplicate groups on the first run, and then twenty-nine more that this batch created *while fixing something else* — the same interval, said the same way, in four consecutive chapters, put there by a date correction. A gate run once, at the end, from memory, by the pass that wrote the prose, would have published the second figure as clean. **Run it first, run it again, and run it against the files rather than against what you meant to write.**
