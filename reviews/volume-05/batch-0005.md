## Canon changes this batch makes, which a later writer must inherit

1. **The mark on the first carries the account through the fourth day and no further.** Every docket
   after it is the broker's to mark and his to answer for.
2. **The first of August docket is the one he gave a standing instruction on.** She offered once, on the
   third, with the twelfth of July as the date, and will not offer again in the life of the account. He
   refused in writing with the hour under it and two officers watching. **The twelfth of July is two days
   past the close of this volume and has not been written.**
3. **A member of a division may not report on a return her own name stands on.** The entry goes up with
   Ines's true report. She may be put at a desk where she is the subject. She says three days is the
   ordinary answer and a fortnight is all she can pay two rooms with.
4. **A head may give up a room.** There is an instrument, a week, and the broker's signature, and it would
   price the household at five. **He did not put it in front of Ines.** Marguerite carries the difference
   as a difference.
5. **Ines will not be read to in her own rooms.** He has agreed to read to her on the landing instead.
6. **Wren's finding: nine days on the second, seventeen on the tenth, unacted.** The true report: three
   days on the ninth, four on the tenth, filed.
7. **Petra has been on no roll since the fourth of June** — thirty-three days on the seventh, thirty-six
   on the tenth. The batch had been printing "nine days," which was wrong.
8. **The dead list of the fourth went up without the one line.** The plate stays lodged. Beatrix stays
   encumbered and the guild's tail stays on the fourth page, with no price left on the table to strike
   it with.
