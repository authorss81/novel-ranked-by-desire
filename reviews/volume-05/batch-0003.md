- **221 The Weight At The Top** — Beatrix Halm has been aimed since the seventh and the notebook has one
  line in it and four mornings of nothing, and the thing she has not written down is that **a plate lodged
  in a chained book is a weight at the top of Ferrier Street that about forty signal operators can find**,
  and she wants to be the only one who can find it, and she has twenty days before the panel.
- **222 Seven Where It Says Six** — **the mechanism of Ch 219 moves, and it moves because of an
  institution.** A correction with a form number on it says the audit division's index records seven where
  the head's sworn answer says six, because **an audit index is constituted from returned lines and not
  from totals, and an empty line cannot be struck out by anybody who has not got a name to strike it out
  with.** Ines reads it standing up and says one word, and is asked nothing.
- **223 What The Division Did Not Tell** — where seven came from, a counter-signature at the foot nobody
  read, a woman in a doorway asking for one impossible thing, and the lead's want: **to know what Wren
  Kallo saw before she decides what to do about it, and there is no address to write to.**
- **224 Six Persons Of Reading Age** — **the irreversible decision, and it is his.** The fourth page takes
  a figure and nothing else and he writes six, and the page is answerable against a plate lodged in a
  chained book, so his lie has a guild tail on it. **Ines Duarte asks him one question and he answers it
  and she goes up the stairs and says nothing else all night.**
- **225 A Bench Under The Wall** — **a Thursday, and it repaints.** Wren Kallo countersigned the
  correction, because every alteration of an indexed schedule is countersigned, and a correction raised by
  an office has no author, **and the only thing on it with a person in it is her name.** She asks to come
  back on the eighteenth. The man at the foot of the ladder says a name cannot come off that board with
  paint and there is a hole at the low end.
- **226 Nothing Moved In It** — **the quiet one.** Tessa Okonkwo examines her own fourth rib for eleven
  minutes and finds nothing and writes it in the day book, and is frightened, **because a clinician who
  cannot find the thing is a clinician who has been treating a symptom.** She decides to enter a clinic
  that has been safe for nine years because a hole in a wall is only safe while nobody looks down it.
- **227 The Space At The Foot** — **Petra Ilyin and the Vant.** What an unmarked docket does on its own
  date: **an account not produced at a valuation goes on the dead list on the fourth of that month and
  every security standing against it comes off on the day it goes on**, which puts the plate back on the
  fourth of July. The thirteenth docket was issued out of the June run and dated forward into July. Somebody
  outside the guild asked for that account's production file on the ninth and was refused in four minutes.
  **She will not say who.**
- **228 The Shape Of An Edit** — **the scene. 3,788 words.** Wren Kallo, the Reading Square after ten at
  night on the eighteenth, at the foot of the ranks wall, outdoors, in public, the first scene in this book
  staged outside a building at night. **Every edit in his file is a removal and there is no addition in it

  asks for four hours and a half with nothing altered in it, which is the only thing she has ever wanted
  off another person, and it is given, and afterwards she tells him he is going to have to lose somebody in
  the next nine days, and then she finds his name in the dark wall with her hand in two seconds and gives
  him the reason: **darkness is a thing a city switches off at eleven.**
- **229 The Roll Comes Down** — **the angry chapter, and she is not a broker and she is right.** An
  assessment sheet values a security early because an unpriced security cannot be carried a year running,
  and the description is six names in the broker's own hand. Sun Meiling says the part that is hers —
  **she spent eight days in June making a wall and a file agree with each other, and that is what let two
  offices see them** — and cuts the roll at the ruled lines in a kitchen in about eleven seconds.
- **230 The Twenty-Sixth** — a bare nail and a pale rectangle, **nine sentences from Ines Duarte about
  numbers and not one about herself**, Wren's finding read correctly as an indictment of this floor, and

## The three gate-record inaccuracies — **these were false zeros, not rule breaches**

**7. *Bold, underscore and italic markup → 0* was false.** The gate pattern (`\*\*[^*]+\*\*|_[^_]+_`)
**cannot match single-asterisk italics**, and there were two: `chapter-0224.md:21` *answerable* and
`chapter-0229.md:93` *which list are you on*. AGENTS.md permits italics sparingly, so the prose was lawful
and the **report** was wrong. **Both spans are now plain — *it used the word answerable four times in nine
lines* and *asking which list are you on* — so the batch prompt's own *no italics* line is satisfied and
the record and the pages agree.**

**8. *The word Thursday → 6* was wrong; it is 5** (221 ×1, 223 ×1, 225 ×2, 226 ×1). **The error is in the
method, not the arithmetic: a per-file `grep -c` counts LINES, and Ch 225 puts both of its uses on line 3.**
The stated breakdown *three times at 225* was the symptom. Corrected here and in `state/open-threads.md`.

**9. The repetition fix removed the wrong instance.** The drafting pass cut *she said it without any weight at
all* from Ch 227 as a fourth use and **left Ch 223:67 reading *she said it without weight***, so the batch
added a fifth rather than cutting one. **223 is now *She said it the way she said a thing she had checked
twice*, which is also what the woman actually does — she checks instruments.** Three earlier uses survive
in Ch 205, Ch 212 and Ch 218; they are closed batches and are left alone, and **the volume now carries four
across forty chapters rather than five across ten.**

fade are not the target*. **It is now written on the page: 2,925 → 3,788 words, inside the 2,600–3,900 scene

**What was added and what was not touched.** Added: **consent, which the chapter never had on the page.** He

shaking in the second hour and the whole of *What She Left* are untouched.**

**Why the scene is still this scene.** The engine is her professional discipline, which is the staging-grid

sedation, no exhaustion, no toxin, no restraint, no trance, and no seal.** The register is the same
throughout — there is no violence in this chapter and none was added.
