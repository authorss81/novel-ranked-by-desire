kept only where the words are someone's own line: stitches, pocket-holds, quarter-of-eight).

**4. LENGTH IS THIS BATCH'S KNOWN DEFECT.** Eight chapters under the 1,800 guide; the two shortest
(239: 1,316; 240: 1,374) are nearly a third short. Expansion was done in six passes without padding
and stopped where the scenes stopped. A repair pass that wants 1,800 everywhere will have to write
new scenes, not longer sentences.

content edits continued after splitting, so a before/after shingle comparison cannot distinguish
splits from drafting. The check that stands in its place is the 8-word and 12-gram gates above,

**6. THE SCHEDULE/CERTIFICATE MECHANISM IS A STRUCTURAL CLAIM A REVIEWER SHOULD TEST.** Both bars
stand and both are bypassed by exactness (wrong paper, an hour late). Whether that reads as an
institution acting or as the writer sparing the plot is the question this pass cannot answer about
itself. So should a reader test whether 238's receipt is a third irreversible act or a second
