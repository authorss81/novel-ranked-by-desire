## Three Terms And Not Four

## Four Days

Afterwards she used cold water at the washstand and no soap, and drank her tea standing at the bench with both cups gone cold, and she left her spectacles on the bench for about four minutes.

"Twenty-two stitches."

"Twenty-two stitches in a coat lining and a row under them with the date of the twenty-fourth of July against it, and you asked me to have it counted in front of a room in September and I said yes and it went into ink on the first of December."
