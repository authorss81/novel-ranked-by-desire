## What She Files, And What It Costs

“I am going to file a finding of nothing,” said Ines Duarte. “Against this office, on the matter of the papers it holds. It is the first one in my service.”

“A finding of something is admissible in a division in this city and a finding of nothing is also admissible, and the second one is the one that does not get filed. I am filing it because I have looked at about four hundred and thirty rows and about four hundred and nineteen lines and there is nothing in them that any officer of anything can act on, and the only true sentence available to me is that there is nothing there.”

## The Question She Had Not Asked Him Before

“Is there anything in Ostara that your office has ever been told?”

He put his coat on. The plate went back on the outside of it and the night shift went back to four nights and three off.
