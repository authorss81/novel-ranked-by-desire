# A List Of Six

He said it at about seven in the evening on the twenty-eighth, standing up, with his coat still on, before anybody had put anything on the table.

"Six."

The kitchen had seven women in it, counting the one at the back with the door open, and a form on the table, and a plate at 8,138 that has not moved a grain in five years and has a margin of three.

"Say the figure and say what it is out loud," said Ines Duarte. "Every figure you have said in this house for a month has been said with the subtraction attached and I have never once had to ask."

"Thirty-one of the sixty are gone," he said. "Twenty-nine are left. There are two days of intake left and the last working day of this month is the thirtieth and the counter shuts at four.

"And the figure at the top of the form is six. Six members of a standing household keeping an address at Ferrier Street, and the head's name in my hand, and my name in the type before I ever saw it, and one sentence at the foot in a size made to be skipped.

"I am going to write six, and I am going to sign it, and the line six is going to be a name, and on the twenty-seventh of June there is going to be a certificate with six members on page two of it, and that certificate does not care what I write on a piece of paper at a counter on the thirtieth."

"Then write it," said Runa Sato.

The room did not look at her.

"He said six," she said. "Six matches the schedule of the fourth of May. Six means the head's sworn list and the certificate of record say the same thing, and there is no figure on this form that disagrees with anything, and a schedule that does not disagree with itself is the only kind that has ever issued in my life. Say the rest of it."

"The rest of it is on page two and it is Sun Meiling's," he said.

Four women answered before the woman it belonged to.

"Six," said Ines Duarte. "And I am putting that on the record of this room in my own words: six agrees with a schedule of record filed on the fourth of May, which cannot now be amended by anybody in this house or out of it.

"Five is the only figure on that sheet this office would treat as a second filing on an address, and a second filing on this address is the thing that put a re-issue through the door on the first of this month in the first place."

"Six," said Nadia Ferrous, "because a household with a hole in it is a household with a question in it, and I have watched you all spend four months being certain that a question is the same thing as nothing. I am on page nine of a service file and it does not say why I am there, and this is not the document that fixes it, and I am going to say that out loud and then stop."

"Six," said Beatrix Halm. "I struck myself off a roll on the second of this month in nine seconds because I wanted to be unfindable and it cost me the only protection I had, and I would do it again.

"Do not choose five because you think it spares her. That is what I did in April, in nine seconds, to spare myself, and it was the same mistake."

"Six is not mine to want," said Marguerite Vale, from the door. "I hold the bottom of it and I put it in a public book on the twenty-fourth of this month, and a broker does not get a preference in a matter of the head's list. I have been priced out of my own succession for it. You may put that in as a reason if you need one, and it is a better reason than hers and worse than mine."

Sun Meiling was on the second stair with a lamp in her hand, and she had been on that stair since about half past six, and she said, "Then you have not finished the arithmetic and I would like you to go back and finish it in front of these people."

He went back and finished it.

"The certificate issues on the schedule of the fourth of May. That schedule has six members on page two. Five of them are on the roll on my wall and the sixth is entered as absent with reason, in four hundred words, filed at a counter on the sixth of this month, by Sun Meiling, in her own hand.

"So she is on the certificate. Whatever I write. That happened on the fourth of May, before I had signed anything, and it is going to happen on the twenty-seventh of June, and I did not do it and neither did she.

"And there are two figures in front of me and both of them cost her something.

"Six. I write six. My hand, my signature, my answer for every member of it. Her line is a member. It is a member of a standing household, certified, filed, dated, and in eleven years there is a woman in an office who reads it, and she reads it beside four hundred words in a queue saying that the person who wrote them is the one at that address whom papers are served on and not one of the people they are served on.

"And the head's sworn answer beats the queue. It is not because anybody has read them and disagreed with her. It is because there is a man's name at the top of one and not the other, and in nine years that is how documents decide which one a person was.

"Five. I write five. Then the head's sworn list and the schedule of record do not agree, and the small print says I state the figure and the reason, and the reason is hers, and the only true reason available is the four hundred words she wrote.

"So I write her four hundred words into a Bureau file in my own hand, and they are in it in my hand and not in hers, and there is a copy of that on a district sheet, and a woman in an office in eleven years reads a queue's statement written by one person and a head's statement about why that statement does not count, written by another, and it is my hand on the second one.

"That is the whole of it. Both ways the four hundred words come out of her hands and into mine."

The kitchen was very quiet.

"You may not do it for me," said Sun Meiling.

"I know."

"No, I do not think you know, and I am going to say it again because there are seven women in this room and about four of them think you are doing a kind thing.

"You are not doing a kind thing. You have priced it both ways and you have chosen the way in which you do not have to write down what she wrote in a queue, and you have dressed that up as sparing her, and it is sparing you.

"That document is mine. I filed it. I timed it. I stood in a line for it on the sixth of this month and I gave an answer at a counter because the answer was mine to give, and on the twentieth I put a card on this table and said it costs me something to hold and I am holding it anyway.

"And you have had it for ten days and you have been carrying it around the kitchen doing sums, and the sum you have been doing all month is not six against five. It is hers against mine."

"Sun—"

"No." She came down one step, which put her in the light from the stair window, and the light stopped about two inches above the seventh row and she was standing in it.

"You have been trying to keep Ines off that sheet since the nineteenth of May and you did it beautifully. She asked you on the twenty-fifth to stop doing it and you are going to go on doing it, and I watched you agree to her face, and I know exactly what agreeing to her face has been worth you in four years.

"And now you want to do the same thing to me, and you want to do it for the best possible reason, and the best possible reason is the only one that has never once worked in this house.

"So write five. Write it and put her four hundred words in the reason column and I will read them at the counter when I want to and a woman in an office in eleven years will have your handwriting on them, and it will be the first time in eleven years that anybody has touched that document who was not me, and I will survive it, because it is the worst version and I wrote it and I can hold it."

"No," said Lin Hao.

"That was not a question."

"I am not writing five," he said. "I have had a week to do it and I did it on the fourteenth of March in a kitchen when I said put my name in it, and I am not doing it again on the twenty-eighth, because a man who decides for six people once a month is going to decide for them again in September, and then again at Christmas, and then in four years when the guild sits.

"And I am not writing your four hundred words in a reason column," he said, "because they are the only thing you own and I have watched you build them since the sixth of May, and I would rather be the man who took your row off you than the man who took your words out of your hand and put them in mine."

The landing was quiet for a long time.

"Six," said Sun Meiling.

"Six."

"Then do it on the thirtieth at about half past two so that you are early, because a man who is early looks like a man who is sure of himself and I am not going to give you the satisfaction of being early by accident."

She went up the stairs with the lamp.

He stood in the kitchen and looked at six women and one at the back with the door open, and did the sum again out loud because that is what he had promised himself on the first of this month, and there was no subtraction left to do.

"That is the last figure in this house that I will ever be able to do out loud for you," he said, "because after tomorrow there is a number on a document, and the number is six, and it is going to disagree with every other number on that wall for as long as any of us are alive."
