# Batch 0001 — Volume One, chapters 1–10

**Batch goal:** Survive the plate, learn what a draw costs, and be read by the only person in the city who
can read records.

**Starting condition:** Lin Hao, 25, rank four, four years at the Fourth Branch, no reservoir awareness, one
photograph of two men on a tram step, nobody in Ostara who knows his name except his colleagues.

**Starting relationships:** Ines Duarte has never spoken to him. Nadia Ferrous has never seen him. Lin Wei
is three years gone and sealed above his reach. Tobias Renn considers him a colleague at best.

**Batch midpoint (Ch 5–6):** Tobias's filing puts the branch on the draw-down list early, and Lin Hao is
forced to spend during his own abstention. He chooses a stranger over his own safety and buys it with a
line in the ledger.

**Batch climax (Ch 7–8):** Ines opens his file, finds the two-year gap and the brother's name, comes to his
rooms, and the scene happens. Afterwards she says a name from his ledger that he did not know he had.

**Aftermath:** he has a mechanism, its price, an auditor in his life, a witness who will not shut up, and
a number of people on his own floor who felt something cold tonight.

## Distribution

Chapters 1–2 investigation · 3–4 demonstration and its cost · 5 social pressure · 6 action with a deadline ·

## The two hard gates for this batch

**Chapter 1, inside roughly the first eight hundred words, all three of:**

1. **A concrete objective with a deadline.** Submit his Thursday plate like everybody else, get it stamped,
   keep his posting. If the plate is wrong he loses the shift, and the shift is the only place in Ostara
   where nobody audits him.
2. **An anomaly he can see and cannot explain.** The machine returns a figure with no interval on it, and
   the interval is what a rank is made of.
3. **A consequence.** He takes it to the counter clerk, which puts him on the Thursday review list, which
   puts him in an auditor's queue and costs him a day of work and the only anonymity he has.

**Chapter 3, by the end of it, by event and not by explanation:** the reservoir moves merit out of a
stranger and the stranger feels it. The reader must have *seen* a cold minute before anyone names one.

## Chapter cards

### Chapter 1 — The Second Basin

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. Fourth Branch washroom, second floor, 5:40am, before the building fills.

**Immediate goal:** Get through the morning scan and the plate submission without anyone looking at his
reading. Keep the posting.

**Resistance:** The plate returns 3,412 grains and **no interval** — no band, no rate, nothing the band is
calculated from. The counter clerk, a tired man called Faber who has processed nine thousand plates and
does not want any of them, calls it *unsettled*, wraps it in cloth, and puts him on the Thursday review
list. Taking the plate back to the counter is the choice, and it is the choice that costs him.

**Information revealed:** A reading is a number *plus an interval*, and the interval is what makes a rank.
He has a number and no interval. "Unsettled" is a Bureau word, not a diagnosis.

**Action or decision:** He does not argue. He asks for the review date and takes the wrapped plate home,
because arguing means a second interview and he cannot afford to be a second interview.

**Emotional change:** Dread, and underneath it a flicker of something he refuses to look at — the specific
shame of a man who is used to being looked through suddenly being looked *at*.

**Power or resource change:** Zero to a wrapped plate and a Thursday date. Nothing else changes, and that is
what makes the next chapter frightening.

**Continuity to preserve:** Lin Hao is 25. Rank four. Lin Wei went three years ago at 28. He owns one plate,
one knife, one chair. He rides past the Board on Thursdays and does not look at it.

**Ending type:** Consequence.

**Pull:** What is on a Thursday list, and who reads Thursday lists?

### Chapter 2 — No Interval

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The second floor of the Fourth Branch, then the auditor's landing
outside the counter, mid-morning.

**Immediate goal:** Find out what *unsettled* means before Thursday, without asking the question in a way
that puts him on a list twice.

**Resistance:** He goes looking by walking where cleaners are allowed to walk and standing where people
must walk past. The answer is a word on a door and a plaque about sealed filings, and every route to more
information passes a person who is paid to notice him.

**Information revealed:** Rank six petitions sealed files. Rank nine opens any file in Ostara. The
Classification Wing has its own sub-level and its own staff, and the phrase a clerk uses when she cannot
place a person is *taken for a reading*.

**Action or decision:** He lets himself be seen asking, deliberately, because a man asking a question is
less alarming than a man standing in a corridor. He is seen.

**Emotional change:** The cold professional interest of a man working out the shape of a room he has been
in for four years and never read.

**Power or resource change:** He now knows the goal is six, not nine, and why six and not five. That is the
first time in the book that a number has told him what to do next.

**Continuity to preserve:** Ines Duarte is rank two, forty-one offices up, and has never looked at him.

**Ending type:** Discovery.

**Pull:** A rank-two auditor is waiting at the end of the corridor.

### Chapter 3 — Nineteen Grains

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Ferrier tram, then the concourse under the Reading, late
afternoon.

**Immediate goal:** Get home with the wrapped plate and not think about it.

**Resistance:** A man on the tram is having something happen to him — an attack, a fit, the thing where a
body stops agreeing with its owner. Lin Hao's hand is on his wrist before he has decided to put it there.

**Information revealed, by event:** The reservoir moves merit out of a stranger and gives it back to him as
relief. The man stops shaking. And **the man feels it** — one cold minute in the place that was worst, and
then he is all right, and he does not understand either half of what just happened to him.

[Ledger. Three lines.]

**Action or decision:** Lin Hao does not run. He sits down on the tram step and lets the man thank him,
because he does not yet know that a man who felt it cold cannot thank him for it properly, and that the
thanking is going to be the thing he cannot live with.

**Emotional change:** Physical nausea at himself, immediately, and the first hot flare of the specific
delight of being able to do something enormous for a stranger for nothing.

**Power or resource change:** First draw. Ledger open at three lines. He is faintly, sickly lighter, as
though something has been taken out of the room.

**Continuity to preserve:** Relief, not strength. The man is not made well. He is *finished*, the way a
person is finished at the end of something they have been surviving. Nobody in this chapter can be made to
want anything.

**Ending type:** Revelation, in short sentences, after four paragraphs of him trying to explain it to
himself.

**Pull:** The ledger says three lines, and he has known exactly one person all day.

### Chapter 4 — Cold Minutes

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. Ferrier Street, his rooms, then the branch floor, over two days.

**Immediate goal:** Stop the ledger from growing. Find out how to close it.

**Resistance:** He tries. He does not touch anybody for two days and it is like holding his breath. On the
third day the count has not gone down, it has gone up, from people he never touched.

[Ledger. Nine lines. One turned.]

**Information revealed:** The ledger shows people, not deeds, and it never will. **Turned** is what the
notation calls a person who has felt a cold minute more than once. He has one. He cannot tell who.

**Action or decision:** He stops spending deliberately. Not because it is dangerous — because he cannot
stand it, and he knows exactly how weak that makes him, and he does it anyway.

**Emotional change:** Shame at his own prudence. Relief at having made a rule. Both, and neither of them
clean.

**Power or resource change:** Nine lines, one turned. Abstention costs him sleep — four hours a night, and
a hand that is not steady at the plate cabinet by the end of the week.

**Continuity to preserve:** Abstinence is a choice he makes here, and it is a choice he is capable of
breaking, and the cost of breaking it is sleep and steadiness, not anything supernatural.

**Ending type:** Cost, and a rule that will be broken.

**Pull:** Somebody on the floor has been writing the cold minutes down.

### Chapter 5 — The Junior's Return

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Fourth Branch filing room, Wednesday.

**Immediate goal:** Work out who has been keeping notes, and get them to stop, before it becomes a second
thing on a list about him.

**Resistance:** Tobias Renn, rank two, four years in the filing room, resents a man who scored four without
trying. Tobias is not a monster; he is bored, and a man who scores four while filing nothing is a
conversation he can have. Tobias mentions, pleasantly, that the draw-down audit has been moved up.

**Information revealed:** The audit is real, it is in eleven days, and it will re-read all ninety-one staff
of the branch. Any member of staff without a settled account goes for classification.

**Action or decision:** Lin Hao does the arithmetic in his head — ninety-one people, and one of them is
him — and asks Tobias, carefully, as though it were idle, what happens to a floor when the audit lands.
Tobias tells him, at length, happily.

**Emotional change:** Anger, first time in the book, and it is not noble. It is anger at a man enjoying
himself, at twenty-four, about something that will send people to rooms with no windows.

**Power or resource change:** Deadline set at eleven days. And Lin Hao has now committed an act he cannot
take back — he has told a colleague he has no settled account without meaning to.

**Continuity to preserve:** Tobias is not the villain of this volume and does not know about the reservoir.
His file goes in Chapter 23 because of his own arithmetic, not because Lin Hao framed him.

**Ending type:** Threat, and a deadline.

**Pull:** Supply room three, where the branch's cleaning stock is issued, has been closed pending audit.

### Chapter 6 — Supply Room Three

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. Supply room three, then the third-floor landing, night shift.

**Immediate goal:** Keep the branch's cleaning stock moving for nine days, or ninety-one people lose the
one job that keeps their plates fed.

**Resistance:** The room has been closed and the key is with a supervisor who is enjoying the closure. There
is a boy, seventeen, on the rota to carry stock up from the yard at six. If the stock does not move, the
supervisor will re-post the boy to the sub-levels and the boy will lose his shift.

**Information revealed, by event:** Lin Hao can move merit *into* a person. He does not understand that yet.
He does it on purpose — badly, wrongly, with the specific clumsy intention of a man shoving a door — and a
stock clerk's Exchange account moves and the clerk looks at it in horror because his account moved and
nothing was drawn.

**Action or decision:** He spends during his own abstention. He chose the stranger over his own safety and
he will not say so to anyone.

[Ledger. Fourteen lines. Two turned.]

**Emotional change:** The first time he does something on purpose for a reason other than kindness, and
it feels exactly as bad as he knew it would, and he does it anyway.

**Power or resource change:** One line he does not understand, two turned, and the stock moves. His
abstention is broken and he has no rule left.

**Continuity to preserve:** Nobody can be compelled by this and nobody is. The clerk's account moves; the
clerk does not want it, does not understand it, and is frightened.

**Ending type:** Cost, and a rule broken.

**Pull:** Somebody upstairs has been writing down the cold minutes. There are four pages of them.

### Chapter 7 — Lantern Row

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. His rooms, then Lantern Row, Friday evening, in the rain.

**Immediate goal:** Stop the audit touching the floor, and do it without a conversation that puts a name on
the account.

**Resistance:** Ines Duarte has been waiting three weeks for him to come to her and has run out of patience
about it. She has his file. It is almost nothing: four years of employment, one plate, and a two-year gap
where he does not appear at all — and at the bottom of the gap, his brother's name, and the only line in
three years that has ever made her look up from the page.

**Information revealed:** She can hold a record and know when it has been touched. She has held his for
three weeks and she knows it is untouched, which means somebody went to the trouble to keep it untouched,
and that is a bigger fact than anything in it. She asks him one question: who helped you.

**Action or decision:** He does not answer. He says he can tell her what her own file says about her sister
instead, and she lets him, and then she does not leave.

**Emotional change:** Terror, then the specific vertigo of being wanted by someone who has the power to end
him. Both women in the room know exactly what is happening and neither pretends.

**Power or resource change:** He learns that his file has been kept deliberately clean by somebody, and that
somebody inside the Bureau has been protecting a man he has never met.

**Continuity to preserve:** Ines is 34, rank two, renting two rooms she cannot afford, and holds records at
a cost that is taking her own memory. She is a professional before she is anything else and stays that way
even now.

**Ending type:** Decision — and the setup for the batch climax.

**Pull:** She has not taken her coat off.

### Chapter 8 — Afterward, Ines

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. Her rooms, Lantern Row, that night.

**Immediate goal:** The scene, and the truth about what his brother did, told to a woman who can tell
whether it is true.

**Resistance:** None in the sense the book needs and all of it in the sense that matters. Both of them are
adults, awake, unmedicated, unrestrained, and neither of them is in a state the book may use to produce
this. What resists is the arithmetic — that she is the only person in Ostara who can read the records he
needs, and that whatever happens tonight is going to put her in a file of her own.

**Information revealed:** He tells her the number from the tram. She tells him what her sister's reading
said four years ago and why it was low, which is that her sister was kind in a doorway where nobody saw
her. **The reservoir is in play as relief and nothing else.** What she is owed is eleven days of a sleep
she has been carrying since that reading. He gives it back. She decides, fast, out loud, with no speech.

**Action or decision:** They are unhurried and neither of them is performing. Write the scene itself —
explicit, physical, arousing, generous, and long enough to be the thing the reader came for. Keep the
registers clean: no violence, no fear, no compulsion, no unearned poetry.

**Emotional change:** Afterwards, he has never in his life been the thing that happened to somebody. She
says it to him plainly and it is the first sentence anyone has said to him in three years that he believed
without checking.

**Power or resource change:** He has spent it on purpose, on someone who wanted it, and it worked. And in
the contact she feels the ledger, and she says a name from it aloud — a name he did not know he had.

**Continuity to preserve:** Every character in this scene is an adult and is written as one. No impaired
state is the mechanism. Nothing about the night is a transaction and nobody regrets it in the morning.

**Ending type:** Revelation. The name is the hook and it must be an ordinary name in an ordinary mouth.

**Pull:** Ines has a notebook, because she is an auditor, and the name in it is not a name Lin Hao expected.

### Chapter 9 — A Cold Minute, Recorded

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao and Ines. Lantern Row, then the Vestry lift, dawn.

**Immediate goal:** Find out what the name is, and how many pages of cold minutes exist.

**Resistance:** The name belongs to **Ola Bright**, 26, a lift attendant's flatmate, who keeps a notebook
because she is bored and who has written down every strange cold minute she has felt for four months.
Nadia Ferrous, 23, in the same lift, has been telling Ola about hers out loud for weeks, in a small warm
lift, on shift, to anyone who will listen. **The turned line has an address and the address has a
flatmate who talks.**

And then he looks at the ledger properly for the first time, at fourteen lines, and he counts the people
he has deliberately put a hand on: three. **Eleven lines belong to people he has never touched.** He has
been drawing on people by standing close to them in a crowded tram, and the reservoir does not require
consent or even contact, and this is the moment the mechanic stops being a gift and starts being a
hazard.

**Information revealed:** Two turned lines exist and both of them are on one floor of a Vestry lift. Ines
tells him, as an auditor and not as a woman, that a turned line is reportable and that a reportable turned
line opens a file on the person it was spent from. So Ola Bright is now a door, and Lin Hao is what is
behind it.

**Action or decision:** He goes to the Vestry to find Ola Bright before the audit does, and Ines comes with
him because she is not going to let him do it alone, and that is the second irreversible thing in the
batch and neither of them pretends it is small.

**Emotional change:** Anger, at Nadia, for being open-mouthed and glad. Guilt, at Ola, who has done nothing
except be interested. And the first real argument with Ines, which is about whether being left alone is
protection.

**Power or resource change:** Fourteen lines, two turned, both located. He must now befriend a woman who
wants to talk to everybody, and the only currency he has is attention.

**Continuity to preserve:** Nadia is an adult and is written as one, and the book never makes her
inexperience a condition of anything. Ola is not an antagonist. She is bored.

**Ending type:** Threat, with a human face.

**Pull:** A man off the fourth floor was taken for a reading on Thursday and he was not back that shift, and
Ola Bright has been waiting in the Vestry corridor since six to ask a porter about it.

### Chapter 10 — Not Back That Shift

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Vestry corridor at six in the morning, then the Classification
Wing's public counter, then the branch floor.

**Immediate goal:** Find out what "taken for a reading" means, in time to stop it happening to ninety-one
people including himself.

**Resistance:** The answer is in a corridor with no window and a form, and the woman behind the counter is
not cruel, she is bored, and she explains it exactly the way the Bureau explains everything: gently, and
because the system is not stupid, it is incomplete. Nobody takes a reading for punishment. They take one
because a person's account does not settle and the instruments need somewhere to put the question. And
then they keep the person until it does.

**Information revealed, in this order, each one landing on a person:** a man off the fourth floor, 51, a
stock clerk, taken Thursday, not back. His sister has been at the Vestry since six because a porter told
her nothing and she came anyway. **The Vestry is full of people waiting for somebody to come back, and
every one of them has been waiting since before Lin Wei.** The Church of the Quiet Number is two streets
over and has eleven thousand members and is the only institution in Ostara that says what he is.

**Action or decision:** He goes to the Vestry clinic instead of the Classification Wing, because he has
decided that the person who can tell him what a relieved body looks like from the outside is a doctor, not
a clerk. He asks Tessa Okonkwo, 31, the most dangerous question he has asked anybody, which is whether a
body can be found in the records that does not match the body in front of her.

**Emotional change:** The specific shame of a man realising that the thing he has been carrying for five
days is what is being done, deliberately, to somebody else, legally, and that it has been happening for
three years.

**Power or resource change:** Fourteen lines, two turned. He now knows the shape of the trap and that he is
inside it, and he knows there is an institution in Ostara that agrees with him and is illegal.

**Continuity to preserve:** **One of his own ledger lines is a Vestry volunteer.** He does not recognise the
name. The reader should notice this and Lin Hao should not.

**Ending type:** Revelation, and the batch closes on a standing threat rather than a resolution: the audit
is in eight days, the reading on Thursday will happen whether he does anything or not, and there is an
eleven-thousand-member church two streets over that has been waiting three years to be asked a question by
somebody in authority.

## Required from this batch

- Rank four throughout. No band change until Chapter 15, which is Batch 0002.
- One explicit scene, Chapter 8. One system message maximum per chapter, and only where the ledger
  genuinely turns.
- No rank-five language anywhere. No term from later volumes — no Channel, no quiet account, no Ninth
  Seal beyond the mention in Chapter 2, no held account until Chapter 50.
- No filler roster. Only Ines, Nadia, Ola, Tessa, Tobias and Lin Wei have lines.
- The prose mix is 60–70 per cent plain, 20–30 per cent documentary, and no more than three documentary
  paragraphs in a row before a person walks into the room.
- After any passage about how the Bureau works, one plain sentence about what it means for one named
  person. The stock clerk's sister is available at any point in chapters 5–10 for exactly this.
