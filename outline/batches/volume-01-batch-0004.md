# Batch 0004 — Volume One, chapters 31–40

**Batch goal:** Get to six without the Vant, with the Field watching.

**Starting condition:** Rank five, 8,141 grains — unchanged since Chapter 19 — **nineteen lines, five
turned**, public on a tile with a fifth figure that is not a rank, declared unsettled in a corridor in
front of ninety-one colleagues, and a weekly standing commitment in the Exchange box that he has
honoured once. The ninety-one are off the wall for a quarter. The audit is held one more fortnight and
that is the last one Rhee will put his name to.

**Starting relationships:** Tessa is in and has a date she has not asked about. Nadia is in and cannot be
contained. Runa has filed once and got four words back, and has just seen something on a wall she has
never seen before. Tobias is off the register and cannot be helped. Ines holds a signed paper with a
month on it. Marguerite has not appeared.

**Batch midpoint (Ch 32–33):** the Vant's offer and his refusal, and the immediate cost of refusing —
he has no leverage, so Runa's next report goes in and the Field gets a mandate.

**Batch climax (Ch 36):** Cordwain. A requisition of a block of flats where scores were adjusted for a
supervisor's convenience. Lin Hao and Runa Sato in a requisitioned room, both injured, and the decision
about whose side he is on.

**Aftermath:** provisionally six at Chapter 37, five turned lines at Chapter 38, and Chapter 40 with the
Winter Review scheduled for the sixth week and **three grains of margin**.

**Distribution:** 31–32 pursuit and the offer · 33 the refusal and its cost · 34 the arc walked into
trouble · 35 the report · 36 scene and consequence · 37 the band · 38 five cold minutes · 39 the wall ·
40 the queue.

## Calendar, fixed by Batch 0003, in dates only

**No weekday arithmetic.** Weekday names are not canonical in this book: speak one only for **Thursday**
(repaint, publication, the readings, the Exchange box) and **Monday the 19th**. Count in dates and
intervals: eleven days, nine days, three days, forty minutes, ten minutes, two hours.

The batch runs **from the 11th to the 26th**. Ch 31 the 11th · Ch 32 the 12th · Ch 33 the 13th ·
Ch 34 the 15th to the 17th · Ch 35 the 18th · Ch 36 the 19th · Ch 37 the 19th into the 20th ·
Ch 38 the 20th · Ch 39 the 23rd · Ch 40 the 26th.

Rhee's second fortnight, granted in the Chapter 28 corridor, **runs out on the 17th**. Tobias said in
Chapter 19 that Lin Hao had **about six weeks** before it mattered, and the Winter Review sits in the
sixth week. Those two clocks now run against each other in public.

## Chapter cards

### Ch 31 — Provisionally Six
The Exchange box at nine on a Thursday, the first time he has kept the promise twice, and the second
clerk has worked out how to make it official — a provisional band that reads on the Board without a
band existing behind it. Goal: find out what a standing public reading buys him that a declaration did
not. Resistance: it buys him a question he must answer every week in front of strangers, and it puts a
date on the quarterly re-read that he does not control. Ends with a name he has not spoken in twenty-one
days arriving in a corridor: **Marguerite Vale**, through the Vant.

### Ch 32 — The Vant's Price
The Vant offers to put the reservoir into a legal quiet account — untouchable, sealed, laundered around
the Board entirely — for a price and a favour. Marguerite Vale is direct, enjoying herself, and does not
pretend about anything, and the offer is genuinely good, and it is the first offer in the book that
costs him nothing in other people's bodies. Resistance: the moment it is accepted he owns a thing that
can be seized, and Solveig Rask's real books are in the room in some form. Ends on the terms arriving in
a way that can be answered.

### Ch 33 — Solveig's Arithmetic
He refuses, and says why out loud, and the refusal is expensive: **he has no leverage, so Runa's report
goes in unrebutted and the Field gets a mandate.** Solveig Rask's arithmetic about what the account is
actually worth gets at least one number right, which is the frightening part. Goal: survive the week
without a patron. Ends with a man in the Fourth Branch's own corridor putting the word *supply* into a
document that is not a file.

### Ch 34 — Cordwain
He walks the arc deliberately because he has nineteen people and no way to spend them, and the arc runs
into a district where the scores were adjusted for a supervisor's convenience. The Field requisitions a
block of flats. Physical risk and a deadline in the same chapter. Ends inside a building with a door that
has been opened from the outside.

### Ch 35 — A Clean Report
Runa's report goes in and it is accurate and it is not about him, which is the first time a Field
document has ever been kind to him by accident. A Wing clerk begins the quarterly re-read early, which is
the threat arriving on a date nobody announced. Ends with a requisition order with two names on it.

### Ch 36 — Requisitioned Rooms
**Scene.** Runa Sato, a requisitioned flat in Cordwain, both of them injured, neither impaired by
anything the book should not use, and the Field's most dangerous operative lying in her own files by the
end of it. Seven chapters after Ch 29 — the accepted short gap. Ends on his decision about whose side
he is on, taken alone.

### Ch 37 — The Provisional Band
Provisionally six. Not by a deed and not by an aggregate — by a thing the machine does when a man who
has already declared an unsettled reading is attached to a requisition he can be shown to have caused.
Ends on the margin.

### Ch 38 — Five Cold Minutes
Five turned lines, located, and the cost of the climb written on five people who said yes to something
else first. Physical cost, real bodies, named. Ends with a cold minute that reaches somebody who must
not be reached.

### Ch 39 — What the Board Does Not Say
The wall, the fifth figure, the register, and what a man can learn from a document that describes him
without naming him. Two women in the same room wanting different things from him, and the negotiation
belongs to him.

### Ch 40 — The Winter Queue
The Winter Review is scheduled for the sixth week and he has **three grains of margin**. The queue is
published. Goal: know exactly how far away six is. Ends on a number and a date and the person the
number belongs to.

## Required from this batch

- **The figure is 8,141 at Chapter 31.** It may move in Ch 37 and nowhere else, it may not move by
  aggregation, and **there is no second aggregate.** If he reaches six by any route the reader cannot
  trace to a person, the Chapter 19 reversal stops meaning anything.
- **The ledger rises to forty-one lines and holds at five turned.** Lines only ever rise. Nineteen at the
  end of Batch 0003, forty-one here, ninety-one in Batch 0005. The count did not move for a stated
  reason last batch; if it moves now, say on the page why.
- **Never speak the word *reservoir*.** Lin Hao's words are *the figure*, *the account*, *it*. The
  Bureau's word is *unsettled*. **The Channel does not appear in Volume 1.** No *quiet account* as a
  term of art, no Ninth Seal, no Concil.
- **Never write *there is no form for this*, never write *there is no column for it*, and do not
  paraphrase either one.** All three escalations are spent and none are available before Volume 7.
  Sweep for the construction, not for the wording.
- **Length: every chapter between 1,800 and 2,700 words.** Batch 0003 landed at 2,021–2,698, mean
  2,378. Nine chapters of Batch 0003 went over the ceiling on first pass. Check before you finish.
- **Paragraphs two to four sentences, nothing over 120 words.** Batch 0003 had nine paragraphs over on
  first pass, one of them 165.
- **One System message per chapter at most, and never the same panel twice.** Batch 0003 used Ch 25 and
  Ch 30. Expect this batch's panels at Ch 37 and Ch 40, where the band and the margin move.
- **No age is stated in narration, for named characters or walk-ons.** Ages live in
  `bible/characters.md`. **Two residual instances survive in Ch 16 and Ch 18** stating Emrys Vaz's age;
  they are flagged in `state/open-threads.md` and were deliberately not touched.
- **Bold is not used anywhere in this book.** Chapter prose carries document text plain. Italic is
  scarce — Batch 0003 ended at twelve across ten chapters.
- **No feeder thanks him.** *Nobody thanked him* is Chapter 50's and is not available here.
- **No weekday name except Thursday and Monday the 19th**, where the world turns on it. Otherwise dates
  and intervals. Batch 0003 removed twenty-two illegal weekday names in one pass.
- **One explicit scene, Chapter 36.** Adults, awake, unrestrained, unmedicated. An impaired state is
  never the mechanism. Her deafness is a cost Beatrix jokes about and is not a mechanism; Runa has no
  such mechanic, so nothing about carrying weight may be used to produce a scene.
- **Physical risk or a deadline in the batch.** Ch 34's requisition and Ch 37's band satisfy it.
- **After any passage of institutional machinery, one plain sentence about what it means for one named
  person.** The Cordwain adjustment lands on a named tenant and not on a district.
- **No filler roster.** Lin Hao, Ines, Tessa, Beatrix, Nadia, Runa, Marguerite, Tobias, Rhee, Vole and
  Ola have lines. Sun Meiling, Wren Kallo, Solveig Rask, Alma Quayle, Yusuf Adeyemi, Anouk Ferreira and
  Dagmar Oyelowo do not appear. **Marguerite's scene is Chapter 46 and is not this batch.**
