# Batch 0003 — Volume One, chapters 21–30

**Batch goal:** Keep ninety-one people out of classification, and pay for it.

**Starting condition:** Rank five, 8,141 grains, nineteen lines three turned all mapped to the Ferrier arc, Field order live in Runa Sato's hands, audit deferred nine days, Branch bought a fortnight.

**Starting relationships:** Ines cannot attest him and is sleeping badly. Beatrix supplies intelligence and expects spending on people. Nadia is a public-relations problem on her fourth minute. Ola keeps the book. Tobias has filed honestly. Rhee's employment offer is open. Runa watches.

**Batch midpoint (Ch 25):** he tests routing deliberately in the Vestry clinic, puts it into the wrong person, and the clinic's intake records stop matching the bodies. Then he realises the ledger has been running for months rather than weeks — he has been taking it for granted. Ch 4 was a man noticing a number move; Ch 25 is a man choosing what to do about it and getting it wrong on a patient. Do not restate Ch 4's dread.

**Batch climax (Ch 26–28):** Tessa catches the discrepancy, they go to the cold store (scene, Ch 26), and on the Thursday line he tells ninety-one colleagues the unsettled reading is his and will be read publicly every week (Ch 28). Permanent loss of anonymity buys the Branch a fortnight.

**Required character changes:** He stops hoarding. Tessa goes clinician to ally. Nadia goes rumour to household. Tobias is destroyed by his own filing.

**Required power changes:** First routing of merit into another account, done badly, not understood. Ledger at seventeen lines? No — carry nineteen forward; batch ends at seventeen? ## Chapter cards

### Ch 21 — Ninety-One Names
Runa interviews him under the Field order; he learns what observation permits and what it costs her to write. Goal: stay unfiled one more week. Ends with the deferred audit list posted — ninety-one names, his among them in plain print.

### Ch 22 — The Draw-down
The draw-down lands on the wrong people. He walks the floor with the list, finds two feeders on it, and understands spending on a listed person marks them. Ends with Tobias asking to be told what he is.

### Ch 23 — Two Kinds of Clean
Tobias's file goes in on his own arithmetic — he attested something he should not have to protect the Branch. Lin Hao could have stopped it and did not. Cost of refusing leverage. Ends with an empty filing chair.

### Ch 24 — What the Clinic Files
Tessa shows him intake charts that almost match: too-well bodies, her uncounted box. He asks to try routing relief the other way — into an account — on a consenting patient. She refuses the ward, allows the store. Ends with permission and a time.

### Ch 25 — The Wrong Direction
In the clinic he routes deliberately, badly, into the wrong person. The patient's Exchange figure moves; the body does not relieve. Records stop matching. He realises the ledger predates his awareness by months. Ends on the wrong chart.

### Ch 26 — Cold Store
Tessa scene, after hours, cold store. Explicit, adults, unhurried. She stabilises the wrongly-routed patient by paying cold minutes out of her own inventory and tells him the cost is not only his. What changes: clinician to ally, medicine not threat. Nine chapters after Ch 17 — ceiling.

### Ch 27 — The Thursday Line
Thursday repaint queue. He stands with ninety-one staff waiting on readings and watches three tiles hesitate. Felt emotion: dread with a use. Ends with him deciding to stand up.

### Ch 28 — Public Record
He stands up in front of Branch staff and says the unsettled reading is his, on their floor, read publicly weekly — because an unsettled floor gets audited and he will not have ninety-one sent for classification over his account. Irreversible. Buys a fortnight, loses anonymity permanently. Ends on silence, not applause.

### Ch 29 — Between Floors
Nadia scene in a stopped lift, Vestry to clinic. Explicit, adults, neither stuck by accident. What changes: household gets a witness who cannot keep her mouth shut — conscience and comic relief. Three chapters after Ch 26 (known short gap, locked, do not move).

### Ch 30 — A Fifth Figure
Board repaints; his tile carries a fifth figure that is not a rank. Classification Wing notice: unsettled readings tracked before his arrival. Ends on the new figure and the question who fed it first.
