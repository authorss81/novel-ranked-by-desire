# Volume 7, Batch 0003 — Chapter cards, 321–330

**Movement 3 of `outline/volume-07.md` §6: The Thing He Had Never Done.** The third to the sixteenth of
November. Ten chapters, fourteen days. **The scene is 329 and it is Marguerite Vale's.**

**THIS BATCH CROSSES A BAND AND LEARNS THE MECHANISM, IN THAT ORDER.** He has to be heard by somebody
entitled to hear him, the only instrument that hears him is a band on a plate, and a band is made of deeds
other people attest in public. **He has refused that since Chapter 48 because every advantage feels like
theft from somebody smaller. He does it here, badly, in front of witnesses, and it costs somebody.**

## The batch in the form the phase system asks for

**Batch number:** 0003. **Chapter range:** 321–330. **Batch goal.** Become a person a requisition can be
heard against, and find out what a movement of unrecorded merit actually requires.

**Starting condition.** Eight names on a public list. The classification is written against the head. The
plate is 8,138 against a floor of 8,144 and the plate has not moved a grain in two hundred and forty
chapters. **Band six on a piece of paper; band five on the plate.**

**Starting relationships.** The kitchen has been told everything. Marguerite Vale has been out of the counting
room since the seventeenth of September and has not said why. Sun Meiling is under a permanent bar and is not
going to be sworn.

**Batch midpoint (325).** **The mechanism beat.** A movement of unrecorded merit out of an address and into
an account requires a **Seal**, and without one it is theft, **and the only seal in this city that would
answer that account is his brother's.** He learns that the thing he has been defending was handed to him.
**He does not open Lin Wei's file. The file opens at Chapter 372.**

**Batch climax (330).** **He puts other people's names on deeds he arranged.** The band moves. He reaches
seven. **The option closed: he can never be unscored again, and the man nobody could read is now a figure
on a wall every Thursday.**

**Required character changes.** Marguerite Vale stops being a woman with a position inside a guild and
becomes a woman who has put her own book on a table. **Sun Meiling's sentence — neither grateful nor
ungrateful — is not restated and not answered by anybody.**

**Required power changes.** **The band moves, publicly, for the first time in the book, and it moves because
other people attested things.** No grain out of the reservoir. No spend. No draw. **A panel may be due for
the first time in seven volumes if a count turns, and if one is written it must be the plain statement and
nothing else.**

**Required world reveals.** **What rank is for, stated by a woman who has one** · **what a Seal is and what
one use of it costs** · **that the reservoir cannot be moved at all without one, and that a man with no
standing cannot obtain one.**

**Open questions after the batch.** *Whose seal is it and who put it where?* · *If the account was
handed to him, what has been feeding it since?* · *What is a man worth in this city who has been scored in
public for the first time at twenty-six?*

## Cards

**321 The Thing He Had Never Done** — the third. The kitchen argument about how a man earns a band, and the
discovery that **nobody in that room has ever asked him for anything in public**, because for five years he
has refused to compete openly and they have all learned not to.

**322 A Deed With Witnesses In The Room** — the fifth. He does the ordinary thing. It is witnessed, written
down, and **it does not score**, because the deed was his and deeds that are his own do not score, and the
reason is a rule nobody in the room knows but him.

**323 The Plate That Had Not Moved** — the seventh. **He learns that the reservoir cannot raise his rank and
that every band he has ever held came from relief converted into somebody else's attested act.** It has been
the engine of the plot for five volumes and it is said out loud in a kitchen once and never explained again.

**324 Four Years Of Being Looked Through** — the ninth. Tessa Okonkwo's assessment of what he would have to
become, delivered as medicine, and **the sentence that the cost of becoming legible is not paid by him and
is paid by whoever attests for him.**

**325 A Transfer That Needs A Seal** — the eleventh. **THE MECHANISM.** He tries the obvious thing — move the
account away from the address — and finds the instrument he needs does not exist for him, and finds out what
does exist, and **how many of them there are.**

**326 The Only Seal That Would Answer** — the twelfth. **His brother's seal is the one that matches, and the
reason a seal matches is a property of the account and not of the man holding it**, and he does not
understand it and Sun Meiling does.

**327 The Seal That Matches** — the fourteenth. The three words of the volume's mechanism beat. **He does not
get an answer. He gets the shape of an answer and a date.**

**328 Seven On A Wall In A New Shade** — the sixteenth. **The band moves, and it moves because three women
put their names to things he did**, and the card comes off the board and the number goes up in a shade the
square has not had, and **Beatrix Halm reads it at a quarter to six in the dark and writes it in a notebook
she has carried for nine years.**

**329 The Vant's Whole Exposure. THE SCENE. Marguerite Vale.** — the night of the sixteenth, in her rooms,
which are the best rooms he will ever stand in. She has decided to put the Vant's whole exposure of him on
a table in front of nine people. **She asks him for the thing she has wanted for five volumes, which is a
position that does not depend on the Vant, and he cannot give it to her, and she does it anyway.** Scene
rules as in Batch 0002.

**330 The Table She Cleared Herself** — the seventeenth, morning. **She has cleared it herself, before he
came, and she has left nothing on it, and the absence is the statement.** The Vant is no longer protecting
her and nobody else has started.
