What it costs, said out loud at that door: another year of hands on that stair and another year of strangers reading three lines about him out loud, with nothing behind them and nobody able to check any of it.** The forty-seventh comes down at four and the forty-eighth goes up on the same two nails carrying no new line in the office's hand, and nothing goes at the foot of the standing sheet.
- **Emotional change:** the first time in nine years he has put something into the world that he cannot take back, and the discovery that what it feels like is not triumph and is not fear either, and that the nearest word for it is the word he has been refusing to use about a door for a year.
- **Power or resource change:** none. Rank one throughout. No band, permission, standing or shift change and the branch night shift is not moved.
- **Continuity fact to preserve:** the renewed card carries the street, the door and the hours and not one name, in his own hand, with three nails, and there is no transcript of it anywhere and no list of who has come through it; the second column keeps four lines; the bundle stays at twenty-six things; the drawer stays shut; the inside of that coat on the left stays empty; the head is printed once and is not moved, raised, confirmed or corrected; the two written refusals stand and no third is produced; the sooner figure is not rubbed out and the third figure is still owed.
- **Ending type:** irreversible decision in ink with its cost said aloud at the door, and the volume's last line about a thing that stays and a promise that cannot be entered. **Next-chapter pull:** none owed. The volume closes.**
