## THE TEN CARDS

### 433 — 29 May — **Four Minutes And A Box**

She certifies again and knows this time. **The first of May's morning was the thirtieth of March and this is the next one.** Four minutes at a
desk. **`PC-87` IS UNDER HER:** there is no certificate for the quarter that begins on the first of July, a form cannot be drafted without one,
and on the thirtieth of June a keeper is going to fill the fourth block of a carry-forward out of her own memory, in her own hand, with a name
on it that no certificate stands behind. **She does not know that yet and this is the chapter in which she certifies against it.**

*May not:* have her learn the July position from him · make her say the word for what it is · turn it into a scene.

### 434 — 1 June — **The One Who Was Never Told**

**THE VOLUME'S MIDPOINT REVERSAL.** Sun Meiling. She certifies the composition of a house she sleeps in, four times a year, in her own division,
in her own hand, under her own name, at the head of it. The requisition is administered weekly against that composition. A line with the address
on it went into a Church book on the first of June. A keeper is about to enter her name into a fourth block with no certificate behind it. **She
cannot be sworn and cannot be protected by the roll, and he has not told her any of it.**

**AND IT IS LOADED BY 423, 427, 431 AND 432 RATHER THAN BY THE PLAN.** **This card is where he may at last tell her the rest.**

*May not:* redo what 411 already did, have her find out twice, have her tell him first, or have her be told by anybody but him · let her be
made whole by it · give her a gift, a mark, or an ending.

### 435 — 3 June — **The Chair Who Will Not Meet**

**The Council sits on the thirtieth and this is not that sitting.** A woman who chairs refuses to meet him, and 425 has already put a recused
member in the room at a chair that is not one of the nine, hearing everything and taking no part, with Alma Quayle telling him to count her.

*May not:* lift Alma Quayle's recusal · let this sitting change the number of seats · make it the thirtieth of June.

### 436 — 5 June — **A Correction She Has Not Filed**

A correction that exists, is correct, and has not been filed, and the cost of filing it is somebody else's. **A clerk may only enter and cannot
correct, so the cheapest available act is an entry, and an entry made by a woman is paid for out of her own future and can never be told she
paid.**

*May not:* let her file it · let it be the requisition's entry · let anybody thank her for it.

### 437 — 7 June — **The Third Sub-Level**

**A corridor and not a door.** A man who does not lock it walks him down to his own door, and **he is not put inside one.** **`PC-62` lands here
as well as at 431:** the administration is the way a held account is administered, on a fixed day, with a medical return to the same clinic, and
there are now two returns in one quarter.

*May not:* put him in the room · name the brother · move the administration off the fixed day · print what is in the file.

### 438 — 9 June — **Nine Years At The North End** — **THE SCENE. BEATRIX HALM.**

**She is the only intelligence source nobody can intercept, and the only person in the book who has been nine years inside the one instrument
that cannot be forged: a woman writing down a time.** Six empty lines in a notebook and one filled with *thirteenth, counter, nine forty-one*, a
time written on the back of a return in pencil on the twenty-second of May by a woman who cannot be asked, **and at 438 the seventh line has
something under it.**

**She is deaf in one ear, the city's noise lives in the other, and she cannot hear herself think on a busy evening. A deaf woman is not an
impaired one and her deafness is her gift's price and must never be its mechanism. She says so in her own mouth before anything else happens in
the room.**

**The duplication risk in this book is *terms, a body described in words three times, a counted minute, then a kettle* — confirmed in 340, 350,
358, 369, 380 and 398. 406 broke it on three axes, 417 on the terms axis and the minute axis, 428 on the body axis, and 428 said all of it on
the page. The three are spent. 438 breaks it again on a new one and says which.**

*May not:* use her deafness as the mechanism · have her fast or be silent for it · write the figure she wrote on the back of her own hand
at 428, which is on the page as a decision and not as a number · make the scene a summary.

### 439 — 10 June — **The Count She Keeps Under**

The count a person keeps and does not say, and what it costs to go on keeping it, and the moment the keeping is the thing being asked about.

*May not:* resolve it · make it a ledger figure · let her be the one to break.

### 440 — 12 June — **The House That Is Nine People**

Eight women at one table and the arithmetic of what the seventh of them has signed and what the gap in the middle of the line protects.

*May not:* move the count · put the ninth signature on anything · have Sun Meiling be the ninth.

### 441 — 13 June — **The Standing To Answer For Himself**

The requisition is called and it is his to answer, and answering it means standing in that room as a member. **`PC-64` is still plan and may not
be assumed; what is on the page is that he cannot ask that room to do anything and has sworn never to make it need something.**

*May not:* have him use the instrument · let the room ask for anything · have the requisition carried, received or decided.

### 442 — 15 June — **The Minutes He Reads Himself**

He reads the minutes of the proceedings that concern him, in his own hand, alone, and what a record looks like from the inside when there is
nobody in it to be angry at.

*May not:* print the word under the fifth line or its second copy · let the record be corrected · let this be a summing-up.
