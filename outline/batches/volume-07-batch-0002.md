**THIS BATCH IS WHERE THE VOLUME TURNS, AND IT TURNS AGAINST HIM.** The offer is real, it is merciful, it is

## The batch in the form the phase system asks for

**Batch number:** 0002. **Chapter range:** 311–320. **Batch goal.** Find out what a description of an address
is obliged to contain, and find out whether a description can be written against a man instead of a house.

**Starting condition.** The account is referred. The address, the figure and the reason are all in the city.
Alma Quayle has asked one question in the kitchen and he could not answer it. **The thirtieth is the last
day of the month and the description has to be made by then.**

**Starting relationships.** The eight have been told what a description is worth by a stranger. **Wren Kallo
is now a named woman in a Bureau book and knows it.** Ines Duarte's standing term holds. Runa Sato's
division holds the priced line.

**Batch midpoint (315).** **He asks that the classification be written against the head and not against the
address.** Eight women are named in a file, in the order they keep the address, one of them in her own
words, one of them with a clerk's number that is hers for ever.

**Batch climax (320).** **The list of persons at an address goes out, and it is a public instrument, and
every woman on it is individually reachable for the first time in the book.** The Field counter reads it at
nine in the morning. **The option closed: the household's invisibility cannot be got back and he may not
unname anybody.**

**Required character changes.** Alma Quayle stops being an office. Runa Sato decides to be an instrument
rather than a liar. **Isolde Marchetti is not in this batch and what she did at 308 is not undone, softened
or regretted by anybody.**

**Required power changes.** None. **No draw, no spend, no turn, no grain, no rank.** A panel is not due.

**Required world reveals.** **What a description of a house must list** · **that the head of premises may
carry the description instead, and what that costs him** · **that the list is a public instrument and can be
read by any division without a reason.**

**Open questions after the batch.** *Can a man be described in front of his own household and still be the
subject?* · *Who reads the list first and what do they do with it?* · *Isolde's reason is in nine hundred
mouths and eight names are in a file — which of those is the one that travels?*

## Cards

**311 The Classification Of A House** — the corridor, the twenty-second. He asks to be told the contents of a
description. He is told that a description of premises is a list of persons, and that the list is not a
judgement and is available to any division that asks.

**312 The Head Of Classification** — the second sub-level, the twenty-third. **Alma Quayle's office, and the
first time a woman in this book tells him what her job is for.** She has refused thirty years of promotions
that took her off classification.

**313 A Woman With Thirty Years Of It** — Ferrier Street, the twenty-fourth. The kitchen again, and the
difference is that this time she comes with a form and asks them to fill it in, one line each, **as a
courtesy**, and the household sees the courtesy working.

**314 The Sentence She Wants To Write** — the twenty-sixth. **He finds out what the sentence is before she
writes it, and it is shorter than he expected**, and it does not contain the word *reservoir*, and it
contains *unrecorded account carried at premises 34-6-118*, and **it is almost entirely true**, which is the
problem.

**315 The Version With Eight On It** — the twenty-seventh. **THE MIDPOINT.** He asks for the description to be
written against the head and not against the address, in a room with a clerk in it, and it is granted in
minutes because it is cheaper to administer and easier to appeal. **Eight women are named.**

**316 The One Who Is Not Counted** — the twenty-eighth. **Wren Kallo's line.** She is on the list, with her
name and her posting, and she looks at it for a while and says the one true thing about it, which is that
she put herself there and would do it again, and that the price of that is her division.

**317 The Price Of A Term** — the twenty-ninth, evening. He tells the kitchen what he has done and why. **He
says the reason is that a description of a man can be examined and a description of a house cannot, and he
believes it, and it is true, and it is also the reason he has used for seven volumes.**

**318 The Report That Was True And Useless. THE SCENE. Runa Sato.** — the thirtieth, after a requisitioned
flat on Cordwain. Her division has the priced line and an order to produce a return. **He tells her to file
the truth and she files it and it is useless and it is the only thing in this volume that was hers.**
**Scene rules: written out, not summarised; no impaired state as a mechanism; nothing compelled.**

**319 The Order She Took On Purpose** — the thirtieth, morning. She has taken a standing order to observe the
premises and has not refused it, and he cannot protect her from an order she has accepted on purpose.

**320 The List Of Persons At An Address** — the thirtieth, after four o'clock. The list goes out in the
ordinary way. **The Field counter reads it at nine the next morning.** End on the list being ordinary and
public and eight names on it.
