## The batch in the form the phase system asks for

**Batch number:** 0001. **Chapter range:** 301–310, ten chapters, eighteen days.

**Batch goal.** **Stop a household that changes its own figure from breaking a priced line in a real book —
and find out that it cannot be stopped, and what the city does with the difference.**

**Starting condition.** Every figure and every standing instrument is `outline/volume-07.md` §2 and
`state/current.md` §2. **The reservoir is priced at seven; the composition sworn on the first of October is
seven; the seventh returned line of the third-quarter district book is blank and cannot be filled; the plate
is 8,138 and the floor is 8,144; the ledger is 118 lines with nine turned and the ninth is himself; nothing
has been spent.**

**Starting relationships.** Eight women in the building and seven addresses. Ines Duarte out of the flat and
still in it on paper. Sun Meiling in ink under a bar. Petra Ilyin on no roll. Wren Kallo on nothing at any
time until about eleven on the second of October. **Marguerite Vale holds a dated undertaking that he will
not ask her again, and the keys of the counting room are in the hands of a woman he has never met.**

**Batch midpoint (Chapter 305).** **He goes to do the third of the three things and finds that it is the
only one that requires him to describe himself.** The month goes out on the thirtieth, so the choice has a
date on it.

**Batch climax (Chapter 310).** **He refuses to swear eight.** The account is referred for classification
with the address, the figure and the reason on it. **What it costs:** the cleanest answer he has ever given
is retired, Wren Kallo's name goes on a page she can never take off, and eight women are inside a
description. **The option closed:** he can never swear that premises at seven again.

**Required character changes.** Wren Kallo goes from the household's conscience to the household's most
expensive member, and she knows both facts and is not allowed to say either. Isolde Marchetti goes from a
woman who was used against him to a woman who has used something against the city for him. Alma Quayle goes
from a name in a file to a person at his kitchen table who wants one sentence from him.

**Required power changes.** **None, and that is a requirement.** No draw, no spend, no turn of the count,
no grain out of the reservoir, no rank earned or lost. **A panel is not due in this batch and one written
because a batch was feeling clever is a defect.**

**Required world reveals.** Three, and all three are ordinary procedure rather than lore: **what a security
carried at a composition that has moved does** · **the three places an account can go when it will not
carry, and the fact that the third one is what happens to it by default** · **that a district premises
return for the quarter that opened on the first of October is a fresh leaf, and adding a name to it is an
ordinary act and writes the composition on the face of the leaf.**

**Open questions after the batch.** *Who writes the sentence when a man will not describe himself?* ·
*What is a description of a house obliged to include?* ·*Can a sworn answer be retired without a
substitute?* ·*Which woman in that kitchen is going to be asked about a classification in her own rooms?*

**Chapter 301 — The Address She Took In The Ordinary Way**

*POV:* Lin Hao. *Location and time:* the district premises counter, about eleven in the morning, and the
kitchen at Ferrier Street the same evening. *Immediate goal:* keep Wren Kallo off every instrument in this
city for one more month. *Resistance:* a counter may not refuse a person of reading age who keeps an address
at premises, she is asking in the ordinary way, and she is the one person in that building who has never
been on a page and has just decided to stop being careful. *Major turn:* **she takes it.** The composition of
34-6-118 is eight from about eleven o'clock on the second of October, and the district writes the eighth name
and the figure on the face of a fresh leaf of the quarter-book that opened on the first. *Emotional or
practical consequence:* he could have stopped her by one sentence in a corridor and he does not say it, and
she does not thank him, and she asks him a plain question about the counter's opening hours on the way out.
*Required continuity facts:* the priced line is dated the sixteenth of September and initialled and not
signed · the production sworn on the first of October was seven · the third-quarter book closed on the
thirtieth and cannot be reopened · her clerk's number in the pencil margin is already hers for ever · **she
is on no roll, no list and no instrument at any time until this morning.** *Ending hook:* **the leaf says
eight and the keeper's book says seven and there is now a second figure for one door, and the clerk is an
ordinary clerk who will read both and think the city is untidy.**

**Chapter 303 — The Book That Would Not Reconcile**

*POV:* Lin Hao. *Location and time:* the district office that keeps the premises returns, the sixth. *Goal:*

**Chapter 304 — A Security Carried At The Wrong Figure**

*POV:* Lin Hao. *Location and time:* the kitchen at Ferrier Street, the eighth, in the evening. *Goal:*
price three doors in front of the eight people who would have to walk through one of them. *Resistance:*

has done — that the blank seventh line was the only reason one of them could not be found, and that she
just made herself findable on purpose, and that she would like it noted that she did it herself.**

**Chapter 305 — The Answer That Stopped Being True**

*POV:* Lin Hao. *Location and time:* the third floor of the Classification Wing corridor, the ninth; then the

describe it instead, to tell them what it is, and finds that the head of that office has been in the
building since four o'clock and has not come to the door.**

**Chapter 310 — The Answer He Did Not Swear**

*POV:* Lin Hao. *Location and time:* the district counter and the second sub-level, the nineteenth. *Goal:*
keep Wren Kallo off a chained page. *Resistance:* **the correction is the only instrument that works and it
costs the one protection in this book that is real**, and everyone who can tell him that is either under his

the thirtieth of October. *Ending hook:* **Alma Quayle writes out the sentence she wants to write, reads it
once, and does not send it, and tells a clerk she will not send it, because she would rather he came and
heard it from her, and the clerk writes down that she said that, which is worse.**
