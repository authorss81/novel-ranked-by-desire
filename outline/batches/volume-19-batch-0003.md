## CARD 921 — The Lane Prices The Door

- **Chapter:** 921. **POV:** Lin Hao. **Location/time:** a street two districts off, then Brack's lane, the tenth of June, from half past four in the afternoon to about seven.
- **Immediate goal:** get one served answer into one pair of hands inside the hours the card gives, and find out what those hours have been doing since the thirtieth of December.
- **Resistance:** the man it is for keeps hours until six, the door's hours stop at half past five, an answer may not be left anywhere unattended, and a fold in a hand for an hour and a half is the whole of the difficulty.
