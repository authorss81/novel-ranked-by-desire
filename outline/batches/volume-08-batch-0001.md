## Chapter 351

**POV and location:** Lin Hao. The counting room, two floors under the Exchange sales floor, at ten to nine on
the first of January. Hanneke Ruis at the desk with the keys; Solveig Rask at the chained real book with the

**Immediate goal:** be present at a valuation he has never once attended, and hear whether a second call is a

had one to carry.** She will not say it twice. It does not go in the book.

**Emotional or practical consequence:** he is safer than he has ever been and finds out at ten to nine in the
morning that the reason is a clerk's convenience. He says thank you and she tells him not to, for a reason
she gives about the room and not about him.

**Required continuity facts:** the pen is on a chain and the book is chained and a mark cannot be scraped off
without a line in a destruction book and the broker who wrote it. **The call is a word and not a figure. A

**Ending hook:** the line is called on the first of February and on the first of March, until somebody pays

## Chapter 352

**Immediate goal:** have Wren Kallo back at the table without asking her one question.

**Resistance:** **she asked for a week and he has kept it to the day.** She will not say whom she told. He is

## Chapter 360

**POV and location:** Lin Hao. The Classification Wing, second sub-level, on the twenty-sixth of January, at
about eleven in the morning. Alma Quayle, behind her own desk, with a sheet she drafted on the nineteenth of
October at 11.40, read once, and did not send.

**Immediate goal:** be in the room when the sentence she did not send arrives, because it has his address
four lines under her name and there is no way in this city to stop a thing that has been in a division for
three months.

**Resistance:** she is head of classification and she is recused on her own signature and she cannot lift it,
and everything she says in this room is now something she says **as a witness and not as a seal.**

**Major turn:** she sends it. **Four lines, and it says this: an account carried at a composition is reached
through that composition and through nothing else in this city, and a composition can be lowered by any
person keeping that address giving it up at a counter, at any hour, without asking anybody's permission —
which is true, is ordinary, and is the answer to four months of everything the room has tried.** She is not
wrong. **She asks for her recusal to be lifted so that she can answer for the file, and the chair refuses her
in one word, and the one word is *no*, and she says the same four lines out loud in her own building to a
clerk, and it goes in.**

**Emotional or practical consequence:** **the man who buys his brother a door has just been told, by the
best classifier in the city, exactly how the household can be taken apart instead — and she told him because
she is right and because she could not send it for three months and it has been sitting in a division with
his address on it.** And she is diminished by it and the chapter does not soften that either.

**Required continuity facts:** **she is not a villain and is not wrong about a single thing she does in any of
these ten chapters.** She wants to be the person who has to say what a thing is while somebody with an
interest in the answer is standing in front of her. Her son scored two and died in a queue. **Her recusal was
minuted before anybody voted and she asked for that.** **A person may give an address up at any counter at any
hour and it will be dated and it will lower the composition, and the list of the thirtieth of October will
still say eight because it is made and dated and it issues at four.** **Mrs Imrie's entry of *because it is
cheaper* has the hour against it and she is a clerk and is not a character.** No ages for any of them.

**Ending hook and the batch's closing beat:** he goes back up the sixty-one with four lines in his head and
**the wall behind him says nothing at all about anybody**, which for the fourth week running is the only
sentence he gets about his own safety, **and Sun Meiling has not said one word about the third floor since
the sixteenth of January. Four days ran out on the twentieth. He has waited ten days for the sound of it and
there is no sound, and a woman who has stopped announcing that she is going to do a thing is a different
thing from a woman who never said she was.**
