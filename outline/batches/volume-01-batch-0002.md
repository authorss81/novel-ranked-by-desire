# Batch 0002 — Volume One, chapters 11–20

**Batch goal:** Find Ola Bright, make one real public good deed that scores, and take the district's
turned lines off Ola's notebook and onto Beatrix Halm's tram.

**Starting condition:** Rank four. Fourteen lines, two turned, both located on one floor of a Vestry
lift. An audit in eight days. An auditor in his bed who cannot file what she knows, and whose
superiors will one day find out she requisitioned a premises register illegally at four in the
morning.

**Starting relationships:** Ines is sleeping with him and filing true things. Nadia has told everybody.
Ola has a notebook and no fear. Colm Deveraux is frightened and has a door locked. Bernt Halse is in
the sub-level and Ditta is on the benches. Tobias Renn knows there is a supply and is waiting to be
asked the right question. Nobody on the floor knows Lin Hao has said anything to anyone.

**Calendar, fixed by Batch 0001.** The audit opened Monday the nineteenth. Batch 0002 runs
**Monday the 18th to Sunday the 27th** — Ch 11–20, with Ch 11 on the Monday and Ch 20 on the Sunday.
Ch 19's band change is mid-week and Ch 20 is the Sunday night the Field order lands.

**Batch midpoint (Ch 15):** the Ferrier interchange. He pulls a woman out of a tram's path in front of
two hundred witnesses and is scored four hundred grains for it. First deliberate act of public
visibility. Rank five. The cost is that three feeders feel it at once and one of them keeps track.

**Batch climax (Ch 17–18):** Beatrix Halm, who has been keeping the notebook properly since Chapter
eleven, takes him into the cab at the end of her shift, and he uses a full scene budget on her.

**Aftermath:** rank five, three turned, an intelligence source nobody can intercept, and a woman from
the Field standing in a corridor at nineteen with a sealed order and his name on it.

## Chapter cards

### Chapter 11 — Ola Bright

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Vestry clinic corridor and the arcade, Saturday morning, then
the Vestry kitchens.

**Immediate goal:** Get Ola Bright's notebook out of circulation without frightening her, and find out
how many other people in Ostara are writing the same thing down.

**Resistance:** She will not give it up and she will not stop, and stopping is not something she can
do without deciding that she was frightened, which she was not. Nadia Ferrous is in and out of the
kitchen all morning, cheerfully, and Lin Hao cannot be in a room with Nadia Ferrous. And a man from
the Church of the Quiet Number took notes off her, which means the notebook is already somewhere it
should not be.

**Information revealed:** Forty-one pages and eleven entries. A third one is the threshold. Ola's
timetable, her flat, her flatmate, her rota, and the exact words she uses: *cold*, *arcade*, *lift*,
*again*. She has never asked anybody to explain it and she does not want to.

**Action or decision:** He does not take the notebook. He takes something better, which is her
attention — he asks to read it, properly, all of it, sitting down, for as long as it takes — and it
is the first time in four years he has asked a stranger for anything at all. He leaves it with her.
He tells her one true thing to keep it off the counter, which is that a third one is different, and
she writes that down.

**Emotional change:** The first time in the book he uses the reservoir's existence as a *fact in
another person's life* rather than as a problem in his own. It is the most adult thing he has done and
he is not sure yet that it was kind.

**Power or resource change:** The pattern is located, dated and mapped: two places, one lift, one
arcade, a fortnight of Fridays and a Tuesday rotation. He has an intelligence source that does not
know it is one.

[Ledger. Fourteen lines. Two turned.] — *no new message needed; this chapter may carry one only if the
count moves, and it must not.*

**Continuity to preserve:** Ola is not an antagonist, is not frightened, and is 26. Nadia is 23 and an
adult and written as one. Nothing about the reservoir compels anybody.

**Ending type:** Discovery, with a person attached to it.

**Pull:** Beatrix Halm's name is in the notebook eleven times, and on four of them it is spelled
*Bright*, which means somebody in this city has been writing down which cars she has driven.

### Chapter 12 — The Ferrier Line

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Ferrier line, Tuesday morning and evening, running the whole
length from the yards to the Slough.

**Immediate goal:** Find out whether the eleven locations in Ola's notebook have a shape, and whether
the shape is a tram route.

**Resistance:** They do have a shape and the shape is a route, because the woman who feels it is on
the roster for the Ferrier line's arcade car four days a week. He rides it end to end twice and finds
that the nine hundred and eighty is not a number of people at all. It is a number of *passengers*.

**Information revealed, by event, not by explanation:** the account fills by proximity at the rate of
unrecorded good in his vicinity, and a tram full of strangers helping each other to the door is the
densest thing in the city. He gets on with a good day and gets off heavy. He cannot bank it and he
cannot stop earning and both of those are the same sentence.

**Action or decision:** He buys a ticket. He has never in four years paid his own way onto the Ferrier
line because his plate opens the staff gate at seven, and he stands in the queue with the public for
the first time in his working life and nobody looks at him at all, which he had expected to be the
worst thing and which turns out to be the best.

**Emotional change:** A specific, unfamiliar hunger — he wants to be in the room. That is the first
time in the book he has wanted to be *counted*, and he does not know what to do with it.

**Power or resource change:** He understands the mechanic as a job rather than a curse, and it has
already begun to ruin the only advantage he had.

**Continuity to preserve:** A rank-four plate opens the staff gate. Rank five opens held accounts and
does not buy him anything here.

**Ending type:** Escalation — the rule is understood well enough to exploit.

**Pull:** In Ola's notebook, one of the four mis-spellings of his own name is a *date*, and the date
is a Tuesday, and Tuesdays are the days Beatrix Halm drives the arcade car to the yards.

### Chapter 13 — Nine Hundred and Eighty

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Fourth Branch ground floor, the filing room, and the public
counter, Wednesday.

**Immediate goal:** Find out what rank five actually buys, and whether it can be had before Monday the
twenty-second.

**Resistance:** A reading is not a sum of deeds you remember; it is a sum of deeds that get *filed*.
Rank five is 8,000 grains a week and the last four thousand seven hundred of Lin Hao's are not in
any ledger he can see, because the only deeds that count are the ones somebody else stands in front
of and writes down. Tobias Renn explains the mechanism with pleasure. The Exchange counter is where
the distance becomes real: a rank-four man cannot buy a hearing this week. A rank-five man can.

**Information revealed:** the plate office's witness box. A deed is scored when a second person
stands in a marked place and attests it, and the attestation is free, and the only reason Lin Hao has
never used it in four years is that using it would mean standing in the box.

**After any institutional passage, one plain sentence about a named person:** Emrys Vaz, who has a rota
line until the twenty-second and no witness box in his life, and nobody has ever asked him to attest
anything, and it has never once occurred to anyone that he might.

**Action or decision:** He goes to the witness box. He gets into it. He stands in it with nobody to
witness anything and finds out, from a clerk who is not unkind, that a man may attest his own hands.

**Emotional change:** The hoard's first visible crack. He wants the box. That is the whole emotion and
it is shameful and it is the correct one.

[Ledger. Fourteen lines. Two turned.] → moves to sixteen lines, three turned, by the end of the
chapter. *(One message, placed after the mis-spellings are explained.)*

**Power or resource change:** The witness box exists and he has stood in it. The rules of the climb are
now known. Nothing has been gained.

**Continuity to preserve:** Rank five permits held accounts. Rank five is 8,000 grains a week. Lin Hao
is at 4,012 and cannot reach it by any sum of quiet acts, because quiet acts score nothing.

**Ending type:** Decision, taken alone, in a marked place, with nobody watching.

**Pull:** He has a box and he needs a witness, and there are two kinds of witness in this city, and
one of them is a woman who cannot leave a lift.

### Chapter 14 — Two Witnesses

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Vestry, the tram line, and the Fourth Branch, Thursday.

**Immediate goal:** Find a witness who will stand in a box in front of him and be written down.

**Resistance:** Witnesses must be attested, must hold a plate, and must be willing to be associated
publicly with a rank-four attendant. Nadia Ferrous will do it instantly, loudly, and for nothing,
which is the problem. Ines can do it and cannot, because an auditor attesting a drawing account is
the end of her seal. And Ola Bright has no plate and cannot hold one, which means the one person who
is genuinely willing is the one person the city cannot record.

**Information revealed:** association is a public act. Two people can be seen together and read as
one, and rank is a social fact before it is a number. The first time anyone in this book chooses to
be seen standing next to Lin Hao on purpose, it is a woman in a lift who did it before anybody asked
her to.

**Action or decision:** He does not use Nadia. He goes to the one person in Ostara who sees forty
thousand people moving and therefore knows which witnesses are about to do something.

**Emotional change:** Loneliness of a specific kind — he has run out of people who will stand next to
him, and he did that to himself, deliberately, over four years, and now he needs the one thing he
refused to have.

**Power or resource change:** He knows where Beatrix Halm is at the end of her shift. That is not an
ally yet. It is an address.

**Continuity to preserve:** Nadia's gift is that she arrives before anyone else and cannot go up. Her
only route is down. The stopped-lift scene is Chapter 29 and must not be spent here.

**Ending type:** Discovery.

**Pull:** Beatrix Halm does not write the cold minutes down because she is frightened. She writes them
down because nobody in her own division has ever asked her what she feels, and she has been keeping
the real figures in a book for four years and calling it a headache.

### Chapter 15 — The Interchange at Four

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Ferrier interchange, Tuesday evening, four o'clock, rain.

**Immediate goal:** Get four hundred grains, which is four thousand eight hundred of the eight
thousand he needs, in a place where two hundred people can see him doing it.

**Resistance:** The deed does not present itself. He has to *make* it, and the specific clumsy
intention of a man shoving a door is not available to him here, because a staged rescue in front of
two hundred people is a staged rescue, and the moment he has decided to do it on purpose he has
already made it a performance, and there is a woman about to walk into the front of the number
nineteen tram because nobody at the interchange is looking at the road.

**Information revealed, by event:** he is scored. In public. Permanently. The figure arrives on a slip
that two hundred people watch being printed, and it is the first time in his life that a machine has
credited him for something he did rather than something the branch's aggregate decided about him.

**The cost, in the same minute, in the same city:** three feeders feel it at once, and the ledger
reports it, and one of them keeps track. Beatrix Halm feels it in the cab of the nineteen at four
o'clock with thirty people on board and has to finish her shift.

[Ledger. Sixteen lines. Three turned.]

**Action or decision:** He does not go back. He stands in the interchange and watches his name go up
on a plate that is not the Board and know exactly what he has done.

**Emotional change:** The specific delight of being visible, immediately followed by the specific
horror of finding out what it costs other people at a distance he cannot reach.

**Power or resource change:** 4,412 grains. Rank five is 8,000. He has moved four hundred and he has
spent three cold minutes and one of them turned.

**Continuity to preserve:** all three feeders who feel it get a name, a room, a job and a line of
dialogue in this chapter, not later. Do not defer.

**Ending type:** Win immediately followed by its cost.

**Pull:** A rank-four attendant has just been scored four hundred grains in public and the Exchange
counter opens at five, and he has never in his life been in a room with a rank five in it.

### Chapter 16 — What a Band Costs

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Exchange, then the Fourth Branch, Tuesday into Wednesday.

**Immediate goal:** Find out what rank five buys with four hundred grains, and find the rest of it.

**Resistance:** It buys a hearing this week instead of in March. It buys an hour of a scarce machine.
It buys a choice of district and a bed in the Vestry clinic with a name on the door. He does the
arithmetic and it comes out at four more identical deeds at four hundred grains, on four wet
Tuesday evenings, in front of four sets of two hundred people, and every one of them costs three
people a cold minute, and the ninth of those is a person who keeps track.

**Information revealed:** the band is not a reward, it is a queue. Rank five is the rank at which you
stop waiting, and the price of stopping waiting is that other people wait longer, and the person
waiting longest in this arrangement is a man who has spent four years holding doors and has no plate
to attest anything.

**After any institutional passage, one plain sentence about a named person:** Emrys Vaz's rota line is
deleted on the twenty-second, and his shift goes, and he will be re-posted to the sub-levels, and
there is no witness box at the bottom of a yard.

**Action or decision:** He takes a day off. Nobody at the Fourth Branch has ever taken a day off. He
does it because the arithmetic only closes while he is standing still.

**Emotional change:** He is doing exactly what his brother did — converting unrecorded acts into
visible ones, on purpose, in public — and he catches himself doing it and cannot stop and does not
want to stop and that is the most frightening thing in the batch.

**Power or resource change:** Rank five is 4,412 and climbing at four hundred a week on a plan that
takes four more weeks and the plan requires four more Tuesdays.

**Continuity to preserve:** no rank change yet. The band arrives in Chapter 19.

**Ending type:** Momentum, and a cost that is somebody else's.

**Pull:** Tobias Renn has worked out what Lin Hao is doing on Tuesday evenings and has worked out why
a man with no settled account is standing at an interchange.

### Chapter 17 — The Cab at the End of the Shift

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The cab of the number nineteen at the yards, eleven at night,
and the roof of a tram.

**Immediate goal:** Get the district's turned lines off a woman's exercise book and onto somebody who
can use them.

**Resistance:** She is not overcome. She is a signal operator who has been writing down the city's
trouble for four years in a book she calls a headache diary, and she has wanted somebody to *read* it
since she started it, and she has a division that calls it a condition. She opens the scene because
she has decided to, quickly and out loud, and because she wants the use more than she wants him, and
she is not shy about saying so. Her deafness in one ear is a cost she jokes about and it means the
yards at eleven at night are very loud and she has to lean in.

**Information revealed, by event and by conversation:** she feels forty thousand people moving. She
knows where something is wrong before anybody does. She has been keeping the real figures in a book
for four years and the real figures say that the cold minutes in this city cluster along the Ferrier
line, and that there are more of them every month.

**The scene:** written as the scene. Explicit, physical, unhurried, generous, and specific to a cab
with a window and a tram roof with rain coming off the yards. Adults, awake, unmedicated, unrestrained.
Neither impaired. No violation is a beat; the book may not use her deafness as a mechanism and must
not use the access she has to other people's states as a mechanism.

**Action or decision:** She gives him the whole book, all four years, and the price is one sentence
out loud: *and you spend them on people.* He agrees. She says it before they start, which is the
character.

**Emotional change:** Afterwards he has been handed something he cannot be audited for holding, by a
woman who wants him to spend it, and it is the first time in the book that anybody has made him
responsible for the account out loud.

**Power or resource change:** an intelligence source nobody can intercept. Turned lines, locations,
dates, four years. And one of the names in the back of that book is a name he has already seen on a
staff register.

**Continuity to preserve:** she is 27, deaf in one ear since the Veil, and has been told for four years
that her headaches are a condition. She is not a mystery and not a test.

**Ending type:** Alliance, and a promise made out loud.

**Pull:** On the roof of the tram in the rain, she says the word *turn* and tells him what a pattern of
nine does to an analyst in his own division.

### Chapter 18 — Signal

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Ferrier line, the yards, and the branch floor, the following
week.

**Immediate goal:** Work out whether Beatrix's four years of figures and his own ledger agree, and get
them to agree.

**Resistance:** They do not. She has been counting something slightly different from what he has been
carrying — she counts the *effects*, he holds the *addresses* — and when the two are laid on top of
each other the map makes a shape neither of them can say out loud: the unrecorded good in the Slough
and the Vestry is not spread across the city, it is in one arc between the yards and the free
clinics, and it moves along it four times a day.

**Information revealed:** the account is not a private accident. There is a geography to it. And the
geography means that the only man in Ostara who is fed by the whole of the Slough is a man who
commutes the Ferrier line, which he does because his rent is eleven minutes' walk from the stop.

**Action or decision:** He starts taking the tram the long way on purpose, every day, in both
directions, and ines finds out within four days because an auditor notices a pattern in a man's
commuting and cannot help herself.

**Emotional change:** The hoard inverted for the first time. He is deliberately, openly, increasing
what he takes, on purpose, for no reason he can defend — and he cannot stop, because the alternative
is that the Slough stops being kind in doorways.

**Power or resource change:** the ledger at nineteen lines, three turned, all located. A map.

**Continuity to preserve:** Lin Hao's flaw is that he hoards. This is the first time the hoard reads as
the correct choice and it is still wrong.

**Ending type:** Revelation with a geography attached.

**Pull:** Two hundred grains on Tuesday's plate and four hundred is not going to be enough, and there is
an audit in five days.

### Chapter 19 — Field Order

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The Fourth Branch, Wednesday morning.

**Immediate goal:** Get from 4,612 to 8,000 before Monday without another interchange, and stop Tobias
Renn from saying the word *supply* in front of anybody with a seal.

**Resistance:** He cannot. One interchange on a Tuesday is a Tuesday. And Tobias Renn, who is bored
and has been counting for four years, has put the two facts together and has gone to the one person in
the building who can act on them, and Tobias did not do it to destroy him; Tobias did it because a man
with no settled account standing at an interchange four times a month is a *discrepancy*, and Tobias
loves the branch and the deputy warden is the branch.

**Information revealed:** rank five is granted at 8,000 and his figure crosses it mid-week on the
strength of a single public deed and an aggregate the branch has carried for four years. **The band
appears.** White tile, rank five, and it is the first time in his life a tile has moved and the first
time in his life a number has been the answer to a question rather than a distance from one.

**Action or decision:** He takes the band and does not celebrate, because on the same plate the
deputy warden's office has written a request for a Field observation, and Lin Hao knows what a Field
observation is because he has cleaned the corridor they come in through.

[Ledger. Nineteen lines. Three turned.]

**Power or resource change:** rank five. Held accounts, private office, and the right to be asked
before requisitioned. Eight thousand one hundred and forty-one grains.

**Emotional change:** elation, exactly once, for about ninety seconds, in a corridor — and then the
thing he wanted has arrived and it has cost four cold minutes a week to get.

**Ending type:** Payoff, immediately shadowed.

**Pull:** Cassius Rhee wants to see him, and he has forty minutes, and he has never once in four years
been asked to sit down in the Fourth Branch.

### Chapter 20 — A Woman With a File

**POV / location / time:** Lin Hao. The deputy's office, then the corridor outside it, then the street,
Wednesday at nineteen.

**Immediate goal:** Find out what the deputy warden actually wants, and get out of the building.

**Resistance:** Cassius Rhee is not a monster and does not behave like one. He is forty-six and rank
five and his district is nine hundred and eighty grains short *per person per week* and he has been
losing ground to men half his age with better files for two years. He does not want Lin Hao punished.
He wants Lin Hao *employed*. He has a room in his own office with a door that closes, and he has a
proposal, and the proposal is that a portable merit source inside a branch is a *stabilisation*, and
that a stabilised district is nine thousand people who do not get moved.

**Information revealed:** he has known what Lin Hao is for eleven days and he has read every plate Lin
Hao has submitted and he is not going to file anything, because a filed supply is a supply somebody
else owns. He says the arithmetic out loud and it is correct.

**Action or decision:** Lin Hao does not take the job and does not refuse it outright, because a man
who says no to the only person in the building who is offering him anything gets nothing at all. He
asks for one thing — the audit — and Rhee grants it in about eleven seconds, and it costs him
everything he was going to use the audit for.

**Emotional change:** the vertigo of being *recruited*. He has spent four years being looked through
and the worst thing that has ever happened to him is a man in a good coat being kind to him.

**Power or resource change:** rank five held. The audit is deferred by nine days, which buys the Branch
a fortnight and buys Lin Hao nothing, because the audit is the only clock he has.

**Ending type:** Threat with an offer attached, and a woman in the corridor.

**Pull:** Runa Sato is standing at the end of the corridor with a Field order and a file, and she
looked at him before she looked at Rhee's door, and the file has a wax seal on it that Lin Hao has
only ever seen on one other thing in his life.

## Required from this batch

- **Rank four throughout until Chapter 19.** No rank-five language before the Exchange chapter.
- **Never speak the word *reservoir*.** It is named in Volume 2. The Bureau's word is *unsettled*.
- **No feeder thanks him.** Halvard Rees sets the pattern in Chapter 3: he refuses to, out loud, and
  warns him against doing it twice. A feeder who thanks him breaks the mechanic.
- **No paragraph over roughly 120 words.** Ch 8 and Ch 10 of Batch 0001 already ran long; Batch 0002
  should not.
- **Ration, do not spend:** *there is no form for this* (all three escalations spent) and *there is no
  column for it* (spent, none until Volume 7).
- One explicit scene, Chapter 17. One System message at most per chapter, and only where the ledger
  genuinely turns. Chapters 11, 13, 15 and 19 carry one.
- No term from later volumes. No Channel, no quiet account, no Ninth Seal, no held account.
- No filler roster. Beatrix, Ola, Nadia, Ines, Tessa, Tobias, Cassius Rhee and Runa Sato have lines.
  Marguerite Vale, Sun Meiling, Wren Kallo and the Concil do not appear.
- The prose mix is 60–70 per cent plain, 20–30 per cent documentary, and no more than three
  documentary paragraphs in a row before a person walks into the room.
- After any passage about how the Bureau works, one plain sentence about what it means for one named
  person.
- **Beatrix's deafness is a cost she jokes about. It is never a mechanism.** No scene in this batch
  may read differently if it were removed.
