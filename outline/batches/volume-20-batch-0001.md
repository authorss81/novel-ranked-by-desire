- **Resistance:** everything available to him is paper, and paper is what he has refused six ways already: a list is refused, a correction is refused, a copy is refused, a transcript is refused, a third refusal is refused, and **there is no instrument anywhere in this city that covers a man standing still.** He says all six out loud with the door open and the clerk counts them and does not agree with the count of six.
- **Information revealed:** **the only thing left that is not paper is where he is.** A man at the bottom of that stair is a man nobody can be led to and a man nobody can be charged for reaching, because the reaching has already happened. And the second half, which is worse: **a man at the bottom of his own stair is a man standing in the only public room this office has ever had.**
- **Action or decision:** **HIS, AND IT IS A PRACTICE AND NOT INK: FROM TOMORROW, THE TWENTY-FOURTH OF MARCH, HE STANDS AT THE FOOT OF THAT STAIR FROM A QUARTER TO TEN TO HALF PAST FIVE ON WORKING MORNINGS, FIVE DAYS IN SEVEN.** He says the cost out loud with the door open before he has done it once: **about four hundred yards of distance gone, a door nobody can knock on without walking past him, every carrier's back visible for as long as this goes on, and about four people a day who will learn his face before they learn anything else.** **AND THE CARD IS NOT TOUCHED AND GETS NO FOURTH LINE, AND THE THING HE HAS NOT SAID, WHICH THE CHAPTER SAYS ONCE AND DOES NOT ARGUES, IS THAT HE HAS ALSO DECIDED HE WILL NOT BE THE ONLY ONE, AND THAT DECISION IS NOT ENTERED ANYWHERE EITHER.**
- **Emotional change:** a man who spent nine years making sure he could not be found spending the last private thing he had left on being the obstacle in a third court, and doing it out loud, to a clerk who has to write none of it down.
- **Power or resource change:** none. Rank one throughout. No band, permission, standing or shift change, and the branch night shift is not moved.
- **Continuity fact to preserve:** the card carries the street, the door and the hours and not one name, and it is not taken down and not re-nailed and gets no fourth line and no hour about a turn of the year; the second column keeps four lines; the bundle stays at twenty-six things and nothing is taken off that table and nothing is put on it; the drawer stays shut; the inside of that coat on the left stays empty; **no figure of comers in any form and the price at the foot of the hill is not given a figure in this chapter**; the two written refusals stand and no third is produced; the sooner figure is not rubbed out and the third figure is still unchalked and still owed; nothing goes at the foot of the standing sheet; the head is not printed and not mentioned; no form ordinal.
- **Ending type:** irreversible decision with its cost named aloud at the place it is spent. **Next-chapter pull:** the thirtieth of March, a sheet that comes down at four, a head said at a counter by a man who is paid nothing, and the first morning at the bottom of that stair.**
- **Title:** The Bottom Of His Own Stair (6 words). **Footer:** rail at eighty-three, re-derived against the thirtieth of December and not carried down from 959; drawer one thousand nine hundred and fifty-two, sixty-five months and two days.
