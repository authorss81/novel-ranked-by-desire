## Chapter 51 — The Held Band

**POV and location.** Lin Hao, Fourth Branch second floor into the Reading pavement, morning after the Review, trolley at ten behind him.

**Immediate goal.** Get through the first morning holding six without spending anyone: clean the floor, carry the copy slip and four counterfoils, and do not look at the Board.

**Resistance.** The branch looks at him now — ninety-one people who watched, a corridor board with only a date, porters who nod. The figure drifts where he stands. Three new turned carry colds he caused. Ines keeps to the corridor.

**Major turn.** He presents the plate at the branch socket for the morning check and the machine reads the band against the floor in front of a queue: band holds on the instrument, figure reads 8,138. The room learns what held means — the rank stays and the merit is not there.

**Emotional or practical consequence.** He understands the gap is now public property: anyone with a socket can read that the band sits above the plate. Relief at surviving the Review curdles into exposure.

**Required continuity facts.** Plate 8,138; band six seated on requisition plus finding; ninety-one lines eight turned one himself; query closed; copy slip pocketed with counterfoils; petition turned face down the night before. Branch board empty except the Review date.

**Ending hook or completed beat.** A Wing runner leaves a plain summons at the plate office: attend with the plate, bringing the finding. No charge, no box ticked. Ends on a new danger.

**Immediate goal.** Satisfy the summons: present the plate, show the instrument, be described, and leave.

**Resistance.** The inventory room has procedures for every shape except his. **Ending hook or completed beat.** Ends on a cost incurred: the clerk stamps the attendance sheet with a second date. He is now owned by a calendar.
