- **What it costs, said before the ink would be dry if there were any ink:** about nine answers a day will now be given by people who have not been in that room for years, by a woman who has never read that book, and by a man who has carried a strap and has never read a row to anybody in his life, and the whole of nine years of argument about the words used is now three people's argument and not his. **It cannot be un-said, because it was said in a room with the door open and there is not one instrument in this city that can call a mouth back.**
- **Power or resource change:** none. Rank one throughout. The branch night shift is not moved. The mornings are not bought and not asked for and the four nights in seven are not touched.
- **Continuity fact to preserve:** **there is no ink in this chapter**; **the thirteenth practice is not quoted back as an authority and he acts on it in his own words and is not asked to produce it**; the third carrier is a man who carries the strap and is not aged and not named; no figure of comers; no list; no form ordinal; the head is not printed; the standing figures are not counted.
- **Ending type:** irreversible decision in a mouth with the door open, and the closing image is a court at twenty to five in the dark with two people going up a stair saying the three lines to each other, one of them wrong, and a man at the bottom who does not stop them and does not correct them.
