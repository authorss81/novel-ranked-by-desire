# Volume One — Rank Four (chapters 1–50)

> In this volume, **Lin Hao must hold rank six through the Winter Review**, or **the reservoir is requisitioned by the Fourth Branch and he never gets it back**.

## Central pressure

Rhee's branch is nine thousand people short. The Bureau is running a **draw-down audit** across the
Fourth Branch, which means every member of staff is being re-read at short notice and anyone who cannot
show a settled account is sent for classification. Lin Hao is the only person on ninety-one staff who has
nothing to show. He needs rank six — the first rank with a right to *petition a sealed file* — and he has
eight weeks, a stranger's cold minutes, and a job that consists entirely of being the person nobody looks
at.

## Starting state

**Lin Hao, 25.** Rank four. Four years cleaning six floors of washrooms at the Fourth Branch. Owns one
plate, one knife, one chair. Rides the tram home past the Board every Thursday without looking at it.

**Starting relationships:** his brother Lin Wei, three years gone. No partner. One person in the world who
has looked at him with any attention, and that person is a photograph of two men on a tram step.

## Starting power level

Rank four, measured, 3,000 grains a week. Reservoir: nothing yet. He does not know he has one.

## Major locations

The Fourth Branch washrooms, second floor, no carpet. The Reading Square and the Board. The counter at
the branch plate office, where the clerks work. Ferrier Street, where he rents a room above a chop-house.
Lantern Row, where Ines Duarte rents two rooms she cannot afford on a rank-two salary. The Vestry clinic.
The Ferrier tram line.

## Major factions

**The Fourth Branch** and its ninety-one staff, who are all being re-read. **The Field**, in the person of
Runa Sato, sent to watch. **The Classification Wing**, unnamed until Chapter 46. **Tobias Renn**, who
wants Lin Hao to fail and has a filing to prove it.

## Midpoint reversal (Chapter 25)

Lin Hao spends nineteen grains on a stranger to stop something going wrong in the branch supply room, and
the stranger's cold minute is not the worst of it — the worst is that the nineteen grains **came from
somewhere he cannot see**. He goes looking for the source and finds that the ledger has been running for
months, and that people in his building have been feeding it for weeks without any of them knowing.

**The reversal:** the reservoir is not something that happened to him. It has been running the entire
time and he has been taking it for granted, and the first thing he must do now is decide whether he is
going to go on taking it. From Chapter 25 he is no longer a man with a strange reading. He is a man with
a supply.

## Escalation sequence

**1–10 — the plate and the cost.** The reading comes back *unsettled*. He is put on the Thursday review
list, which puts him in Ines Duarte's audit queue. He spends, by accident, on a stranger on a tram, and a
lift operator named Nadia Ferrous — who does not know it yet — is one of the people the ledger is taking
from. He spends on purpose and the cost is the same. Ines opens his file and finds the two-year gap and
his brother's name. Chapter 7 she comes to his rooms and does not take her coat off. **Chapter 8** she
sleeps with him, and afterwards she says a name from his ledger that he did not know he had. Chapter 10
he sees what classification looks like from the outside: a man off the fourth floor, taken for a reading,
not back that shift.

**11–20 — the first ledgers.** Tobias Renn's filing puts the branch on the draw-down list early. Lin Hao
has to find the one thing he can do that the Bureau cannot audit, which is make somebody's week survivable
and be seen doing it. At the Ferrier interchange on a Tuesday evening he pulls a woman out of a tram's
path in front of two hundred people and is scored four hundred grains for it. Rank five. The cost is that
Nadia Ferrous and two others feel the spend at once and one of them *keeps track*. Beatrix Halm, a tram
operator who has been writing down the cold minutes she feels, takes him to the cab at the end of a shift
and decides what to do about him. Runa Sato arrives at 19 with a Field order and a file.

**21–30 — the staff audit.** The draw-down lands on ninety-one people who are not the problem. Lin Hao
discovers that his supply is not only relief he can give — it can be *routed*, and that routing it into
someone makes their Exchange account move, though he cannot yet do it deliberately and cannot yet explain
it. He uses it once, badly, and the clinic's records no longer match. Tessa Okonkwo catches it, because
she is the only person in the building who notices what a body does when it has been relieved of something
it should still be carrying. At the Thursday line on Chapter 28 he makes his irreversible decision: he
stands up in front of the branch staff and says that the unsettled reading is his, it is on their floor,
and it will be read publicly every week whether he likes it or not — because an unsettled reading attached
to a floor is a floor that gets audited, and he will not have ninety-one people sent for classification
over his account. He loses his anonymity permanently and buys the branch a fortnight. Chapter 30: the
Board repaints, and his tile carries a fifth figure that is not a rank.

**31–40 — the price of six.** Marguerite Vale finds him through the Vant and offers to put the reservoir
into a legal quiet account, which would make it untouchable, for a price and a favour. He refuses. The
refusal costs him: he has no leverage, so Runa's report goes in. He climbs by committing witnessed deeds at
a rate that is going to be visible by February. In Cordwain the Field requisitions a block of flats where
the scores were adjusted for a supervisor's convenience, and Lin Hao ends up in a requisitioned room with
Runa Sato and both of them injured and neither of them fit for anything else. Chapter 37: he is provisionally
six. Chapter 38: five turned lines. Chapter 40: the Winter Review is scheduled for the sixth week and he
has three grains of margin.

**41–50 — the Review.** Everything he has been counting on collapses: the person he was going to spend on
to reach six is already turned, and spending on a turned person is how you kill them. He has to reach six
from nothing, in public, in front of nine thousand people watching a board. Chapter 45 Marguerite terms
arrive. Chapter 46 she takes him to the best rooms he will ever stand in and neither of them is pretending
about anything. Chapter 47 is the Review. Chapter 48: he takes the Review's open-question slot — the live
public exercise where a candidate answers a question on the Board floor with no notice — and he uses the
reservoir **on himself**, once, for the first time in the book, and the answer is true.

That is the irreversible decision. It gets him rank six. It costs him: a line in his own ledger that says
himself, which has never been there before and should never be, and three feeders turning at once because
nothing he has ever done was this size.

**Consequence, Chapter 50:** the Winter Review reads him six. He petitions Lin Wei's file at rank six,
which is legal. The file has **four thousand one hundred and twelve folios; four thousand one hundred and
eleven are sealed to him; one is not.** The one is a transfer receipt, in his brother's hand, recording
214,000 grains moved on the eleventh of a month three years ago into an account that the index does not
contain. And underneath it, in the hand of the Classification Wing, a second line: the file of the person
who received it is **Held Account One**. There is no Held Account Zero.

## Concrete resolution

Rank six held through the Review. The file is open by one folio. He knows his brother moved merit
somewhere. Rhee does not get the reservoir, and knows that he will have to go over Lin Hao's head to get
it, and starts preparing to. Ines is in his life and in his professional danger. Six women have decided
he is worth something. And the Fourth Branch is not being audited, because a man in a washroom told the
truth in front of his colleagues, and ninety-one people know it, and nobody has thanked him.

## New question for Volume Two

If the reading is unsettled and it is being fed by the whole floor, then who was feeding it before he
arrived — and why does a file three years old say it was already open?

## Final chapter image

Ch 50, late, the Fourth Branch washroom, second floor, no carpet. Lin Hao is cleaning the last row of
basins with a filed petition drying on the counter beside the soap, and the Board is nine streets away with
his name on a black tile for the first time since he was a child, and he does not go and look at it. Three
people in the building felt something cold tonight and none of them know why.

# Batch plans

Detailed chapter cards for Batch 0001 are in `outline/batches/volume-01-batch-0001.md`. Batches 0002
through 0005 are planned here at batch level; their chapter cards are written by the phase that writes
them, and must not contradict the beats below.

## Batch 0001 — chapters 1–10

**Batch goal:** Survive the plate, learn what a draw costs, and be read by the only person in the city who
can read records.

**Starting condition:** Rank four. No reservoir awareness. One photograph. Nobody knows his name except
the staff who employ him.

**Starting relationships:** Ines Duarte has never spoken to him. Nadia Ferrous has never seen him. The
brother is three years gone.

**Batch midpoint (Ch 5–6):** Tobias Renn's filing puts the branch on the draw-down list early, and Lin Hao
is forced to spend during his own abstention. He chooses a stranger over his own safety and buys it with
a line in the ledger.

**Batch climax (Ch 7–8):** Ines opens his file, finds the gap and the brother's name, and comes to his
rooms. The scene happens. Afterwards she says a name from his ledger that he did not know he had.

**Required character changes:** Lin Hao goes from a man with a strange reading to a man who has spent it
on purpose and knows it hurts somebody. Ines goes from an auditor with a queue to a woman who cannot file
what she knows. Nadia Ferrous goes from a girl who felt something odd to a witness who is going to keep
talking about it.

**Required power changes:** Reservoir awareness. One draw by accident, one by choice, one refused. Ledger
learned. Three lines in it, one of them turned. Abstaining for nine days costs him sleep and steadiness.

**Required world reveals:** *unsettled* is a Bureau word and means a figure with no interval. Rank six
petitions sealed files. Classification exists, has a physical address, and is not survivable on your own
terms. Being read publicly is permanent.

**Open questions after the batch:** Who is the turned line? What is in the file that Ines is not saying?
What happens to the ninety-one people on the floor?

## Batch 0002 — chapters 11–20

**Batch goal:** Find Ola Bright, make one real public good deed that scores, and take the district's turned
lines off Ola's notebook and onto Beatrix Halm's tram.

**Starting condition:** Rank four, one turned line, an auditor he cannot get rid of and cannot get out of.

**Starting relationships:** Ines is sleeping with him and filing true things. Nadia has told everybody.

**Batch midpoint (Ch 15):** the Ferrier interchange. He pulls a woman out of a tram's path in front of two
hundred witnesses and scores four hundred grains doing it. First deliberate act of public visibility, and
the cost is three feeders feeling it at once.

**Batch climax (Ch 17):** Beatrix Halm, who has been keeping the notebook properly since Chapter 11, takes
him into the cab at the end of her shift.

**Required character changes:** Lin Hao learns he can be seen on purpose. Beatrix goes from observer to
ally. Ola Bright goes from a nuisance to an asset who wants to be paid in gossip.

**Required power changes:** Rank four to five. Ledger at nine lines, three turned. First use of a public
deed as a deliberate instrument.

**Required world reveals:** Rank five permits held accounts. The Field has an order form. Turned lines can
be triangulated and the Bureau knows the technique.

**Open questions after the batch:** Who put the Field order on him? What did Tobias get out of it? How
many people are turned and where?

## Batch 0003 — chapters 21–30

**Batch goal:** Keep ninety-one people out of classification, and pay for it.

**Starting condition:** Rank five, three turned lines, draw-down list in force, branch staff frightened.

**Starting relationships:** Ines cannot report him and is sleeping badly. Beatrix is supplying
intelligence. Nadia is a public relations problem.

**Batch midpoint (Ch 25):** he tests routing deliberately in the Vestry clinic, puts it into the wrong
person, and the clinic's intake records stop matching what the bodies in front of her are. Then he
realises the ledger has been running for months rather than weeks, and that he has been taking it for
granted. **Do not restate Chapter 4's dread here.** Chapter 4 was a man noticing a number move. Chapter 25
is a man choosing what to do about it and getting it wrong on a patient.

**Batch climax (Ch 26–28):** Tessa catches the clinic discrepancy, they go to the cold store, and on the
Thursday line he tells ninety-one colleagues that the reading is his and will be public.

**Required character changes:** He stops hoarding the one thing he has. Tessa goes from clinician to ally.
Nadia goes from rumour to household. Tobias is destroyed by his own filing.

**Required power changes:** First *routing* of merit into another account, done badly and not yet
understood. Ledger at seventeen lines, five turned. Permanent loss of anonymity.

**Required world reveals:** Merit that is routed moves an Exchange account. The Board can show a figure
that is not a rank. The Classification Wing has been tracking unsettled readings since before Lin Hao
arrived.

**Open questions after the batch:** What is the fifth figure on his tile? Who has been feeding the ledger
since before he worked here?

## Batch 0004 — chapters 31–40

**Batch goal:** Get to six without the Vant, with the Field watching.

**Starting condition:** Rank five, public, exhausted, five turned lines, and a supply he does not
understand.

**Starting relationships:** Marguerite has found him. Runa has a file and no instructions beyond
observation.

**Batch midpoint (Ch 32–33):** the Vant's offer and his refusal, and the immediate cost of refusing —
he has no leverage, so Runa's report goes in and the Field gets a mandate.

**Batch climax (Ch 36):** Cordwain. A requisition of a block of flats where scores were adjusted. Lin Hao
and Runa Sato in a requisitioned room, both injured, and the decision about whose side he is on.

**Required character changes:** Lin Hao takes the first deliberate public risk of his life and chooses a
side. Runa falsifies a report. Marguerite keeps investing after he refuses her.

**Required power changes:** Provisional rank six. Ledger at forty-one lines, five turned — he stops
spending, which is the first time the count goes down.

**Required world reveals:** The Field's requisition protocol. The Cordwain score adjustments, and why
nine thousand people are three hundred grains short in a district Rhee runs.

**Open questions after the batch:** He is six in draft. What stands between him and the Board?

## Batch 0005 — chapters 41–50

**Batch goal:** Hold rank six through the Winter Review, or lose everything to the Fourth Branch.

**Starting condition:** Provisionally six. Three grains of margin. His best remaining lead is already
turned, and spending on a turned person is how you kill them.

**Starting relationships:** Everyone is waiting to see what he does at the Review.

**Batch midpoint (Ch 44):** the empty account. He has no one left to spend on, and the Board is repainted
every Thursday.

**Batch climax (Ch 47–48):** the Review, the open-question slot, and the one time he spends the reservoir
on himself.

**Required character changes:** Lin Hao spends himself. Ines finds out and cannot decide whether to file
it. Marguerite's terms are accepted for the wrong reason.

**Required power changes:** Six held. Ledger at ninety-one lines, eight turned, and one of the lines is
himself.

**Required world reveals:** Four thousand one hundred and twelve folios, one unsealed. A transfer receipt
in his brother's hand. Held Account One.

**Open questions after the batch:** Held Account One has no zero. What is inside it, and what does the
Classification Wing know about an account that has been open for three years?
