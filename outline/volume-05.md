# Volume Five — The Auditor's Price (chapters 201–250)

> In this volume, **Lin Hao must keep Ines Duarte off his file while she starts wanting to be off her
> own**, or **the only person who can read him honestly is removed from his life**.

**This file was reconstructed during the Batch 0001 review repair, eleven chapters into the volume, and
that has to be said plainly because it changes how much weight anything in it carries.** Volumes One
through Four each had an owned outline written before their first chapter. This one did not; the Volume 4

## 1. The sentence, and where it came from

`outline/volume-04.md` §8 closed Volume 4 with a question and not an answer:

> **What does a woman do who is the only person in Ostara who can read the record correctly, when the
> record is now her own household's?**

**Batch 0001 answers it in Chapter 205, twenty-three pages before the chapter that will have to live with
the answer.** She reads the household's file out loud on her own floor, in date order, and finds two
things: her own division raised her premises and did not tell her, and her own line on the certification
schedule carries no instrument. **She does not say why. Nothing in Volume 5 before Chapter 246 may make
her say why**, and `bible/characters.md` does not record a reason.

The volume's real shape, therefore, is not a mystery about Ines. **It is a woman deciding whether to be
a fact about herself on purpose.** Batch 0001's last line is her asking to be put on a sworn list on his
word rather than on an instrument — the weakest standing available to a person in this city, voidable,
and gone the day his plate goes — because for four years the one thing she owned was that she was in
nobody's book, and she worked out on the twenty-third that being outside a book is not the same as being
safe in a room.

## 6. THE MOVEMENTS, 201 to 250. REWRITTEN TWENTY THIRTY CHAPTERS IN.

**Movements 1, 2 and 3 are now WRITTEN. This file has been rewritten against the page twice — by Batch
0002 at twenty chapters and by Batch 0003 at thirty — because a plan written eleven chapters into a volume
is an act of invention and a plan written thirty chapters into one is an act of cowardice. What follows is

answer takes the better answer and rewrites this file in the same commit.**

1. **The Auditor's Price — 201–210. WRITTEN.** He certifies six and it costs Sun Meiling her only
   standing; she takes it back a night earlier, in pencil, on her own row. Ines asks to be carried.
2. **The Enforcer — 211–220. WRITTEN.** Petra Ilyin comes to collect from Marguerite and stays. **The
   movement's subject turned out not to be the certificate at all: it is that a household acquires members
   inside obligations it cannot see.** Eleven years before Batch 0002, Marguerite secured a thirty-year
   account against a woman instead of against money, and the woman could not leave a room, and neither of
   them ever asked the other a question. **What it spent:** the lead's plate, pledged in a chained book
   with no right of withdrawal; **what it bought:** a debt for Volume 6, a standing a stranger can call on
   the twenty-seventh, and a household that is now on a public board in ink.
3. **The Notice — 221–230. WRITTEN.** The batch's real discovery is not that the certificate is coming.
   It is that **an audit index is constituted from returned lines and not from totals, so an empty line
   on a district return is read, four divisions away, as a person.** Marit Kolb left one on the third of
   June at ten past seven in the evening; the audit division counted it on the twelfth; the household
   answered it on the fourteenth with six. **What the batch spent:** the last thing the lead had, which is
   that he is a man whose answers are all true; and the household's only copy of itself, cut up at a
   kitchen table on the nineteenth. **What it bought:** Wren Kallo back on the page and a Bureau
   counter-forensics finding about him that reaches a desk on the twenty-third; a description of six women
   in a broker's hand; a dated death for the thirty-year account on the fourth of July; and a seventh row
   in pencil in a coat pocket. **And the certificate is now recorded on the page as Batch 0004's, with the
   reason it is Batch 0004's — the twenty-first to the thirtieth — stated in the state files and not left
   to be discovered.** Ch 228 is Volume 5's third scene and it is written: Wren Kallo, the Reading Square

   makes a house visible is now carried by the household's own act: Sun Meiling cuts the roll at the ruled

4. **The Price — 231–240. WRITTEN.** **The schedule goes up on the twenty-sixth and the certificate
   is dated the twenty-seventh, and both issue despite the two bars, because an institution acts

   to her.** **CH 241 IS DECIDED: AGNETE LINDQVIST'S SCENE.** Batch 0003 proposed it and Batch 0004
   takes it, for the reason Batch 0003 gave: her gift is that a room where she stands cannot compel

**Escalation, stated once so no batch has to re-derive it.** Early he investigates the record. **At Ch 217
he stops investigating and starts spending** — the first irreversible act of his own in five volumes, and
it costs him the only thing he has. **At Ch 224 he spends again, and this time he spends his own
reliability**: a false sworn answer in his own name to keep a woman out of an index, and it costs him the
last thing that was true about him. Mid-volume he manipulates it: the head's sworn answer outranks a
queue's statement because a man's name is at the top of one, and a roll that agrees with a sworn answer is
a copy, and a copy can be produced out of a file. **And at Ch 229 a woman destroys the household's only

opposition is not an institution but a woman walking out, which is what `outline/series.md` line 104
already promises. **And the volume's personal sacrifice is Ines's, made deliberately, in public, in her
own hand.**
