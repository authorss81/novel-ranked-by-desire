### 1b. THE OTHER DOOR, AND WHY IT IS NOT THE SAME ONE

**Wren Kallo is not the woman who told him. She is the woman who is going to tell somebody else**, and the
volume sentence's two halves are one door because of what her speaking does.

- **Chapter 350, last line:** she goes down sixty-one steps at twenty to two in the morning of the last night
  of the year, *and she does not know yet who she is going to tell.*
- **She may never be asked about it.** Chapter 316 and 350 and 342: *you may not put one question to me
  about that address and I will say I do not know what you are talking about.* **He has asked once, on the
  sixth of December, and received the standing answer, and said he would not ask again.**
- **What she has is a shape, and it is this one (350):** two documents about the same eight women at the
  same door, made inside a month by the same pair of hands, in one of which she is a Bureau officer
  certifying a composition at a distance and in the other she is the fourth line of a list of persons at
  her own address. *There is not another person alive in this city who can see that shape, and there is not
  another person alive in this city who is forbidden to hear about it either.*
- **And what she says she is going to do with it, if anybody asks her:** *tell them it costs nothing to be
  wrong about me and a great deal to be right.*

**SO THE TWO DOORS JOIN LIKE THIS, AND IT IS THE WHOLE OF THE VOLUME'S PRESSURE:** the description of
premises 34-6-118 was referred for classification on the nineteenth of October with **the reason being a song
made in public on the fifteenth which cannot be reproduced in writing and for which no author has been paid**
(302, 311). Every division that wants a reason on that file now wants an author for that song. **The only
person in Ostara who can supply one is the woman who sang it, and she is at premises 34-6-118 on a public
list with her room against it.** **So when Wren Kallo speaks — which she has decided to and has not chosen
whom — the shape she gives them ends up at the iron stair.**

**And he cannot find out whether it already has, because he is not permitted to ask, and she will lie to his
face in whatever room he asks it in.**

**THAT IS WHAT THE VOLUME SENTENCE MEANS AND IT IS THE FIRST TIME IN EIGHT VOLUMES THAT THE WOMAN AND THE
DOOR HAVE BEEN THE SAME MAN.** Every scene in 351–400 is between a woman and a man who cannot pay for what
she is doing. This one is the volume's engine and it is not a scene and it must never be written as one.

**IT IS ALSO THE ONLY THING IN THIS FILE THAT WAS NOT OBVIOUS, AND IT IS WHY THE PROMPT ASKED.** The cheap
answer is that the volume is about Wren Kallo's decision. **It is not.** Wren's decision is Volume 8's clock;
Sun Meiling is Volume 8's woman; and the thing the volume is about is a man buying a door by making himself
the holding, in front of eight women who will pay for it and one of whom is the reason.

**FOUR LESSONS THAT EACH COST A VOLUME 7 BATCH DAYS AND THAT ARE INHERITED WHOLE:**

1. **NEVER RUN A PARAGRAPH-LENGTH SPLITTER OVER PROSE THAT CONTAINS DIALOGUE.** Batch 0004 ran two and lost
   three of four days and had to restore a closed chapter from git twice. **The only permitted pass is a
   reporter that prints paragraph lengths and writes nothing.** A dialogue paragraph opens with a quotation
   mark and closes with one and no speech runs across a paragraph break. **Run the line-boundary check
   before anything else and again after every hand split**, because a hand split is exactly how a blank line
   goes missing.
2. **COUNT PER FILE AND SUM. NEVER RECORD A VOLUME WORD COUNT FROM A CONCATENATION.** `cat
   chapters/volume-07/*.md | wc -w` returned 107,439 against a true 107,477, because thirty-nine of the
   fifty files carry no trailing newline and concatenating merges the last word of each file into the first
   word of the next. It loses 38 words.
3. **A BORROWED BODY IS A CANON ERROR AND A TWELVE-GRAM SCAN CANNOT SEE ONE. Volume 7 shipped three** — a
   wrist scar, a grey temple and an ink claim — **and the twelve-gram found exactly one match in the whole
   volume, the framed refrain at 344 and 345, which was the right answer.** **Check borrowed bodies before
   borrowed sentences. When you find one, remove the claim; do not move it.** The first repair of the grey
   temple kept the borrowed detail and moved it to the other side, which put an unmarked exception into a
   rule Chapter 72 states out loud, and had to be redone from nothing. **The table of marks already owned is
   in §4 and a batch reads it before it writes a body.**
4. **A GATE RECORD IS A CLAIM. RE-RUN IT FROM OUTSIDE THE PASS THAT WROTE IT AND LET THE RE-RUN OUTRANK THE
   RECORD.** The Volume 7 close audit found its own gate block printing Chapter 350 at two different numbers,
   both stale; a stale volume plan glued into the wrong row of a state index; a scene figure printed against
   the wrong stated convention; a cross-reference pointing one item short; and a claim that a directory was
   absent in the same commit that created it. **No gate that this phase or any batch writes is true until a
   pass that did not write the prose has re-run it.**

**TWO CARRIED DISCREPANCIES THAT VOLUME 8 MUST SETTLE ON THE PAGE AND NOT IN A RECORD:**

- **Wanda Rej's plaster.** Chapter 341, on the first of December: *the plaster comes off on the twelfth.*
  Chapters 342 to 350 cover the sixth to the thirtieth of December and **not one of them says a word about
  it.** Either it came off on the twelfth of December off the page or the sentence is stale. **Volume 8 must
  state, on the page, when the plaster came off and what her hand says now, and must not resolve the
  discrepancy by explaining it away in a review file.**
- **The floor of 8,142, 8,143, 8,144, 8,145** was printed on the thirtieth of July, August, September and
  December, and Volume 7's own text calls the card a *quarterly* card and the thirtieth *the last working
  day of a quarter.* **Both readings stand on the page and this file does not resolve them.** The rule for
  Volume 8 is narrow and it is enough: **the floor does not move again until the thirtieth of March, and no
  chapter prints a threshold that is not a plate or a card printed in that chapter.**
