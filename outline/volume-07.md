## 1. The sentence, and what Volume 6 handed it

`outline/series.md` inverts the direction of travel for the third time in three volumes. **Volume 5 was
people being taken off his file without his asking. Volume 6 was him putting things on paper about himself
on purpose. Volume 7 is the volume the paper starts working.**

Volume 6's failure condition was fulfilled to the letter and at his own request: **the reservoir is priced,
the price is seven, and the price was asked for by him** (296). Its close is a sentence on the last page of
Chapter 300, and the sentence is this:

> **A classification does not need a man in it. It needs an address, and a figure, and a reason.**

**He has all three and he has not been classified.** Seven documents about premises 34-6-118 in four or five
divisions, and not one of them has his name at the top of a page.

**The volume's engine is therefore not a chase and it is not a raid. It is a piece of arithmetic.** An
account is carried at an address and is answerable by the composition of that address. **That is true and it
is in a real book and it was true on the morning of the first of October.** **On the morning of the second of
October, at about eleven, at a counter, in the ordinary way, a woman of reading age takes an address at that
premises — and the composition is eight, and the priced line says seven, and both figures are now standing
in the same book.**

Nothing in this city can unwrite the first figure. **So the account cannot be carried as it stands, and an
account that cannot be carried is not a security any more. It is a thing that has to be described.**

**And a description of an address does not stop at the address.** It goes through the door and lists the
people who live behind it, in order, with dates, because that is what a description of a house is.

**That is Volume 7's whole pressure, and it is worse than Volume 6's: there is nothing to refuse.** Every
defence he built in six volumes — a blank seventh line that cannot be attached to the woman it was cut for, a
composition sworn twice at the figure of seven — is a defence of the household's invisibility, and **the
classification is aimed at the invisible part.**

**The volume's title is its turn.** He has been band six on a piece of paper and band five on his plate since
Volume 5, and his plate has not moved a grain in two hundred and forty chapters. **Seven is the band that
carries requisition of merit from a person and opens the sealed archive.** To survive a classification he
has to be heard by somebody entitled to hear him, and the only instrument in this city that hears him is a
band on a plate, and a band is earned by deeds that are attested and written down by other people.

**He has refused to do that since Chapter 48, because every advantage feels like theft from somebody
smaller. Volume 7 is the volume he does it, publicly, in front of witnesses, on the record, and finds out
what it costs.**

**Batch 0001 owns the second of October to the nineteenth of October. Nothing in it may be taken back.**

## 5. The fixed calendar, and one defect in it that this volume must not widen

- **A month is thirty days and has no thirty-first. The thirtieth is the last working day of a quarter.**
- **August 1 is a Tuesday.** August's Thursdays are the third, the tenth, the seventeenth and the twenty-fourth.

> **THOSE TWO LISTS DO NOT AGREE, AND THEY HAVE NOT AGREED SINCE VOLUME 5. A month is thirty days and
> advances the weekday by two. August 1 a Tuesday makes September 1 a Thursday, which puts September's

> governs and the two lists stand as they are printed.**
