1,172 lines with three stacked Gate A / Gate B blocks, one per batch, and the Batch 0004 prompt says the
state files are to stay bounded and point here instead.

**Nothing in this file has been deleted.** Every interval trace and every date screen for Chapters 151
to 170 is here, in full, exactly as it was written.

**A writer who needs one of these should read it, and should read it knowing what Batch 0003's review
found.** The Batch 0003 Gate A trace asserted, in three rows, that a figure was correct on the strength
of the subtraction alone, without anyone having read what happened to that character on the anchor date.
That is how *nine days* and *eleven days* sat in Chapter 178 and Chapter 180 for a whole batch. **A row
in this archive is not a verification. It is a claim about a subtraction, and the subtraction is only the
easy half.**
