## WHAT MOVED IN 841–850, AND WHAT DID NOT

**PAGE: SIX LINES IN THE OFFICE HAND AND THAT IS THE WHOLE OF IT. THE SIXTH, ENTERED ON THE SECOND OF DECEMBER OF THE YEAR BEFORE, IS STILL THE LAST, AND NO LINE WENT UNDER THE FOOT OF ANY PAGE IN THIS BATCH BY ANYBODY** — not at 841, where the practice of 823 was entered as a row, and not at 846, where the decision was entered and where what was forbidden was a row in the bound Register and not a line at the foot of the page. The head, eight hundred and fifty-two, **is printed at 850 and at no other chapter in 811–850**, with both counts named in words and the subtraction shown, and `3,569 − 1,499 = 2,070 = 23 × 90`. SECOND COLUMN **FOUR LINES**, and no fifth line and no name went in, and the fifth line he wrote on a slip on the fourth of this month was torn in two by the clerk who is not allowed to keep it either. Four sentences at the foot. Six lines under those with the fourth struck and a name under the striking. Two inches of nothing. BUNDLE TWENTY-SIX, **quoted at 846 as it will be at the end of next month: twenty-five and one sheet.** DRAWER SHUT, NEVER OPENED. **THE INSIDE OF THAT COAT ON THE LEFT HELD FOUR AND NOW HOLDS NOTHING.**

**AND THE HEAD OF THAT SHEET IS SAID A THIRD TIME IN A THIRD MODE, WHICH IS NOT A REPEAT OF EITHER OF THE FIRST TWO.** 823 said it in a room to two people and the clerk refused to let it be said at a rail. 837 said it at a rail in public by him and it went home in about nine heads. **850 has him read it off the page at about half past two, to one other person, in that room, with about four people on the other side of that door — and then say out loud that neither of the two women who will stand at that rail that afternoon is going to hear either count from him.**

**AND THE BATCH'S INSTRUMENT IS FIRE AND A REFUSAL TO WRITE IT DOWN.** The only copy of the foot of that sheet in this city was burned at the back of a laundry on the eighteenth of November in nine seconds in front of the woman who wrote it, along with the two four-line answers that had been in the same coat since the third of May; the last piece of paper in that coat, a blank sheet out of his own drawer, went into the same stove at half past six in the morning on the second of December; and the fact of the burning is not in any book and may never be.

**AND THE BATCH'S FINDING IS THE ONLY THING A VOLUME CLOSES ON: THE OFFICE CAN REFUSE TO ENTER IT AND CANNOT REFUSE TO HAVE HEARD THAT IT WAS SAID**, said on the eleventh of November by a woman who has said one thing a month on two steps for five years and eight months and who told him nothing at all and took what she wanted anyway.

**AND THE BATCH'S COSTS ARE PAID BY OTHER PEOPLE.** A woman who is paid a wage for carrying paper is not in a list, and has not been thanked in about fourteen months. A clerk who would have entered the burning correctly and within four minutes and was told not to. A man who is paid nothing, told that the thing he was handed is a delivery and not a confession, and told it by a man who came to his rail because he could not carry it up four flights any more. Two women who came up four flights and went down again without saying a word, one of them because of a man on a half landing who is paid by nobody and who this office did not put there. And a stranger who sat in the corridor outside that room for four minutes on the nineteenth and read afterwards, in the four inches of dressed stone at the foot of a hill, about herself.

**THE REGISTER.** Rows added, each with a date and an hour in the words used, and every printed total carries its scope. **AND FROM THE TWENTIETH OF OCTOBER NOTHING THAT IS A QUESTION ABOUT A QUESTION IS ENTERED, INCLUDING ITS REFUSAL.** A row from the twenty-second of October carries a wrong hour above a right one nine minutes apart and nothing in this city can take the top one out. Eleven sentences from twelve strangers. One row at 849 has nothing under it and is a question about a sister who cannot leave a house, and the answer under it is a permission and not a fact.
