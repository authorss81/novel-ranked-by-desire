# Continuity

## Fixed canon — may be relied on, never contradicted

**World.** Ostara, a city of about two million. Twenty years ago the Veil opened under the Saltworks and
roughly one person in four changed. Merit is published weekly on the Board in the Reading Square. Ranks
one to nine; nine opens any file in Ostara; there is no ten and the reason arrives in Volume 9. Bands are
public, exact grains are private.

**Ranks.** 1 = 100 grains, 2 = 400, 3 = 1,200, 4 = 3,000, 5 = 8,000, 6 = 20,000, 7 = 55,000, 8 = 140,000,
9 = 400,000. Rank six petitions sealed files. Rank six also carries warrants, requisition of property, and
compelled testimony.

**Lin Hao.** 25. Rank four at the start. One plate, one knife, one chair, one photograph. Cleans six floors
of washrooms at the Fourth Branch. Reads people well because he is looked through all day. Wants rank nine.
Needs to accept that being seen is not being used. Flaw: he hoards.

**Lin Wei.** Brother. 28 when he vanished, so 31 now. Was a gifted registrar at the Reading Office — the
person who physically wrote the numbers onto the Board. Scored rank six in one reading three years ago,
said publicly that the number was wrong, gone by morning. **Fixed answer: he donated 214,000 grains into an
account that exists on no ledger. He is alive, classified, in the Classification Wing, and never escapes.**

**The reservoir.** Accrues to a person who cannot be audited for the deed. Has mass and no score. Moves
only on contact with the person it came from, and only as relief. Is felt as a cold minute. Must keep
moving or it settles in about forty days and becomes classifiable. Keeps a ledger of faces and streets and
never of deeds. **It cannot take, cannot be compelled, cannot be traded, and cannot raise his rank.**

**Ages, fixed.** Ines Duarte 34 · Beatrix Halm 27 · Tessa Okonkwo 31 · Nadia Ferrous 23 · Runa Sato 29 ·
Marguerite Vale 41 · Sun Meiling 33 · Wren Kallo 38 · Agnete Lindqvist 45 · Petra Ilyin 35 · Isolde
Marchetti 39 · Dagmar Oyelowo 50. **No chapter may state or imply a different age. Every sexual character
is an adult and is written as one.** **No age is stated in narration anywhere in Chapters 21–30, for
named characters or for walk-ons.** Two residual instances survive in the *earlier* batches — Ch 16 and
Ch 18 both state Emrys Vaz's age in prose — and `state/current.md` no longer certifies otherwise.

**Opposition.** Cassius Rhee, deputy warden of the Fourth Branch, 46 — the recruitment, not the villain.
Alma Quayle, Classification Wing, 57. Yusuf Adeyemi, Correction Office, 44. Solveig Rask, Vant's real
books, 42. Anouk Ferreira, chair of the Concil of the Nine, 63. Perrin Sowe, Veil-Research, 55 — Lin Wei's
colleague, the man who filed instead of donating. Tobias Renn, junior filing clerk, 24.

## Hard content rules

- Every sexual character is an adult.
- An impaired state — sedation, exhaustion, toxin, restraint, psychic contact, trance, seal — is **never**
  the mechanism that produces a scene. The reservoir cannot compel, and no scene may read differently if it
  did.
- Women are willing participants and are written as enjoying themselves. Reluctance is never the emotional
  core of a scene.
- Violence and sex stay in separate registers. A fight does not become a scene.
- Nothing in a chapter announces ages or genre. No disclaimers, no fourth-wall asides.

## Prose rules specific to this book

- Ledger notation is a System message: square brackets, own line, plain language, at most one per chapter.
  It reports in people, never in acts.
- No unit other than grains and bands. Not points, not levels, not XP.
- Rationed constructions, each usable once or twice across the whole book: "nobody asked", "nobody thanked
  him", "there is no form for this" (three times, escalating), "there is no column for it" (Chapter 1
  only, then Volume 7), "the record remained".
- After any passage of institutional machinery, one plain sentence about what it means for one named
  person.

## Planned ending — do not drift

Rank nine. His brother's file opens. The account is returned in full at Chapter 486. He ends at rank one on
a night shift. The Board gains a second line reading **unrecorded — carried by**. Nobody thanks him. No
partner dies. No partner leaves without explaining. Ferreira lives. Sowe lives and is never forgiven or
condemned on the page. No new final enemy.

## Mechanics now on the page

- **The figure did not move in this batch.** He is 8,141 at Chapter 30, the same figure he took in
  Chapter 19. He did not climb, and there is no route by aggregation, because there is no second
  aggregate. Everything he spent he spent into people who are not his rank.

  the long way. The rule as it now stands on the page: **the figure keeps moving wherever he stands, and
  the faces stop coming.** Accrual is proximity; the ledger counts *people*; remove yourself from the
  densest corridor and no new faces arrive, but the mass still grows. He has nineteen people and can
  only ever have those nineteen. This is the trap the whole batch turns on, and it is the price of
  Chapter 22's decision to spend nobody on the ninety-one.
- **Turned lines 3 → 5**, both first-time spends, both people who had said yes to something else:
  - **Teodor Mace** (Ch 25) — an Exchange counting-house clerk, plate 2,240 / 402 a week. He agreed at

  - **Tessa Okonkwo** (Ch 26) — nine unexplained colds since June on other people's chests, called
    being tired. She keeps a count from here.
- **Spending on a listed person marks them.** A cold minute is invisible relief's shadow but a visible
  anomaly, and a person on an audit list who feels a cold and then settles is a reportable episode, and
  the Bureau's threshold is three in a season.
- **Wrong-direction spend, done on purpose and aimed, does the wrong thing cleanly.** He touches a wrist,
  aims, and four hundred grains go into a *figure* instead of a body. The figure moves; the rate does
  not; the body is untouched and is no better and no worse. The person who was aimed at is drained of
  nothing and gains nothing, because the thing went past her.
- **The signature is inverted and now exists on the page in both directions.** A relieved body presents
  as **too well** — chart says one thing, colour and chest say another. A routed body presents as
  **no better and no worse with a figure that has jumped**, which is testable and is what the Exchange
  reconciliation is about. Both are Volume 3 material.
- **An unaccounted figure is a referral.** A plate carrying grains with no deed behind or in front of
  them fails reconciliation at the next gate press; a reconciliation failure at the Exchange is a
  referral to the Wing, which is not a letter to the man, it is a message to his employer.
- **The wrong-direction error can be absorbed.** Tessa can hold a body past the point it should stop and
  the cost lands in her own frame. She used that to move 424 grains of reconciliation error out of
  Teodor Mace and into an account nobody checks, which is her own. Her forearms are grey-black stippled
  from the wrist to mid-forearm and it is eleven years old.
- **The Board can print a figure that is not a rank.** A tile carries a rank numeral. Lin Hao's carries
  four more printed figures: district, year of issue, the standing of the account, and the date it was
  declared. The **fifth figure is 8,141** — his exact grains — because a declared unsettled reading
  cannot be banded (no interval, no band) and the Reading Office fell back on the raw number. **No exact
  figure has ever been printed on a public tile in Ostara.** The number is not his: 400 of it came off a
  woman with a basket, 200 off a door held in the rain, 3,529 out of the Branch aggregate.
- **A declared unsettled reading is re-read quarterly** and is carried on a printed board in the branch's
  own corridor, in a clerk's hand, in red.
- **The Classification Wing keeps a register of accounts.** Posted at the left-hand end of the Board,

  persons**, listed by plate number with no names at all. The oldest entry predates the printed column —

## Mechanics now established on the page

- A reading is a **figure plus an interval**. The interval is the rate; the band is calculated from it.
  Lin Hao's first slip: `FIGURE 3,412` / `INTERVAL —`. The dash is the machine declining to write down
  something it cannot place. A zero would be a finding.
- The wrapped plate's figure **moves** and is the only place the movement shows. It is not a rate.
  **The chain, all four readings on the page: 3,412 (Ch 1) → 3,393 (Ch 3, the 19-grain draw) →
  3,481 (Ch 4, up 88 in two abstinent days) → 3,568 (Ch 5, up 87 in the five days since).
  Total movement Ch 1 to Ch 5 is 156 grains, which is the figure Ines quotes in Chapters 9 and 10.**
- **Accrual is proximity, not contact.** He fed the account 88 grains in two abstinent days and 156 in the
  nine days after, without touching anybody, because the doors of a tram and a Bureau building are held
  open by strangers for people who cannot manage them alone.
- **A draw is contact and only contact, and only relief.** Nineteen grains out of Halvard Rees in
  under two seconds; Ines took four months of her sister's staircase in a longer, harder spend.
- **A wrong-direction spend moves account instead of relief.** Colm Deveraux's Exchange figure rose
  1,001 grains (2,168 to 3,169) in three days while his rate did not move at all. Lin Hao does not
  understand this yet.
- **Failure costs are real and physical, not supernatural.** A touch on a non-feeder (Tobias Renn's
  shoulder) produced nothing and left him grey at the left temple with a black shape of vertigo. The
  grey at his left temple is permanent from Chapter 4 onward. Abstention costs four hours a night,
  then two, and an unsteady hand — he put a bucket of water across the fourth-floor landing.
- **A turned line is reportable** (fourth subsection), and a reportable turned line opens a file on
  the person it was spent out of. Three felt episodes is the threshold, because a pattern gets an
  address.
- **The notation is a wall of people.** Fourteen lines, two turned, at Chapter 10. Eleven of the
  fourteen belong to people he has never touched.

## New world facts established

- **The Classification Wing's public counter** is brass-fronted, has three stools, a bell and a form
  rack, and is the only part of the sub-level citizens may stand in front of.
- A taken reading is Class 2 with a single stroke in a column, and the card is kept face-down in a
  rack of about two hundred.
- **The Vestry shelter hall** has benches put out overnight by somebody who has done it before. Thirty
  people waiting on the Saturday morning; most have been there since six the previous evening.
- **Tessa Okonkwo** runs the largest Vestry free clinic for the gifted, has a physical box in the cold
  store of bodies that do not match their own charts, and does not count them.
- A relieved body presents as **too well** — chart says one thing, colour and chest say another. This
  is the clinical signature of the reservoir's cost and it is what lets the Clinic's records be tested
  in Volume 3.
- **The Church of the Quiet Number** has a chapel two streets from the Vestry clinic, holds no ranks,
  asks for no reading, keeps no register, has eleven thousand members, and a rank-five woman in grey
  has stood on its step every morning for three years filing the same refused petition: *publish the
  count of good deeds no instrument ever sees.* Four auditors have refused to read it. **This is T3,
  planted. Do not develop before Volume 4.**
- Ola Bright's four sheets of diary are a **predecessor** of the notebook, not the same object: the
  sheets were left under a saucer on the landing rail; the notebook has forty-one pages and was
  started afterwards.
