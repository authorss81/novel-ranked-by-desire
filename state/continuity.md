# Continuity

## Fixed canon — may be relied on, never contradicted

**World.** Ostara, a city of about two million. Twenty years ago the Veil opened under the Saltworks and
roughly one person in four changed. Merit is published weekly on the Board in the Reading Square. Ranks
one to nine; nine opens any file in Ostara; there is no ten and the reason arrives in Volume 9. Bands are
public, exact grains are private.

**Ranks.** 1 = 100 grains, 2 = 400, 3 = 1,200, 4 = 3,000, 5 = 8,000, 6 = 20,000, 7 = 55,000, 8 = 140,000,
9 = 400,000. Rank six petitions sealed files. Rank six also carries warrants, requisition of property, and
compelled testimony.

**Lin Hao.** 25. Rank four at the start. One plate, one knife, one chair, one photograph. Cleans six floors
of washrooms at the Fourth Branch. Reads people well because he is looked through all day. Wants rank nine.
Needs to accept that being seen is not being used. Flaw: he hoards.

**Lin Wei.** Brother. 28 when he vanished, so 31 now. Was a gifted registrar at the Reading Office — the
person who physically wrote the numbers onto the Board. Scored rank six in one reading three years ago,
said publicly that the number was wrong, gone by morning. **Fixed answer: he donated 214,000 grains into an
account that exists on no ledger. He is alive, classified, in the Classification Wing, and never escapes.**

**The reservoir.** Accrues to a person who cannot be audited for the deed. Has mass and no score. Moves
only on contact with the person it came from, and only as relief. Is felt as a cold minute. Must keep
moving or it settles in about forty days and becomes classifiable. Keeps a ledger of faces and streets and
never of deeds. **It cannot take, cannot be compelled, cannot be traded, and cannot raise his rank.**

**Ages, fixed.** Ines Duarte 34 · Beatrix Halm 27 · Tessa Okonkwo 31 · Nadia Ferrous 23 · Runa Sato 29 ·
Marguerite Vale 41 · Sun Meiling 33 · Wren Kallo 38 · Agnete Lindqvist 45 · Petra Ilyin 35 · Isolde
Marchetti 39 · Dagmar Oyelowo 50. **No chapter may state or imply a different age. Every sexual character
is an adult and is written as one.** **No age is stated in narration or in dialogue, for named characters or for
walk-ons.** That claim was made for Chapters 21–30, certified, and was **false**: `chapter-0028.md`
stated a walk-on's age in prose and `chapter-0029.md` had Nadia Ferrous say she had had twenty-eight
years of her life when she is 23. Both are corrected. **Four residual instances survive in earlier
certified batches** — Ch 3 and Ch 8 on walk-ons, Ch 16 and Ch 18 on Emrys Vaz — each listed with a line
number in `state/open-threads.md`. `state/current.md` certifies nothing about this any more.

**Opposition.** Cassius Rhee, deputy warden of the Fourth Branch, 46 — the recruitment, not the villain.

## Hard content rules

- Every sexual character is an adult.
- An impaired state — sedation, exhaustion, toxin, restraint, psychic contact, trance, seal — is **never**
  the mechanism that produces a scene. The reservoir cannot compel, and no scene may read differently if it
  did.
- Women are willing participants and are written as enjoying themselves. Reluctance is never the emotional
  core of a scene.
- Violence and sex stay in separate registers. A fight does not become a scene.
- Nothing in a chapter announces ages or genre. No disclaimers, no fourth-wall asides.

## Prose rules specific to this book

- Ledger notation is a System message: square brackets, own line, plain language, at most one per chapter.
  It reports in people, never in acts.
- No unit other than grains and bands. Not points, not levels, not XP.
- Rationed constructions, each usable once or twice across the whole book: "there is no form for this"
  (three times, escalating), "there is no column for it" (Chapter 1 only, then Volume 7), "the record
  remained".
  **"nobody thanked him" is no longer rationed — it is retired.** The construction occurs twice, at
  `chapter-0012.md:19` ("Nobody thanked the boy.") and `chapter-0050.md:69`, and the Batch 0003 review
  repair ruled it retired rather than rationed. **No chapter may use it again, with any object.** The
  Volume 1 close audit briefly certified it as spent-once; that was false and is corrected.
  **"nobody asked" is two different things and must not be rationed as one.** The **note** in Ola's own
  hand — "Nobody has asked me to do this", Ch 38 — is spent once. The **motif** — *nobody asked him,
  nobody had ever asked, nobody's ever asked* — runs fourteen times across Ch 11–43 and is deliberate
  texture, because the volume's subject is the unrecorded and the unasked-for. **Motif available, note
  spent.**
- After any passage of institutional machinery, one plain sentence about what it means for one named
  person.
- **Chapter-ending beats vary.** Batch 0005 ran a *For <person>, his <verb> meant* turn in all ten
  chapters in a row, with three subjects repeated across it. AGENTS.md fails a batch for using the same
  construction as emphasis more than twice running, so **that turn now appears in Ch 41, Ch 44 and Ch 48
  only, and no two of them are adjacent.** The beat survives in other shapes in the others. A later batch
  must not reinstate the ten.
  **The repair that fixed that one put a second construction in its place, and that is the volume's
  outstanding prose debt.** Chapters 42–49 — eight consecutive — close on a paragraph carrying **"He
  wanted, concretely,"** with five opening the final paragraph on it and three embedding it inside it.
  **A Volume 2 batch may not end two consecutive chapters on the same construction, and must check what
  the page actually ends on.** When checking, test whether the final paragraph **contains** the string
  rather than whether it **begins** with it — the stricter test returns five and hides three.

## Mechanics now canon

**1. The open-question rule.** A Review panel may put one question to a candidate on the floor of the
room, in public, without notice. **A true answer given to it is a finding, and a finding is an
instrument.** That is an instrument rule, not a scoring rule.

**2. The instrument ruling — how rank six is actually won.** The printed rule seats a candidate on
published standing, and standing is a five. The panel's ruling in Ch 48: **a candidate is seated on
standing unless the panel is satisfied the band was struck on an instrument.** The requisition of the
19th — thirty-one dwellings, executed in the open, disputed by nobody — is on the table, and the
open-question answer is a finding. **Six, held, not provisional, seated on an instrument of that room.**
**CANNOT CONTRADICT.** A later phase may not find the standing crossing 20,000, and may not find a grain
awarded out of the account.

**3. The three-grain tolerance and the drift ladder.** A provisional band carries a floor and a
**tolerance of three grains**, below which the band lapses at the next press. **The ladder is 8,140
(Ch 44, one down) → 8,139 (Ch 45, two down) → 8,138 (Ch 47, three down). Four down lapses a band, and
Ch 47 shows it lapsing a man who held five for six years.** That is the proof and it is on the page.
**Figure drifts wherever he stands; drift is not a spend and is never a scoring event.** The published
floor stays 8,141 and the figure only ever drifts down. **CANNOT CONTRADICT.**

**4. What held six is worth.** Band six opens at **20,000**. He holds it on **8,138**. **The distance is
11,862 grains and nothing in Volume 1 closed it.** This number is the live consequence of the volume and
the sharpest fact in the book. **CANNOT CONTRADICT — and a Volume 2 batch may not quietly close it with a
score.**

**5. The reservoir scores nothing.** Verified across all fifty chapters. It cannot be demonstrated on
demand, cannot raise a rank, cannot be taken, traded or compelled. The one four hundred in Ch 25 moved
into a *figure*, not a body, and the chapter's subject is that the figure moved and the body did not.
The four hundred in Ch 48's answer is the *composition* of the standing, not an addition to it.

**6. The ledger.** 91 lines, 8 turned, **one line reading himself**. Lines only ever rise. The two
intervals where the count holds while the figure moves both have a cause stated on the page: he gave up
the long ride on the 29th so the faces stopped coming, and he walked the arc on purpose on the 15th
because he had nineteen people and no way to get a twentieth.

**7. What a turned person costs and who counts them.** A cold minute into a listed person is a
reportable episode and **the Bureau's threshold is three in a season**, which gets a file. He turned
three in four seconds in the Review square: **Nell Ardery, Garrick Neve, the yard checker.** **An
analyst can put a dot on a wall map and the dot is the Review room.**

**8. A sealed file asks three things.** Held rank (the band on the plate, not the wall), a deposit (the
form with the folio count), and the petitioner in the room on the day. **Provisional is not carried into
review.** A witnessed answer in a room closes a Reading Office query.

years ago, never called. A transfer receipt in Lin Wei's hand: **214,000 grains**, the eleventh three

## New world facts established

- **The Classification Wing's public counter** is brass-fronted, has three stools, a bell and a form
  rack, and is the only part of the sub-level citizens may stand in front of.
- A taken reading is Class 2 with a single stroke in a column, and the card is kept face-down in a
  rack of about two hundred.
- **The Vestry shelter hall** has benches put out overnight by somebody who has done it before. Thirty
  people waiting on the Saturday morning; most have been there since six the previous evening.
- **Tessa Okonkwo** runs the largest Vestry free clinic for the gifted, has a physical box in the cold
  store of bodies that do not match their own charts, and does not count them.
- A relieved body presents as **too well** — chart says one thing, colour and chest say another. This
  is the clinical signature of the reservoir's cost and it is what lets the Clinic's records be tested
  in Volume 3.
- **The Church of the Quiet Number** has a chapel two streets from the Vestry clinic, holds no ranks,
  asks for no reading, keeps no register, has eleven thousand members, and a rank-five woman in grey
  has stood on its step every morning for three years filing the same refused petition: *publish the
  count of good deeds no instrument ever sees.* Four auditors have refused to read it. **This is T3,
  planted. Do not develop before Volume 4.**
- Ola Bright's four sheets of diary are a **predecessor** of the notebook, not the same object: the
  sheets were left under a saucer on the landing rail; the notebook has forty-one pages and was
  started afterwards.
