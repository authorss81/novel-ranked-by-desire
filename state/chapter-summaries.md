**941 · 28 Oct · 4,227 · The Door Used By Everybody.** A woman comes up those four flights at ten past ten with a man a pace behind her who has never been up that stair and has, and both of those were visible inside about four seconds. The way up cost her something and she did not hide it. She asks about her husband, who was there in the spring and would not come. **He is in the room. HE ASKS THE MAN IN THE ORDINARY VOICE WHETHER HE WANTS THE ANSWER, THE CLERK'S PEN AN INCH OFF THE PAGE, AND THE MAN SAYS NO AND THEN SAYS WHY: twice to that office, once at two in the morning in the eighth month to a shut door because a piece of paper sent him, never angry, embarrassed, which is worse.** The clerk enters the worst available sentence and says so out loud before she writes it. **Ending: the only thing the no-list rule produced this morning was a doorway in which to refuse, and about four people had stopped on the second landing to hear a man use it.**

**942 · 5 Nov · 4,234 · The Dark Comes Early. CROSSING.** Thirty less twenty-eight is two, and two with five taken out of the next month is seven.
