| **842** | the second column holds four | **HE WRITES A FIFTH LINE ON A SLIP AND TEARS IT IN TWO, AND THE REASON IS A WAGE AND NOT A RULE.** Five words and a date and the word *and* in the middle of it where a name would go, under two printed words that say *carried by*, which are a works department's routing line and not this office's. **Nobody is paid twice**, and the woman in the fourth line of that column has been paid once for exactly that since the third of December of the year before, which is three hundred and thirty-one days ago, and three hundred and thirty-one days is eleven months and one day. **A clerk may only enter what she is given, and a thing he did not enter is not hers to keep either**, so she turns the slip over, sees that her own handwriting is not on it, tears it in two, and puts both halves in the wastepaper basket, and says it is the first thing written in that room on purpose and destroyed in it on purpose in nine years. **THE SECOND REASON IS SAID FOUR MINUTES LATER AND IS WORSE: a fifth line would give that column two kinds of name in it, and a column with two kinds of name in it is a roll, and a roll is a list.** END: about nine people went up and down those four flights that day and about four came about a paper they had brought up themselves, and one went down with a fold in her hand and nothing else on it. |
| **843** | the steps say one thing | **SCENE, AGNETE LINDQVIST, 279 AFTER 564, REPORTED AND NOT CERTIFIED. | **846** | the copy is ash | **THE VOLUME'S IRREVERSIBLE DECISION, AND IT IS HIS, AND IT IS A REFUSAL AND A FIRE AND NOT INK.** The hundred-and-first of the forms has been on that board since the seventh of last month with both printed squares empty, and that is not an event, and the boy is not on the page because the seventh was not a chapter day. **HE FORBIDS THE RECORD AT NINE IN THE MORNING WITH THE DOOR OPEN**, and not because it is a fact: a row saying a copy existed gives a stranger, in about four years, where it was kept, who made it, and — the part he had not thought of — **that it was not alone.** The clerk's answer is the better half of his own argument: a clerk told not to write something is being instructed; a clerk told a thing who has to work it out herself is being trusted. **WHAT IT CLOSES, THREE REFUSALS, ALL HIS:** he will not ask that woman to write it out again, he will not go to a flat file and read the foot of the page off the sheet, he will not write four lines out of his own head. **WHAT IT COSTS, SAID OUT LOUD: for twenty-eight days to the thirtieth and for the rest of his life there is no check at all on what this office said about a person, and Perla Quist watched it burn and did not thank him and was right not to.** And then he asks the question he had been carrying since half past eight — **would you have entered it if I had not told you not to — and she says no, and so it was not the office that refused it, it was a man in a white plate frightened of four answers in a bound book.** END: at half past six the next morning, before the tables are up, the last piece of paper in that coat goes into a stove that is barely lit, and he stands at the laundry door about four minutes with both hands empty while the smoke comes out of the flue straight up and does not bend and about four people walking past look up at it. The twenty-sixth thing on the table four floors up is not this and the count will be twenty-five and one sheet again. |
| **847** | the cost in three rooms again | **THE COST IS SAID OUT LOUD IN THREE ROOMS IN THREE DAYS BEFORE THE QUARTER ENDS, BECAUSE AFTER THE THIRTIETH HE WILL NOT BE ABLE TO SAY HE SAID IT FIRST.** That room at about nine, the laundry at about ten, the counter at about half past two. **IN EACH OF THEM HE ASKS THE SAME QUESTION — IS THERE A WAY TO PUT IT BACK — AND GETS THE SAME ANSWER IN THREE DIFFERENT WORDS AND IMPROVES NOTHING**, and the refrain is built out of three different sentences so that the refusal is the only thing the three rooms share. **THE THREE SECOND SAYINGS ARE THE CHAPTER.** *She:* you have taken away the only instrument that could have proved that this office is careful, and in about four years somebody will write down that this office did not care about anything and they will be writing down a true thing. *Her:* you will tell a woman in four years that you carried that paper and she will say *of course you did*, and nothing in your mouth or mine can show her what it was like to be the only person who had made it and then had not got it — **and I will not say I am sorry, and if you ask me whether I am sorry I will not answer you, so that you cannot stand in that room in four years and say that I did not care.** *Him:* **you did not come down here because you wanted me to know, you came because you have been carrying something since the eighteenth and I am a man you do not have to be polite to — that is a delivery and not a confession, and I am going to treat it as one, and neither of us will ever find out whether I did.** END: he will be standing at that rail on the thirtieth, and the two men in this city who read the foot of that page out loud can then be asked side by side which of them was right, and he will be standing next to the man who is paid nothing and he will not be able to tell them, and neither will he. |
