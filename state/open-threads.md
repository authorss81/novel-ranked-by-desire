### WHAT THIS BATCH SPENT THAT BATCH 0003 WAS HOLDING

1. **THE SCHEDULE AND THE CERTIFICATE, WITH A MECHANISM AND NOT A SPEECH (236, 237).** The schedule
goes up at ten on the twenty-sixth with six on it and no roll behind it, certified by an auditor of
record who is a member of the household. At eleven an extract arrives: the office has removed Ines
from a certified schedule by line, date and initial — the correction of the twelfth, countersigned
by Wren on the thirteenth — leaving the correction authorless as it should be and touching nothing
on the board. **An hour late and a paper wide.** The certificate issues on the twenty-seventh upon
the posted schedule. Neither bar is resolved. Both are bypassed by exactness, which is what
institutions do.
2. **WREN KALLO'S FINDING (233).** On the desk of Hadewig Mers, head of counter-forensics, at ten on
the twenty-third. Four edits, every one a removal, no addition in five years. It is now a division's
file. The chapter ends with a person who will be stood in front of, not a conclusion.
3. **THE RECEIPT (238). THE IRREVERSIBLE DECISION, AND IT IS HIS, AND IT IS THE THIRD THING IN HIS
OWN HAND.** Plate pledged (217), six written (224), receipt signed (238). The option that closes is
the right to say the certificate is not his to answer for. Cost: three papers in his hand agree with
each other and not with the index that holds him.
4. **RUNA'S ANGER AND THE HALF-PAST-THREE COUNTER (234).** This batch's anger belongs to Runa Sato,
not a broker. She carries his false six into a fourth office with her own hand and tells him so to
his face. Physical risk and a real deadline: the Field division counter closes at half past three
and the bundle goes through with one minute to spare.
5. **THE QUIET ONE (235).** A day with Agnete Lindqvist, a pail, soup, no figures. It earns its place
because every other chapter in the batch moves paper and this one does not.
6. **TESSA'S ENTRY (235, 240).** Decided on the sixteenth, held at Runa's counsel past the schedule,
carried on the thirtieth. A clinic into the returned lines. Counted from the morrow.
7. **INES DUARTE'S REVIEW IS NOT RESOLVED, NOT DISCUSSED AND NOT NEARLY SAID.** She reads, signs a
carrier's book (237), says procedure without content (240: an office with a door on it, in writing,
with a reason, in the way it is done), and is asked nothing. **She said on the ninth of June she
would ask to be removed; through the thirtieth she has not, and the one removal applied to her was
an institution's act on the wrong paper.** Her removal remains an act an institution applies to her.

### Proposed canon, in

1. **A REMOVAL APPLIED TO THE WRONG PAPER STOPS NOTHING (236).** The only instrument for an officer
with a conflict takes a line, a date and an initial. Applied to the correction of the twelfth at
eleven on the twenty-sixth, it leaves the certification schedule posted at ten untouched. **An hour
late and a paper wide.**
2. **A CERTIFICATE IS RECEIVED, NOT ARRIVED (238).** The carrier takes a signature in a book (Sun,
237); the head signs the return slip in his own hand (238). From the signature the household is
findable without being looked for.
3. **A FIELD COUNTER CLOSES AT HALF PAST THREE (234).** An answer in before it closes is a hearing
with a paper. After it closes is a hearing without one.
4. **HADEWIG MERS HEADS COUNTER-FORENSICS (233).** In the division since the Veil broke. Does not
hurry. First pulls the index entry, second the correction, third asks nobody anything for three days.
