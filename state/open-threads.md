# VOLUME 17 BATCH 0005 ON DISK — **THE VOLUME-WIDE GATE. CHAPTERS 841–850 WRITTEN. MOVEMENT FIVE CLOSED THE VOLUME ON FIRE AND ON A REFUSAL TO WRITE IT DOWN. ## WHAT 841–850 DID TO THE THREADS

**CLOSED, AND IT IS THE ONE THIS VOLUME WAS PERMITTED TO CLOSE: THE COPY IN HIS COAT.** Burned at the back of a laundry on the eighteenth of November in about nine seconds, in front of Perla Quist, without her leave and without needing it, together with the two four-line answers that had been in the same coat since the third of May. The last paper out of that coat, a blank sheet out of his own drawer, went into the same stove at half past six in the morning on the third of December, which is the morning after he forbade the row, and the fact of the burning is forbidden on a page. **THERE IS NOW ONE PLACE LEFT IN THIS CITY WHERE FOUR LINES STAND TOGETHER AND IT IS A RAIL, AND THE PROOF THAT THEY STOOD TOGETHER ON THE DAY THEY WERE MADE IS GONE.** No chapter of 851 and onward may produce, reconstruct, recall or describe that copy as though it existed.

**ANSWERED, AND IT IS THE ONLY ANSWER THIS VOLUME CLOSES ON: WHAT MAY NOT BE ANSWERED.** A thing said on steps once a month and left unanswered is not a claim and may not be entered, because entering it would make the steps a counter. **THE OFFICE CAN REFUSE TO ENTER IT AND CANNOT REFUSE TO HAVE HEARD THAT IT WAS SAID** — and the second half is not a rule anybody can keep, it is a fact that arrived in about four mouths in September and cannot be got back out.

**ADVANCED, NOT CLOSED: any person may put one question about any paper, answered in writing, both in the Register in the words used.** About nine a month came up those four flights in the winter and about nine had come up them in February, and the two are the same size and the second is not evidence of anything. **THE KIND OF ROW THAT HAS NO INSTRUMENT IS NOW SIX KINDS AND NOT THREE**: a question, an answer with no question joined to it, a question with no answer under it, a request, a row carrying a wrong sentence above a right one nine minutes apart, and a row carrying the refusal of a question about a question. **AND A SEVENTH, WHICH IS NEW: A ROW WITH NOTHING UNDER IT AND A DATE AND AN HOUR ON IT, WHICH IS A QUESTION ABOUT A PERSON WHO CANNOT COME UP FOUR FLIGHTS, AND WHETHER SHE EVER DOES IS NOT A THING THAT GOES ON A PAGE.**

**STILL PERMITTED TO OPEN AND NOW OPEN IN FACT AND WRITTEN: that carrying every hard answer himself was becoming the list he refused to make, and that the instrument which could have found out who was asking about whom was closed by his own decision at 840 and then the instrument which could have proved what he spent was burned at 844, and neither of those is anybody else's fault.**

**NEW AND NOW OWING: A WRONG HOUR IS SITTING ABOVE A RIGHT ONE IN A BOUND REGISTER.** It went in on the twenty-second of October, the correction went under it nine minutes later in a clerk's hand in the words the woman used, and the wrong hour is still above it and about nine people a month can read both and nothing in this city can take the top one out.

**NEW AND NOW OWING: A MAN ON A HALF LANDING.** Between the second and third floors of that building, at about two o'clock on every working afternoon, for about three years, paid by nobody, put there by nobody, and the office did not put him there and cannot move him. Two of this month's nine came up those stairs and went down again without saying a word and one of them was on the nineteenth of November, the morning after a man in a white plate told a corridor what he had done. **HE REFUSED OUT LOUD TO WATCH WHO COMES UP HIS OWN STAIR AND WANTED IT WRITTEN DOWN NOWHERE THAT HE HAD SAID SO.**

**NEW AND NOW OWING: A HALF-FINISHED SENTENCE IN THE STONE.** About fifty words in about six hands stand in four inches of dressed stone at the foot of that hill, and the newest of the six is about a woman who came up four flights and went down again, and it stops about four inches from the left-hand end with the chisel clean behind it. Nobody knows who wrote it and nobody is going to ask and it will still be stopped there in about four years.

**CARRIED UNPAID FROM EARLIER BATCHES: Brack's four-year sentence, which he will go on giving whether it is true or not; the woman who mends umbrellas, who asked for a day in four years; and a woman at a market end who asked for a standing thing in the autumn. A FOURTH WAS ADDED AT 843: a woman who has said one thing a month for five years and eight months told a man she has not missed one since about the four hundredth day and will not start now, and not one person in this city is going to find out whether she ever does.**

## AND FOUR THINGS THIS BATCH SETS DOWN THAT A LATER PASS MUST NOT UNSET

**1 · THE INSIDE OF THAT COAT ON THE LEFT IS EMPTY, AND IT WAS FOUR AND NOT THREE UNTIL THE EIGHTEENTH OF NOVEMBER.** The four were the four lines of the fourteenth of April, Perla Quist's copy, the blank sheet out of his own drawer, and the four lines that went back out of a doorway in daylight on the third of May. Three went into a laundry stove on the eighteenth and the fourth into the same stove at half past six in the morning on the third of December. **THE TWENTY-SIXTH THING ON THE TABLE IN THAT ROOM IS NOT THE SHEET THAT WAS IN THAT COAT, IT IS STILL THERE, AND 848 IS WHERE THE TWO WERE NEARLY CONFUSED AND WERE SEPARATED OUT LOUD AND IT IS SAID ONCE AND NOWHERE ELSE.**

**2 · NO ROW MAY EVER BE WRITTEN THAT DESCRIBES A COPY OF THE FOOT OF THAT PAGE.** It was forbidden on the second of December by a man and not by the office, because the clerk would have entered it correctly and within about four minutes, and a row saying a copy existed gives a stranger where it was kept, who made it, and that it was not alone. **THE INSTRUMENT THAT ROW WOULD HAVE DESCRIBED IS GONE AND THE ROW THAT WOULD HAVE PROVED IT WAS GONE IS ALSO GONE, AND A LATER CHAPTER MAY NOT RECONSTRUCT EITHER.**

**3 · FROM THE THIRTIETH OF DECEMBER BOTH MEN WHO READ THE FOOT OF THAT PAGE ALONGSIDE A MAN IN A WHITE PLATE WILL BE STANDING AT ONE RAIL, AND NOTHING IN THIS CITY CAN SET EITHER OF THEM AGAINST THE OTHER.** One is paid nothing behind the counter and has begun putting words of his own into the sixth line. The other is a member of the public with four words of pencil paper in his coat who promised on the thirtieth of September to tell nobody and told nobody. About four people have already disagreed about which of them was right, and the office may not ask what is in its own Register.

**4 · THE TWENTY-SIXTH THING ON THAT TABLE STILL HAS A MEANING AND IT MAY NOT BE USED.** A blank sheet of office paper is what you put in front of a person who cannot write down the question they came up four flights with. Three people know it, one of them is now the man whose coat is empty, and it is not in this batch and may not be in any later one. **THE COPY IN HIS COAT IS NOT IN THAT CATEGORY AND IS NOT AN INSTRUMENT OF ANYTHING. IT WAS A COPY AND IT IS ASH.**
