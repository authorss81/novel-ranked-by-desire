## Canon-change guard

Any proposal to change a fixed canon item, to move a character's age, to reschedule a scene, to change the
ending, or to introduce a final enemy not already named here goes in this file as a flagged proposal with
a reason, before it is written into a chapter. It is not silently adopted.
