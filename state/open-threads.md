# VOLUME 17 BATCH 0004 ON DISK — **THE VOLUME-WIDE GATE. CHAPTERS 831–840 WRITTEN. MOVEMENT FOUR CLOSED ON A REFUSAL AND NOT ON INK. NO THREAD CLOSED. ONE THREAD ADVANCED INTO VIEW, ONE THREAD CLOSED AT 837, AND TWO NEW ONES OPENED. NONE OF THE ELEVEN MUST-NOTS BROKEN.** THIS IS THE ONLY BLOCK IN THIS FILE.

## WHAT 831–840 DID TO THE THREADS

**ADVANCED, NOT CLOSED: any person may put one question about any paper, answered in writing, both in the Register in words used.** About nine questions a month came through that door in this batch and **about four rows in every nine are about a person**, and that figure is printed identically at 833, 836 and 840 and is not merged with any other four. **AND THE KIND OF ROW THAT HAS NO INSTRUMENT IS NOW FOUR KINDS AND NOT THREE**: a question, an answer with no question joined to it, a question with no answer under it, a request, a row carrying a wrong sentence above a right one nine minutes apart, **and a row carrying the refusal of a question about a question, which is the first row in that book that points at itself.** Nothing anywhere in this city says which is which and nothing ever will, and from the twentieth of this month the sixth kind cannot be entered at all.

**STILL PERMITTED TO OPEN, AND NOW OPEN IN FACT AND WRITTEN: that an answer given by hand to one person is overheard, copied by ear, and carried up a lane, and that the only answer in this city that cannot be overheard is one said in a room with the person in it — and that there is a third place, which is a stopped lift, where a sentence has neither a day nor an hour on it and therefore cannot enter any book and is said to the person and not to the book.** 833, and the third place is now a standing fact of this world.

**STILL PERMITTED TO OPEN AND NOW OPEN IN FACT AND WRITTEN: that carrying every hard answer himself is becoming the list he refused to make, and that the instrument which could have found out who was asking about whom has been closed by his own decision rather than by anybody else's.** 836, 840.

**AND ONE NEW THREAD OPENED BY THE REPAIR PASS, WHICH IS A CHANGE RATHER THAN A CONTINUANCE: THE HEAD OF THAT QUARTERLY SHEET HAS NOW BEEN SAID OUT LOUD AT A RAIL, BY HIM, IN PUBLIC, WITH THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN ITS TWO COUNTS GIVEN IN THE SAME BREATH, AND ABOUT FOUR OF THE NINE PEOPLE STANDING THERE UNDERSTOOD IT AND ONE MAN AT THE FRONT ASKED WHETHER A NUMBER THAT GOES OUT FOUR TIMES A YEAR COULD CHANGE BETWEEN THE TIMES IT WENT OUT.** In about four years some of those nine will have the two counts and not the difference, and that is the price of having said it in the open instead of in a room with a clerk and two other people in it.

**CLOSED AT 837, AS PROMISED AT 822: A STRANGER CAME BACK TO THE RAIL ON THE THIRTIETH OF SEPTEMBER.** He read the foot of the page with his own eyes, held four words of pencil paper up beside the wood for about four seconds, put it back in his coat, and told neither the clerk nor the office nor the man in the white plate whether he was right, exactly as he said he would. **THE THREAD IS CLOSED AND WHAT HE DID WITH IT IS WORSE THAN THE ANSWER: at about half past four he asked the counterman whether he might read the foot of it aloud, and the counterman said no, and he read it anyway at about half the speed, and about four people were in that passage, and two of them have already told other people, one of them saying he read it wrong and one saying he did not.**

**NEW AND NOW OWING, AND NOTHING IN THIS VOLUME CAN PAY IT: THERE ARE NOW TWO PEOPLE IN THIS CITY WHO READ THE FOOT OF THAT QUARTERLY PAGE OUT LOUD TO STRANGERS, AND NEITHER HAS EVER HAD THE PAGE IN HIS HANDS.** One is paid nothing behind a rail and has begun putting words of his own into it without deciding to; the other is a member of the public with four words on a piece of pencil paper in his coat. About four people have heard the second man and two of them disagree, and from the twentieth of this month the office may not ask what is in its own Register.

**NEW AND NOW OWING: THE SHEET.** A blank sheet of office paper, unfolded, undated, with nothing on it, is what you put in front of a person who cannot write down the question they came up four flights with. Three people know this and not one of them may write it down, and it may not be used, and it is the standing fact of 841–850.

**CARRIED UNPAID FROM EARLIER BATCHES: Brack's four-year sentence, which he will go on giving whether it is true or not, and the woman who mends umbrellas, who asked for a day in four years and has not asked anybody for anything in that room in nineteen years.** And now a third, opened at 833: **a woman at a market end asked for a standing thing — if anybody there ever puts a question to her about herself she will come up four flights and he will read out whatever the office holds about her, once, with her in the room — and it is a practice and not a form and not one person will ever know whether either of them is still keeping it.**

**THE ONE INSTRUMENT THIS VOLUME MAY CLOSE, THE COPY IN HIS COAT, IS UNTOUCHED.** It may not be delivered, produced or entered anywhere in 831–850 and it is not to be touched before 844. The inside of that coat on the left holds four things and a later pass that counts three is counting it as it stood at 814. **At 836 his right hand went into that coat, found the copy, and came out again with it where it was, and he said out loud that the day it comes out is a day with a name on it and he is not that day and has not chosen the day.** The six short lines on the back of his right hand from the second of August are half gone: four of the six can be said out loud in order and two cannot, and the fifth, which was the one he wanted to say yes to, cannot, and nothing in this city will recover it.

## THE ELEVEN MUST-NOTS, RE-RUN ACROSS TEN FILES

1. **NOTHING RETURNING TO THE REACH OF 486, IN EITHER DIRECTION, BY ANYBODY.** No chapter argues it and no character says it is settled or that it is not. Zero mentions of grains, of a floor, of a total, of the account.
2. **NO FIGURE OF THE ACCOUNT, no floor, no ledger total, no running balance.** `grep -rio 'settl' chapters/volume-17/` returns **zero in every inflection**; `settled` was caught and cut three times in this volume and is not available as a word and is not used.
3. **THE TWO WORDS ON THE STONE NOT PRINTED AND NOT ASSERTED.** `stone` in this batch is a kerbstone at the foot of a hill, the four inches of dressed stone, and about half a dozen ordinary uses of the material. **At 838 they are named only in the form 805 and 811 and 827 used, as two words scratched into that stone which are not among the five hands and never will be, and nothing whatever is asserted about them.**
4. **NOBODY SENT, TRACED OR ASKED.** **The woman who cannot read is not in this batch at all and nothing about her is mentioned; her sheet is named once at 838 as face down under a batten, unmoved.** The broom woman is absent, unnamed and unasked, fifth running volume. **The boy comes up the slope on the seventh with a board under his arm and a paste brush in his fist, is not asked anything, is not aged, and not one person in this city has the business to put that question, which is said out loud.** The form's ordinal is named once in the body of 838 and once in its title and nowhere else in 811–850.
5. **NO YARD, AND NO DOCKET OF LIN HAO'S, AND NO DATE OFF ONE.** No stone yard is entered anywhere in 831–840 and Lin Hao's docket has not left his coat and is not named. **The docket at 830 belongs to the man it was about, a member of the public, and the eighteenth of the last month of the ninth year that he gave out loud in that room in batch 0003 is not named again here and is not in these ten files in any form.** `yard` in this batch is a distance of about four hundred yards and nothing else.
6. **THE HEAD IS PRINTED IN ONE FILE.** `grep -c 'eight hundred and fifty-two' chapters/volume-17/chapter-08*.md` returns 837 and nothing else in this batch. It is due at 850 and nowhere else in the volume, and at that chapter it carries both counts named in words and the subtraction shown: `3,569 − 1,499 = 2,070 = 23 × 90`, twenty-four sheets and twenty-three quarters.
7. **SECOND COLUMN FOUR LINES** in every chapter of the batch. At 835 a woman is told that the day this office hands a sheet of paper to a person it is an office that gives paper, and that in about four months there would be a stack behind that door and not one column in this city could hold which person went away holding one. **No fifth line and no name went in.**
8. **NO SEVENTH LINE IN THE OFFICE HAND, ANYWHERE.** The thirty-ninth went up on the thirtieth of September at 837 at four, carrying no new line, and the rail does not move at 831 through 836. A practice was entered at 834, a refusal at 835 and a decision at 840, and all three are a clerk's hand in a bound Register at the date and the hour, **and none of them is at the foot of anything.**
9. **HIS AUGUST BLANK NEVER COMPLETED.** It is a year old at 831, `3,442 − 3,082 = 360`, and he says out loud why it stands — a row with two mouths in it is the whole of what that book is for — and no hand went near it at any chapter in the batch, and at 837 it is three hundred and ninety-seven days old.
10. **NO RANK, BAND, PERMISSION, STANDING OR SHIFT CHANGE.** Rank one, branch nights four and three off, the rota with four names, a white plate with a name on it and nothing cut into it anywhere. **The chapter at 835 is the one where that is the whole of his power: a person asks the office to hold a sheet of her own paper for nine days and there are three true reasons and not one of them is a judgement about her, and the twenty-sixth thing on that table is four inches from his hand and he does not give it to anybody.**
11. **NO NEW ENEMY.** The opposition this batch is a woman who works a lift at a market end and has worked something out in two years, a clerk who will not write a row about the man standing in her room, a man who is paid nothing who has begun to say a page in his own words, a stranger who promised to tell nobody and told nobody, a woman with a sheet of her own paper who is refused twice, a clerk at a counter entitled to one question a day, a woman who heard something in a corridor and came up four flights with it written down on her own paper, and a boy with a paste brush. **Not one of them is cruel and every one of them is right.**

## AND ONE MORE THING THIS BATCH SETS DOWN THAT A LATER PASS MUST NOT UNSET

**FROM THE TWENTIETH OF THIS MONTH NOTHING THAT IS A QUESTION ABOUT A QUESTION GOES IN THAT BOOK — NOT THE QUESTION, NOT THE REFUSAL, NOT THE FACT THAT ANYBODY ASKED — AND IT IS A PRACTICE AND A REFUSAL AND NOT INK.** The row that records the refusal of the sixteenth is already in the book under a date and an hour, saying that a person asked whether another person had put a question, and there is now nothing in this city that will let anybody ask whether she did. A man who is paid nothing has been told at his rail and has asked the only question that instrument can ask, which is whether he is allowed to know what he is refusing, and the answer was no, and the reason it had to be no is that telling him would make him a man who has been told, which he refused in the spring of this year and has not been told since.
