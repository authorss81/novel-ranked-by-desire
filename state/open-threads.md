### Proposed canon, in

Registered because each is a fact about the world's instruments, figures or offices that did not exist
before this batch and that later batches must not contradict:

1. **A CORRECTION TO AN INDEXED SCHEDULE (Ch 222, 223).** Raised by the audit division against its own
   arithmetic, it carries no requisition and no author. It has a finding, a figure and a stamp, and it is
   countersigned by counter-forensics before it goes out, because a countersignature is a check and not a
   signature. **A correction raised against an office has no person in it except the countersignature.**
2. **AN AUDIT INDEX IS CONSTITUTED FROM RETURNED LINES, NOT FROM TOTALS (Ch 222).** The total in the foot
   of a column is an officer's arithmetic and belongs to the officer. A returned line is evidence, **and an
   empty returned line cannot be struck out of an index by anybody who has not got a name to strike it out
   with.** The Office does not read an officer's refusal as a refusal. It reads it as a person.
3. **THE FOURTH PAGE (Ch 222, 224).** The form that asks a head to reconcile a figure, in the head's own
   name, answerable against his standing. There is also a fifth page, which asks him to explain a
   discrepancy, and a return, which may have a line struck through it — **and striking a line out is the
   only way in this city to ask a question for free.**
