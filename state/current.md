## What he now knows

1. A reading is a figure plus a rate. He has a figure and no rate.
2. It fills by proximity — by being the person in the tram nobody can afterwards place.
3. It gives relief by touch, and only relief, and only back to a person who fed it.
4. Pushed the wrong way it moves *account* instead, and he does not understand that yet.
5. A turned line is reportable. Three is the threshold.
6. Rank six petitions a sealed filing. His want has moved from nine to six and the reason has changed.

## What he wants in Chapter 11
